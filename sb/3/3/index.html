SB 3.3

(3.3)

atha tṛtīyo’dhyāyaḥ

mathurāyāṁ dvārakāyā ca sampannānāṁ śrī-kṛṣṇa-līlānāṁ varṇanam, yadūnāṁ prabhāsa-kṣetra-gamanaṁ ca |

|| 3.3.1 ||

uddhava uvāca—

tataḥ sa āgatya puraṁ sva-pitroś

cikīrṣayā śaṁ baladeva-saṁyutaḥ |

nipātya tuṅgād ripu-yūtha-nāthaṁ

hataṁ vyakarṣad vyasum ojasorvyām ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

tṛtīye mathurām etya vrajāt kaṁsa-vadhādikam |

yat krtaṁ dvārakāyāṁ ca kṛṣṇena tad avarṇayat ||

śam ity avyayam | pitroḥ sukhasya cikīrṣayety arthaḥ | tuṅgād rāja-mañcāt | ripu-yūthānāṁ nāthaṁ kaṁsam | vyasor api vikarṣaṇaṁ pitroḥ sukhārtham ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : uddhava uvāceti | rāsa-līlā-varṇanārambhe prema-vaivaśyād adhyāya-samāptiḥ | paścāt katham api bahir anusandhāne satīti jñeyam | tata iti priyādi priya-jana-pīḍakatvād devādīnāṁ prīti-viṣayakatvāc ca vikarṣaṇam ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

tṛtīye mathurā-dvāravatyoḥ kaṁsa-vadhādikāḥ |

līlāḥ kurūṇāṁ nidhanam api saṅkṣipya varṇitam ||

śam ity avyayaṁ sukha-vācakaṁ pitroḥ sukhasya cikīrṣayety arthaḥ | tuṅgān mañcāt | hataṁ prāptāghātaṁ tato vyasuṁ prāpta-mṛtyum api vyakarṣad iti tan-maraṇa-sambhāvayatāṁ bibhyatāṁ pitrādīnāṁ mūrcchitatva-bhāna-nirāsārtham ||1||

...

|| 3.3.2 ||

sāndīpaneḥ sakṛt proktaṁ brahmādhītya sa-vistaram |

tasmai prādād varaṁ putraṁ mṛtaṁ pañcajanodarāt ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : brahma vedam | sa-vistaraṁ ṣaḍ-aṅgādi-sahitam | pañca-janodara-vidāraṇa-dvārā putram ānīyety arthaḥ ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : pañcajanodaraṁ prāpya mṛtaṁ yama-lokād ānīya prādād iti boddhavyam ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : brahma vedaṁ sa-vistaraṁ ṣaḍ-aṅgādi-sahitam | pañca-janodaraṁ vidāryeti lyab-lopena pañcamī, yama-lokād ānīyeti śeṣaḥ ||2||

...

|| 3.3.3 ||

samāhutā bhīṣmaka-kanyayā ye

śriyaḥ savarṇena bubhūṣayaiṣām |

gāndharva-vṛttyā miṣatāṁ sva-bhāgaṁ

jahre padaṁ mūrdhni dadhat suparṇaḥ ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : bhīṣmaka-kanyāyā arthe sva-varṇa-mātratayāhūtāḥ |

eṣāṁ śriyo jihīrṣayāhvāna-buddhir bhagavatā kṛtā |

suparṇaḥ suparānandāt kākutstho vāci saṁsthiteḥ || iti pādme ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : bhīṣmaka-kanyayā rukmiṇyā ye rājānaḥ samāhūtāḥ | hrasvatvam ārṣam | “samāhṛtā” iti pāṭhe ākṛṣṭā ity arthaḥ | kena sādhanena ? śriyo lakṣmyāḥ sa-varṇena samānena rūpeṇa | yadyapi tayā kevalaṁ śrī-kṛṣṇa evāhūto na sarve, tathāpi tasyā lāvaṇyaṁ teṣām āgamane hetur iti tayaivāhūtā ity ucyate | eṣāṁ miṣatāṁ paśyatāṁ mūrdhni padaṁ dadhat | tayā saha gāndharva-vṛttyā paraspara-samaya-rūpayā bubhūṣayā bhavitum icchayā jahāra | kathaṁ-bhūtāṁ ? sva-bhāgaṁ lakṣmyaṁ śatvāt | tayā svātmano’rpitatvāc ca | suparṇaḥ supatanaḥ | yad vā, suparṇa iva sva-bhāgaṁ sudhām ity arthaḥ | yad vā, śriyo rukmiṇyāḥ samānaṁ varṇa-dvayaṁ vācakaṁ yasya sa śriyaḥ sa-varṇo rukmī, tena samāhūtāḥ śiśupālādayaḥ | kim-arthaṁ ? bhīṣmaka-kanyayā saha teṣāṁ bubhūṣayā bhūtir bhavatv ity etad artham | tatra śiśupālasyāhvānaṁ varatvena bubhūṣayā, jarāsandhādīnāṁ tad vivāhotsavena | śeṣaṁ pūrvavat ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : bhīṣmaka-kanyāyā yā bubhūṣā tayeti vyadhikaraṇa eva tṛtīyā-dvayaṁ svabhāgaṁ śrī-rukmiṇī-rūpaṁ jahāra ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : bhīṣmaka-kanyayeti ṣaṣṭhy-arthe tṛtīyā | bhīṣmaka-kanyāyā rukmiṇyāḥ śriyaḥ sākṣāl-lakṣmyāḥ samānau varṇau dvau rukmīti akṣara-dvayaṁ vācakaṁ yasya tena rukmiṇā ye rājānaḥ samāhṛtā hrasvatvam ārṣam | “samāhṛtā” iti pāṭhe samānītā ity arthaḥ | eṣāṁ rājñāṁ mūrdhni padaṁ dadhat suparṇo garuḍaḥ sudhām iva sva-bhāgaṁ rukmiṇīṁ jahre | eṣāṁ kīdṛśānāṁ gāndharve vivāhe yā vṛttis tayā śiśupālasya varatvena anyeṣāṁ vara-yātratvena yad varaṇaṁ tenety arthaḥ | bubhūṣayā ahaṁ vara iti vayaṁ vara-yātrā iti bhavitum icchayā miṣatāṁ kadācit kṛṣṇa āgatya kanyām apahared ity ata itas tataḥ sa-cakitaṁ paśyatām ||3||

...

|| 3.3.4 ||

kakudmino’viddha-naso damitvā

svayaṁvare nāgnajitīm uvāha |

tad-bhagnamānān api gṛdhyato’jñān

jaghne’kṣataḥ śastra-bhṛtaḥ sva-śastraiḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : kakudmato vṛṣabhān aviddha-nāsikān | viddha-nāsikān kṛtveti vā | tair vṛṣabhais tad damanena ca bhagno māno yeṣāṁ tathāpi tān gṛdhyataḥ kāmayamānān, ata evājñān śastra-bhṛto rājñas tac chastrair akṣata eva jaghāna ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : sveṣām arjunādīnāṁ śastrair jaghne | teṣu sva-prabhāvāveśāt svayam eva jaghānety arthāh | gāṇḍīvī kālayāmāseti śrī-daśama-saṁvādāyetthaṁ vyākhyāyate ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : kakudmino vṛṣabhān aviddha-nāsikān eva damitvā tair vṛṣaiḥ parābhūtatvena bhagno māno yeṣāṁ tān | tad api gṛdhyataḥ kanyām abhikāṅkṣataḥ, ata eva ajñān rājñaḥ sva-śastrair jaghne, teṣāṁ śastrair bhṛtaḥ pūrṇa ācchanno’py akṣataḥ ||4||

...

|| 3.3.5 ||

priyaṁ prabhur grāmya iva priyāyā

vidhitsur ārcchad dyutaruṁ yad-arthe |

vajry ādravat taṁ sa-gaṇo ruṣāndhaḥ

krīḍā-mṛgo nūnam ayaṁ vadhūnām ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yadādityāḥ kuṇḍale dātuṁ svargaṁ gataḥ, tadā prabhuḥ svatantro’pi grāmyaḥ strī-paratantra iva priyāyāḥ satyabhāmāyāḥ priyaṁ vidhātum icchur dyutaruṁ pārijātam ānītavān | yad-arthe yan-nimittaṁ taṁ kṛṣṇaṁ vajrī strī-prerito yoddhum anvadhāvat | sva-kārya-sādhakena tena yuddhodyamas tasyāyukta evety āha—krīḍā-mṛga iti | ayaṁ vajrī ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : grāmyaḥ kāmīvad dṛśyamāno’pi vastutaḥ priyāyāḥ premavatyāḥ priyaṁ premṇepsitaṁ yat tad vidhitsur yad-arthe dyutarum ārcchat vajrī tv ātmānusāreṇa tam anyathā matvaivādravat anyathā mananam evāha ayaṁ kṛṣṇo vadhūnāṁ saundarya-mātra-lobhena svīkṛtānāṁ kāminīnāṁ nūnaṁ krīḍā-mṛgaḥ kāma-vaśa iti ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : grāmyaḥ kāmīva dṛśyamāno’pi vastutas tu priyāyāḥ premavatyāḥ priyaṁ prema-vaśatvena prītiṁ vidhitsuḥ dyutaruṁ pārijātam ārcchat āhṛtavān yad-arthe vajrī vajra-sahita eva tam ādravat ā īṣat yoddhum āgatavān | tatra hetuḥ—andha iti | tatrāpi hetuḥ—vadhūnāṁ śacy-ādīnāṁ krīḍā-mṛga iti | yathā ātmavan manyate jagad iti nyāyena vadhūnāṁ satyabhāmādīnām ayaṁ krīḍā-mṛga iti nindann evādravad ity arthaḥ ||5||

...

|| 3.3.6 ||

sutaṁ mṛdhe khaṁ vapuṣā grasantaṁ

dṛṣṭvā sunābhonmathitaṁ dharitryā |

āmantritas tat-tanayāya śeṣaṁ

dattvā tad-antaḥ-puram āviveśa ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sunābhena cakreṇonmathitaḥ sutaṁ bhaumaṁ dṛṣṭvā tasya mātrā dharitryā bhūmyā āmantritaḥ prārthitaḥ saṁs tasya tanayāya bhagadattāya dṛta-śeṣaṁ rājyaṁ dattvā ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : sutaṁ narakāsuraṁ sunābhenonmathitaṁ hataṁ dṛṣṭvā dharitryāḥ tan-mātrā āmantritaḥ niveditaḥ tat-tanayāya bhaga-dattāya | atra śuddha-mātā-pitṛkasyāpi tasya bāṇāsura-saṅgād eva kubuddhir iti asādhu-saṅga-mahima-prastāve purāṇāntara-kathā jñeyā ||6||

...

|| 3.3.7 ||

tatrāhṛtās tā nara-deva-kanyāḥ

kujena dṛṣṭvā harim ārta-bandhum |

utthāya sadyo jagṛhuḥ praharṣa-

vrīḍānurāga-prahitāvalokaiḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tatrāntaḥ-pure kujena bhaumena yā āhṛtās tāḥ, praharṣaś ca vrīḍā cānurāgaś ca taiḥ prahitāḥ preritā ye’valokās tair jagṛhuḥ svīkṛtavatyaḥ ||7||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tatrāntaḥ-pure kujena narakāsureṇa | praharṣaś ca vrīḍā cānurāgaś ca taiḥ prahitāḥ preritā ye’valokās tair jagṛhuḥ, mamāyam eva vara iti pratyekaṁ svīcakruḥ ||7||

...

|| 3.3.8 ||

āsāṁ muhūrta ekasmin nānāgāreṣu yoṣitām |

sa-vidhaṁ jagṛhe pāṇīn anurūpaḥ sva-māyayā ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : āsāṁ yoṣitāṁ pāṇīṁs tat-tad-anurūpaḥ san savidhaṁ vivāhocita-prakāra-sahitaṁ yathā bhavati ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : sva-māyayā acintya-cic-chaktyā sa-vidhim iti puṇyāraṇyaḥ ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : sa-vidhaṁ vivāhocita-prakāra-sahitaṁ sva-māyayā yoga-māyayā suṣṭhu amāyayeti ||8||

...

|| 3.3.9 ||

tāsv apatyāny ajanayad ātma-tulyāni sarvataḥ |

ekaikasyāṁ daśa daśa prakṛter vibubhūṣayā ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : uttamaiḥ sarvataḥ sāmyaṁ kiñcit sāmyam udīryate ity āgneye ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sarvataḥ sarvair guṇaiḥ sva-tulyāni | prakṛter māyāyā vividhaṁ bhavanaṁ vistāras tad-icchayā | yad vā, prakṛter hitor vividhaṁ bhavitum icchayā ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : prakṛte svarūpasyaiva vaibhavecchayā, prakṛtiś ca svarūpaṁ ca svabhāvaśca ity amaraḥ | vyākhyātaṁ ca ṭīkā-kṛdbhiḥ pañcame prakṛtiṁ bhajasva [bhā.pu. 5.1.19] ity atra prakṛtiṁ svarūpam iti ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : prakṛteḥ sva-svabhāvasya vibubhūṣayā vibhavecchayā ātma-tulyānīty ukteḥ | prakṛtiṁ ca svarūpaṁ ca svabhāvaś ca ity amaraḥ ||9||

...

|| 3.3.10 ||

kāla-māgadha-śālvādīn anīkai rundhataḥ puram |

ajīghanat svayaṁ divyaṁ sva-puṁsāṁ teja ādiśat ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : kālaḥ kālayavanaḥ | rundhata āvṛṇvataḥ | mucukunda-bhīmādibhir nimitta-mātraiḥ svayam evājīghanad ghātitavān | tena ca sva-puṁsāṁ tejaḥ prabhāvaṁ kīrtiṁ vā dattavān ||10||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : svayam iti sākṣād eva kutracit tu tatra tatrāveśenety arthaḥ ||10||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : kālaḥ kālayavanaḥ | anīkaiḥ sainyaiḥ rundhata āvṛṇvataḥ | mucukunda-bhīmādibhir nimitta-mātraiḥ svayam evājīghanad ghātitavān | tena ca teṣāṁ sva-puṁsāṁ tejaḥ prabhāvaṁ kīrtiṁ vā dattavān ||10||

...

|| 3.3.11 ||

śambaraṁ dvividaṁ bāṇaṁ muraṁ balvalam eva ca |

anyāṁś ca dantavakrādīn avadhīt kāṁś ca ghātayat ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : śambara-dvivida-balvalān anyān api kāṁścit pradyumna-rāmādibhir ghātayad aghātayat | “ghātayan” iti vā pāṭhaḥ | dantavaktrādīn svayam avadhīt ||11||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : śambaram iti yugmakam | śambarādīn aghātayat bāṇādīn avadhīd iti yathā-yathaṁ yojyam ||11||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : atha tad-anantaraṁ kāṁś ca nṛpān ghātayad ity aḍāgam ābhāva ārṣaḥ | “ghātayan” iti pāṭhe babhūveti śeṣaḥ ||11||

...

|| 3.3.12 ||

atha te bhrātṛ-putrāṇāṁ pakṣayoḥ patitān nṛpān |

cacāla bhūḥ kurukṣetraṁ yeṣām āpatatāṁ balaiḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : nṛpān ghātayad ity anuṣaṅgaḥ | kathaṁ-bhūtān ? kurukṣetram āpatatāṁ gacchatāṁ yeṣāṁ balaiḥ sainyair bhūḥ sarvāpi cacāla cakampe ||12||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : patitān iti svayaṁ pṛthvī-bhāra-bhūtatvena kāṁścit tat-pakṣa-gāmitvena ca kāṁścid rakṣaṇīyebhyaś cyutān ity arthaḥ ||12||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : kāṁś ca kīdṛśān te tava bhrātṛ-putrāṇāṁ yudhiṣṭhirādīnāṁ duryodhanādīnāṁ ca pakṣayoḥ patitān prāptān kurukṣetram āpatatām āgacchatāṁ yeṣāṁ balaiḥ sainyaiḥ bhūḥ sarvāpi cacāla cakampe ||12||

...

|| 3.3.13 ||

sa karṇa-duḥśāsana-saubalānāṁ

kumantra-pākena hata-śriyāyuṣam |

suyodhanaṁ sānucaraṁ śayānaṁ

bhagnoruṁ urvyāṁ na nananda paśyan ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sa kṛṣṇaḥ | hatā śrīr āyuś ca yasya | bhagnāv ūrū yasya tam urvyāṁ śayānaṁ paśyann api na nananda santoṣaṁ na prāpa ||13||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : sa kṛṣṇaḥ paśyann api na nananda ||13||

...

|| 3.3.14 ||

kiyān bhuvo’yaṁ kṣapitoru-bhāro

yad droṇa-bhīṣmārjuna-bhīma-mūlaiḥ |

aṣṭādaśākṣauhiṇiko mad-aṁśair

āste balaṁ durviṣahaṁ yadūnām ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : anabhinandana-prakāram evāha—kiyān iti | droṇadibhir mūlaiḥ kāraṇa-bhūtaiḥ | yad iti yaḥ aṣṭādaśākṣauhiṇī-yuktaḥ | hrasvatvamārṣam | kṣapito ya harur bhāro bhuvaḥ ayaṁ kiyān | aty-alpa ity arthaḥ | yasmān mad-aṁśaiḥ pradyumnādibhir hetu-bhūtair durviṣahaṁ balam āste ||14||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : āste balam ity asyāyaṁ bhāvaḥ | bhū-bhāras tāvad vyakti-bāhulyena na syāt, parvata-samudrādīnāṁ tatra prācuryāt | kintv adhārmika-prācuryeṇaiva tatra bhāraḥ syāt | tatrāstāṁ tāvat pradyumnādi-mad-aṁśānāṁ yadu-mātrāṇāṁ parama-dhārmikatvam evoktam | brahmaṇyānāṁ vadānyānāṁ vadānyānāṁ nityaṁ vṛddhopasevinām | vipra-śāpaḥ katham abhūd vṛṣṇīnāṁ kṛṣṇa-cetasām ity ādau | śayyāsanāṭanālāpa-krīḍā-snānāśanādiṣu | na viduḥ santam ātmānaṁ vṛṣṇayaḥ kṛṣṇa-cetasa ity ādau tasmaḍ yad atra durviṣahatvena bhāratvam ucyate tat khalu tāṁs tyaktvā kevalasya mamāntardhāne teṣāṁ mad-viyogena pīḍārtānāṁ parārdha-saṅkhyānāṁ saṅgāt pṛthivīyaṁ pīḍitā syād itīty eva gamyate ||14||

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tatra hetuḥ—kiyān iti | yato droṇādibhirmūlaiḥ kāraṇa-bhūtair aṣṭādaśākṣauhiṇī-bharaḥ bhuvo bhāraḥ urur yathā syāt tathā kṣapitaḥ ayaṁ kiyān atyalpa ity arthaḥ | sandhir ārṣaḥ | samāsa-vyākhyāyāṁ vidheyāṁśā-vimarśaḥ soḍhavyaḥ | yasmān mad-aṁśaiḥ pradyumnādibhir hetu-bhūtair durviṣahaṁ balam āste |
