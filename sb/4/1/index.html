(4.1)

prathamo’dhyāyaḥ

manu-kanyānāṁ vaṁśa-varṇanam, yajña-dattayor avatāraś ca |

|| 4.1.1 ||

maitreya uvāca—

manos tu śatarūpāyāṁ tisraḥ kanyāś ca jajñire |

ākūtir devahūtiś ca prasūtir iti viśrutāḥ ||

madhvaḥ: śrī-veda-vyāsāya namaḥ |

punaḥ punaḥ kathāṁ prāhur abhyāsād uttamaṁ phalam |

vijñapayitu-kāmās tu vidvāṁsas tatra tatra tu || ity āgneye ||1||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : śrī-gaṇeśāya namaḥ | namaḥ śrī-paramahaṁsāsvādita-caraṇa-kamala-cin-makarandāya bhakta-jana-mānasa-nivāsāya śrī-rāmacandrāya |

athaika-triṁśatā’dhyāyair visargas turya īryate |

visargas tv īśvarādhīnair brahma-manv-ādibhiḥ kṛtaḥ ||

tatra tu prathame’dhyāye manu-kanyānvayāḥ pṛthak |

varṇyante yatra yajñādi-mūrtibhiḥ prabhavo hareḥ ||

manu-kanyānvayaṁ vistareṇa vaktum āha—manos tv iti | ca-kārād dvau putrau ca ||1||

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) :

oṁ svasti śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ

śrī-kṛṣṇaṁ ballavī-nāthaṁ yaśodānanda-dāyinam |

praṇamya kurve tur yasya skandhasya vyākṛtiṁ mudā ||

atha sarga-kathanottaram | turye caturtha-skandhe | īśvarādhīnair īśvara-preritaiḥ kṛtaḥ sargo visargaḥ eka-vacanāntasyāpy ekatriṁśac-chabdasya bahv-artha-bodhakatvād adhyāyair ity anenānvayo na viruddhaḥ | viṁśaty-ādyāḥ sadaikatve ity amarāt || (1) ||

ekena manu-kanyānām anvayāḥ kathitā iha |

tato rasair dakṣa-yajña-dhvaṁso bhūyaḥ pravartanā ||

tato dhruvasya caritaṁ pañcabhiḥ śrī-pṛthos tataḥ |

ekādaśabhir adhyāyair aṣṭabhiś ca pracetasām |

ity evaṁ kramaśo jñeyo vijñaiḥ prakaraṇa-kramaḥ || [viśvanāthaḥ]

yatra manu-kanyānvayeṣu | ādinā kapilādi-grahaḥ || (2) || pūrvoktam anuvadati prastuta-lābhāya manor iti viśrutāḥ prasiddhāḥ ||1||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) :

caturtha-krama-sandarbhe sandarbhāṇāṁ samāhṛtiḥ |

kriyate yan-nideśena sa me’nanya-gater gatiḥ ||1||

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

praṇamya śrī-guruṁ bhūyaḥ śrī-kṛṣṇaṁ karuṇārṇavam |

lokanāthaṁ jagac-cakṣuḥ śrī-śukaṁ tam upāśraye ||

gopa-rāmājana-prāṇa-preyase’tiprabhuṣṇave |

tadīya-priya-dāsyāya māṁ madīyam ahaṁ dade ||

caturthe kathyate saika-triṁśādhyāyavati sphuṭam |

visargo yaḥ kṛto brahma-manv-ādyair īśvarājñayā ||

ekena manu-kanyānām anvayāḥ kathitā iha |

tataḥ ṣaḍbhir dakṣa-yajña-dhvaṁso bhūyaḥ pravartane ||

tato dhruvasya caritaṁ pañcabhiḥ śrī-pṛthos tataḥ |

ekādaśabhir adhyāyair aṣṭabhis tu pracetasām ||

tatra tu prathame’dhyāye ākūty-ādi-trikānvaye |

varṇyante yajña-datta-śrī-nārāyaṇa-ja-sat-kathāḥ ||o||

pūrva-skandhe kardama-kathā-prasaṅena manor dvitīyāyāḥ kanyāyā devahūter vaṁśān uktvā punas tasyānvayaṁ krameṇa vaktuṁ prathamāyāḥ kanyāyā ākūter vaṁśam āha—manos tv iti | brahmaṇaḥ putrāṇāṁ madhye manos tv iti tu-śabdena maricy-ādibhyas tasya bhakty-utkarṣaḥ sūcitaḥ | ca-kārād dvau putrau ca ||1||

|| 4.1.2 ||

ākūtiṁ rucaye prādād api bhrātṛ-matīṁ nṛpaḥ |

putrikā-dharmam āśritya śatarūpānumoditaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tu-śabdād anyato’pi putra-lābhaḥ sūcitas tad darśayitum āha—putrikā-dharmam āśritya iti |

abhrātṛkāṁ pradāsyāmi tubhyaṁ kanyām alaṅkṛtām |

asyāṁ yo jāyate putraḥ sa me putro bhaviṣyati ||

iti bhāṣā-bandhena kanyā-dānaṁ putrikā-dharmaḥ | sa cābhrātṛkāyāṁ kanyāyāṁ putrārthina eva prasiddhaḥ, tathāpi putra-bāhulya-kāmas tathā kṛtavān ity āha—bhrātṛ-matīm apīti ||2||

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : manos tv iha tu pada-prayoga-phalaṁ darśayati--abhrātṛkāṁ bhrātṝahitām | asyāṁ mat-kanyāyām | yad vā, akṛteḥ putro bhagavān yajño bhaviṣyatīti sarvajñatvena dauhitraḥ sann api me putra eva yathā syāt tathā bhāvena vā putrikā-dharmam āśritavān iti | nṛpaḥ svāyaṁbhuvaḥ | anumoditaḥ suṣṭhu vimṛṣṭam ity uktaḥ ||2||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tathāpi tatra bhagavaj-janma jñātvā tathā kṛtavān ity āha—api bhrātṛ-matīm iti | asmāt sadācārāt bhrātṛ-matyā api tathā dāne vidhir jñeyaḥ | tena vidhi-balena | [kanyā-sampradāna-] mantrākṣara-parivartaś ca kartavyaḥ ||2||

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : bhrātṛ-matīm api kanyāṁ putrkā-dharmam āśritya prādāt | na kevalaṁ putra-bāhulya-kāma eva, kintu ākūti-putrasya bhagvad-avatāratvaṁ sarvajñatayā jñātvā bhagavān mama dauhitro’pi putro’pi bhūyād iti kāma iti bhāvaḥ |

abhrātṛkāṁ pradāsyāmi tubhyaṁ kanyām alaṅkṛtām |

asyāṁ yo jāyate putraḥ sa me putro bhaved iti |

bhāṣā-bandhena kanyā-dānaṁ putrikā-dharmaḥ | atrābhrātṛkām ity alpārthe nañ ||2 ||

mithunaṁ brahma-varcasvī parameṇa samādhinā ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : mithunaṁ puruṣaṁ striyaṁ ca | samādhinā īśvara-dhyānena ||3||

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : sa sa-ruciḥ | bhagavān analpa-jñānaḥ | tasyām ākūtyām | brahma-varcasvī vedādhyānādinātīva tejasvī | parameṇa śrī-hari-dhyānāviṣṭena samādhinā cittaikāgryeṇa | anena rucer brāhmaṇatvaṁ lakṣyate ||3||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ), viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : na vyākhyātam.

|| 4.1.4 ||

yas tayoḥ puruṣaḥ sākṣād viṣṇur yajña-svarūpa-dhṛk |

yā strī sā dakṣiṇā bhūter aṁśa-bhūtānapāyinī ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : bhūter lakṣmyā aṁśa-bhūtā, atas tayor vivāho na viruddha iti bhāvaḥ ||4||

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : tayoḥ kanyā-kumārayor madhye dhṛja-dhāraṇe ja-kārānto'pi dhātuḥ, dhātūnām apy anantatvān nānārthatvāc ca sarvathā ity anubhūti-svarūpokteḥ | bhūtiḥ śrī-bhasma-janmasu iti yādavaḥ | ato lakṣmy-aṁśatvāt | tayor yajña-dakṣiṇayoḥ | śata bhāva iti | lakṣmīr nyasya bhāryā syād vinā viṣṇuṁ svayaṁ prabhum ity-ādi-purāṇād ayam āśayaḥ ||4||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : bhūtair lakṣmyā aṁśa-bhūtā atas tayor vivāho na viruddha iti bhāvaḥ ||4||

|| 4.1.8-9 ||

tuṣitā nāma te devā āsan svāyambhuvāntare |

marīci-miśrā ṛṣayo yajñaḥ sura-gaṇeśvaraḥ ||

priyavratottānapādau manu-putrau mahaujasau |

tat-putra-pautra-naptṝṇām anuvṛttaṁ tad-antaram ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : prasaṅgāt svāyaṁbhuva-manv-antara-gataṁ ṣaṭ kam āha—tuṣitā iti dvābhyām |

manvantaraṁ manur devā manu-putrāḥ sureśvarāḥ

ṛṣayo’ṁśāvatārāś ca hareḥ ṣaḍ-vidham ucyate || [bhā.pu. 12.7.15] iti vakṣyati |

tatra svāyambhuvo manuḥ | tuṣitā devāḥ | marīci-pramukhāḥ sapta-rṣayaḥ | yajño harer avatāraḥ | sa eva sura-gaṇeśvara indraḥ |

priyavratottānapādau manu-putrau pṛthvī-pālakau | tad evaṁ yajñasya dvai rūpyeṇa ṣaḍ-vidhatvam | tayoḥ putra-pautra-naptṝṇāṁ vaṁśair anuvṛttaṁ vyāptaṁ pālitaṁ tan manv-antaram ||8-9||

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : manvantara lakṣaṇamaṣṭama-skandhe vakṣyati | sa eva yajña eva yajñasya dvairupyātṣaṭkamūrtiḥ ||8-9||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : naptāro’tra prapautrādayo jñeyāḥ ||9||

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : prasaṅgān manvantara-gataṁ ṣaṭkam āha—tuṣitā iti dvābhyām |

manvantaraṁ manur devā manu-putrāḥ sureśvarāḥ

ṛṣayo’ṁśāvatārāś ca hareḥ ṣaḍ-vidham ucyate || [bhā.pu. 12.7.15] iti vakṣyati |

tatra svāyambhuvasya manutvāt yajñasyāsyāvatāratvenendratvena ca dvairūpyāt ṣaṭkam uktaṁ jñeyam | anuvṛttam anucaritaṁ, tan-mayam eva, tad-anantaraṁ svāyambhuva-manvantaram ity arthaḥ ||8-9 ||

|| 4.1.15 ||

atreḥ patny anasūyā trī’ jajñe suyaśasaḥ sutān |

dattaṁ durvāsasaṁ somam ātmeśa-brahma-sambhavān ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

brahmasthaiś caiva rudrasthaḥ svayaṁ cāpi hariḥ prabhuḥ |

prajāṁ tripuruṣa-samāṁ yacchatv ity atrir aicchata ||

tasmāt sa brahma-rudrābhyāṁ saha viṣṇur jagat-patiḥ |

āgatya tu trimūrty-aṁśān putrān prādāj janārdanaḥ |

bhāvitvāc caiva kār yasya lokānāṁ mohanāya ca || iti brahma-vaivarte ||15 ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : ātmeśa-brahma-saṁbhavān viṣṇu-rudra-brahmaṇām aṁśaiḥ saṁbhūtān |

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : jajñe utpādayāmāsa | janī prādurbhāve iha prādurbhāvayatīti prādurbhāva ityantarbhāvitaṇijarthatvena sakarmako'py ayam | prādurbhavanaṁ prādubhāva iti niruktyāśrayaṇe tvakarmakoyam iti | ātmaśabdena viṣṇoḥ svātantryamuktamanyayostatpāratantryaṁ ca sṛjāmi tanniyuktohaṁ haro harati tadvaśaḥ | paripāti svayaṁ viṣṇuḥ ity ukteḥ | some hi brahmaṇa āveśamātram eva na tu pṛthagjananam iti tīrthaḥ ||15||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : atrer iti | ātma-śabdenātra viṣṇoḥ svātantryam anyayos tat-pāratantryaṁ ca darśitam | tad uktaṁ—sṛjāmi tan-niyukto’haṁ [bhā.pu. 2.6.32] ity ādi | ata eva ca tatra tatrātma-śabdena harir eva vyākhyātaḥ | tad evam atres tathā saṅkalpo nātijñānād eva bhāvīti bodhitam | tathaiva śaraṇaṁ [bhā.pu. 4.1.20] ity ādikas tad-dṛṣṭo mantraḥ ||15||
