SB 3.14

(3.14)

atha caturdaśo’dhyāyaḥ

diti-kaśyapa-saṁvādaḥ, dityāṁ kaśyapa-dvārā garbha-sthāpanaṁ ca |

|| 3.14.1 ||

śrī-śuka uvāca—

niśamya kauṣāraviṇopavarṇitāṁ

hareḥ kathāṁ kāraṇa-sūkarātmanaḥ |

punaḥ sa papraccha tam udyatāñjalir

na cātitṛpto viduro dhṛta-vrataḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

caturdaśe nidānaṁ tu tad-vadhe vaktum ucyate |

sandhyāyāṁ kaśyapād garbha-sambhavaḥ kāmato diteḥ ||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tenaiveti | yena svāyambhuve pṛthvī samuddadhre cākṣuṣe’pi tenaivety arthaḥ ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

caturdaśe ditiḥ sāyam akāmaṁ cakame munim |

anutepe ca tad-duṣṭa-putrābhūt śiṣṭa-naptṛkā ||

ca-kāra evārthaḥ ||1||

...

|| 3.14.2 ||

vidura uvāca—

tenaiva tu muni-śreṣṭha hariṇā yajña-mūrtinā |

ādi-daityo hiraṇyākṣo hata ity anuśuśruma ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tenaiva yena bhūmir uddhṛtā | anuśuśruma tvan-mukhāt ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tenaiva yena hariṇā svāyambhuve pṛthvī uddadhre, tena ca cākṣuṣīye hiraṇyākṣo hata iti vyākhyeyam ||2||

...

|| 3.14.3 ||

tasya coddharataḥ kṣauṇīṁ sva-daṁṣṭrāgreṇa līlayā |

daitya-rājasya ca brahman kasmād dhetor abhūn mṛdhaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā), viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : mṛdho yuddham ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tasya ceti ca-śabdaḥ punar-arthe ||3||

...

|| 3.14.4 ||

śraddadhānāya bhaktāya brūhi taj-janma-vistaram |

ṛṣe na tṛpyati manaḥ paraṁ kautūhalaṁ hi me ||

na katamena vyākhyātam.

...

|| 3.14.5 ||

maitreya uvāca—

sādhu vīra tvayā pṛṣṭam avatāra-kathāṁ hareḥ |

yat tvaṁ pṛcchasi martyānāṁ mṛtyu-pāśa-viśātanīm ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sādhutve hetuḥ, yad yasmāt tvaṁ harer avatāra-kathāṁ pṛcchasīti | mṛtyoḥ pāśaṁ viśātayati mocayatīti tathā ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : he vīra ! harer yuddha-līlā-śuśrūṣoḥ pṛṣṭam idaṁ sādhu, yata etat praśna-miṣeṇa avatārayor varāha-nṛsiṁhayoḥ kathāṁ pṛcchasi | tad-vaira-kāraṇoktau tayor upasthiteḥ anyān api kṛtārthayitum ity āha—martyānām iti ||5||

...

|| 3.14.6 ||

yayottānapadaḥ putro muninā gītayārbhakaḥ |

mṛtyoḥ kṛtvaiva mūrdhny aṅghrim āruroha hareḥ padam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tad eva darśayati | yayā kathayā uttānapadaḥ putro dhruvaḥ | muninā nāradena | arbhaka eva | yadā dhruvāya sunandādibhir vimānam ānītaṁ tadāsya deha-tyāgo’pekṣitaḥ syād iti matvā mṛtyāv āsanne’pi dehaṁ na tatyāja, kintu sopāna iva tasya mūrdhni padaṁ dattvā vimānam āruhya viṣṇu-padam ārūḍhaḥ | vakṣyati hi—

parītyābhyarcya dhiṣṇyāgryaṁ kṛta-svasty ayano dvijaiḥ |

iyeṣa tad adhiṣṭhātuṁ bibhrad rūpaṁ hiraṇmayam || [bhā.pu. 4.12.29]iti ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : yayā yasyāḥ prabhāveṇārbhaka eva sadā san anta-kāle’pi deha-tyāgān mṛtyor mūrdhni aṅghrim iva kṛtva hareḥ padam āruroha | kintv arbhakatvam atra kiśoratvaṁ jñeyam | rājatve labdhe tac-chravaṇaṁ jātam iti yuktyā labhyate, rājatva-yogya-vayasa āvaśyakāpekṣatvāt, putrādy-utpatti-śravaṇāc ca ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : hari-kathaiva jagad-uddhāriṇīty atra pramāṇam āha—yayā kathayā mṛtyor mūrdhnīti dhruvasyānta-kālaṁ jñātvā yadaiva mṛtyur āgatas tadaiva sunandādibhir vimānam ānītam āroḍhuṁ dhruvaḥ sopānam anveṣaṇe mṛtyum avasthitaṁ dṛṣṭvā bhadram idaṁ jātam iti tasyaiva mūrdhni padaṁ dattvā sa-śarīram atyaktvaiva vimānam āruhya viṣṇu-padaṁ jagāmeti ||6||

...

|| 3.14.7 ||

athātrāpītihāso’yaṁ śruto me varṇitaḥ purā |

brahmaṇā deva-devena devānām anupṛcchatām ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tayoḥ saṅgrāme hetuṁ vaktum itihāsaṁ prastauti—atheti | anupṛcchatāṁ devānāṁ brahmaṇā varṇita itihāso mayā śrutaḥ ||7||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : ye mayā anupṛcchatāṁ devānāṁ sambandhena ||7||

...

|| 3.14.8-9 ||

ditir dākṣāyaṇī kṣattar mārīcaṁ kaśyapaṁ patim |

apatya-kāmā cakame sandhyāyāṁ hṛc-chayārditā ||

iṣṭvāgni-jihvaṁ payasā puruṣaṁ yajuṣāṁ patim |

nimlocaty arka āsīnam agny-agāre samāhitam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : deva-praśna-prastāvāya prathamaṁ hiraṇyākṣa-hiraṇyakaśipūtpatti-prasaṅgam āha—ditir ity-ādinā yāvad adhyāya-parisamāptiḥ | marīceḥ putraṁ kaśyapam | hṛc-chayaḥ kāmaḥ, tena arditā | ataḥ sandhyāyām eva kāmitavatī ||8|| tad apy [agny-agāre] agnihotra-śālāyam | tatrāpi samāhitam | agnir jihvā yasya | yajuṣāṁ yajñānāṁ patiṁ puruṣaṁ śrī-viṣṇum ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : ditir iti yugmakam ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : deva-praśna-prastāvāya prathamaṁ hiraṇyākṣa-hiraṇyakaśipūtpatti-prasaṅgam āha—ditir ity-ādinā yāvad adhyāya-parisamāptiḥ | marīceḥ putraṁ kaśyapam | hṛc-chayārditā kāma-pīḍitā | ataḥ sandhyāyām eva tatrāpy agnihotra-śālāyāṁ, tatrāpi samāhitaṁ kṛta-samādhiṁ sandhyāyām api nimlocaty arkāsta-maya-kāla evety arthaḥ | agnir jihvā yasya taṁ, yajuṣāṁ yajñānāṁ patiṁ viṣṇum ||8||

...

|| 3.14.10 ||

ditir uvāca—

eṣa māṁ tvat-kṛte vidvan kāma ātta-śarāsanaḥ |

dunoti dīnāṁ vikramya rambhām iva mataṅgajaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : kṛpaṇāṁ bahu-bhāṣiṇīṁ [bhā.pu. 3.14.16] iti vakṣyati, tatra eṣa mām iti dvābhyāṁ kārpaṇyaṁ, bhartarīti caturbhiś ca bahu-bhāṣaṇaṁ varṇyate | dunoti pīḍayati | vikramya śauryam āvirbhāvya | rambhāṁ kadalīm ||10||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : eṣa mām iti mudrita-netre patyau kāyikāś cākṣuṣāś ca abhiyoge akiñcitkarā eva vāckeṣv api madhye śabdotthā arthotthāś ca vyaṅgyāḥ | parameśvarābhiniveśita-buddhau kaśyape’nāvakāśaṁ labhanta evety uttamāṅganānām abhidhāyaiva puruṣe sambhoga-prārthanā yadyapi naiva svabhāvaḥ, tad api kandarpa-pīḍayonmāditā ditir lajjā-śaṅke nirgilantīva samādhi-bhaṅgārtham uccair āha—eṣa sākṣād iva dṛśyamāna ity arthaḥ | tvat-kṛte iti yady aham ekākiny asthāsāṁ, tadaiṣa mama kim akariṣyad iti patyau tasminn eva doṣo nyastaḥ | dīnāṁ dunotīti tvaṁ patiḥ kathaṁ māṁ na rakṣasīti bhāvaḥ | nanu lajjāṁ kathaṁ sahasaivāhāsīḥ ? tatrāha—rambhām iveti | yadi prāṇā eva naśyanti, tadā kiṁ lajjayeti bhāvaḥ ||10||

...

|| 3.14.11 ||

tad bhavān dahyamānāyāṁ sa-patnīnāṁ samṛddhibhiḥ |

prajāvatīnāṁ bhadraṁ te mayy āyuṅktām anugraham ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : āyuṅktāṁ sarvato yunaktu samyak karotu ||11||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : na ca kevalam ahaṁ kāmāturaiva ratiṁ yāce, kintu putrārthiṇy apīty āha—tad iti | āyuṅktāṁ sarvato-bhāvena karotu ||11||

...

|| 3.14.12 ||

bhartary āptorumānānāṁ lokān āviśate yaśaḥ |

patir bhavad-vidho yāsāṁ prajayā nanu jāyate ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : bhartari prāpta-bahu-mānānāṁ strīṇāṁ yaśo lokān āviśate vyāpnoti | prajayā putra-rūpeṇa | taj jāyā jāyā bhavati yad asyāṁ jāyate punaḥ iti śruteḥ ||12||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tvat-prasādād yaśasvinī putravatī ca bhūyāsam ity āha—bhartari bhartuḥ sakāśād ity arthaḥ | prāpta-bahu-saṁmānānāṁ strīṇāṁ yaśo lokān vyāpnoti prajayā putra-rūpeṇa | taj jāyā jāyā bhavati yad asyāṁ jāyate punaḥ iti śruteḥ | yad vā, bhavad-vidhaḥ patiḥ prajayā hetunaiva jāyate bhavati, ato’haṁ prajāvatī kathaṁ na bhaveyam iti bhāvaḥ ||12||

...

|| 3.14.13 ||

purā pitā no bhagavān dakṣo duhitṛ-vatsalaḥ |

kaṁ vṛṇīta varaṁ vatsā ity apṛcchata naḥ pṛthak ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : no’smākaṁ pitā no’smān pṛthag apṛcchat | ayaṁ bhāvaḥ—trayodaśānām apy asmākaṁ tvayi bhāva-sāmye vaiṣamyācaraṇaṁ tavānucitam iti ||13||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : sa iti padasyāvṛttyā caturthy-arthaḥ ṣaṣṭhy-arthaś ca yojyaḥ ||13||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : kiṁ ca, vivāhāt pūrvam evāhaṁ tvayānurāgiṇīti pratyākhyeya-vākyā na bhavitum arhāmīty āha—pureti | no’smākaṁ pitā no’smān apṛcchat ||13||

...

|| 3.14.14 ||

sa viditvātmajānāṁ no bhāvaṁ santāna-bhāvanaḥ |

trayodaśādadāt tāsāṁ yās te śīlam anuvratāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā), jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : no bhāvam iti lajjayā tvan-nāmāgṛhṇatīnām apy asmākam iṅgitenaiva janāntara-dvārā sahasā jñātvety arthaḥ | tāsāṁ duhitṝṇāṁ madhye tena trayodaśānāṁ madhye katham aham evāputriṇīti tad vaiṣamyam idam anucitam iti bhāvaḥ ||14||

...

|| 3.14.15 ||

atha me kuru kalyāṇaṁ kāmaṁ kamala-locana |

ārtopasarpaṇaṁ bhūmann amoghaṁ hi mahīyasi ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : moghaṁ na bhavati hi | mahīyasi tvādṛśe mahattame ||15||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : nanu sva-ramaṇārthaṁ sāyaṁ mat-samādhi-bhaṅge yatamānā tvaṁ kathaṁ mat-sama-śīlety ata āha—ārtopasargaṇam iti | sampraty aham ārtā kiṁ karomi—nārtaḥ kālam apekṣata iti śāstraṁ tvaṁ parama-dayālur jānāsy eveti bhāvaḥ ||15||

...

|| 3.14.16 ||

iti tāṁ vīra mārīcaḥ kṛpaṇāṁ bahu-bhāṣiṇīm |

pratyāhānunayan vācā pravṛddhānaṅga-kaśmalām ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : pravṛddhānaṅgena kaśmalaṁ moho yasyās tām ||16||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : he vīreti | yathā bhavad-vidhā dharma-vīrās tathā na kaśyapa iti yato’nunayann eva pratyāha—na tu sakopa-kaṭākṣaṁ tarjann iti | yadi kṛtrimam api kopam adarśayiṣyat tadā tasyāḥ kāma-pīḍāpi bhītyā antar adhāsyataiveti bhāvaḥ | pravṛddheti na hy anaṅga-ghūrṇitā anunayaṁ sahata iti bhāvaḥ ||16||

...

|| 3.14.17 ||

eṣa te’haṁ vidhāsyāmi priyaṁ bhīru yad icchasi |

tasyāḥ kāmaṁ na kaḥ kuryāt siddhis traivargikī yataḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sandhyā-kāla-vañcanāya bhāryā-praśaṁsā—eṣa iti pañcabhiḥ | yato yasyāḥ sakāśāt ||17||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : eṣa te’ham iti | kaśyapasya bahu-vāg-vilāsa-racanā sāyaṁ-kāla-yāpanārtham eveti jñeyam ||17||

...

|| 3.14.18 ||

sarvāśramān upādāya svāśrameṇa kalatravān |

vyasanārṇavam atyeti jala-yānair yathārṇavam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sarvāśramān upādāyeti | tān apy annādi-dānena kṛcchratas tārayan svayaṁ taratīty arthaḥ ||18||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : sarvāśramān iti | vānaprasthasyāpi gṛhasthād evotpatteḥ ||18||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : sarvāśramān iti | caturo’py āśramān annādi-dānena kṛcchratas tārayan svayaṁ taratīty arthaḥ ||18||

|| 3.14.19 ||

yām āhur ātmano hy ardhaṁ śreyas-kāmasya mānini |

yasyāṁ sva-dhuram adhyasya pumāṁś carati vijvaraḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : ātmano dehasyārdham | karmasu dvayoḥ sahādhikārāt | yac-chabdānāṁ tāṁ tvām iti tṛtīya-ślokena sambandhaḥ | sva-dhuraṁ dṛṣṭādṛṣṭa-karma-bhāram | vijvaro niścintaḥ ||19||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : yāṁ striyam ātmano dehasya ardho vā eṣa yat patnī iti śruteḥ | sva-dhuraṁ dṛṣṭādṛṣṭa-karma-bhāram ||19||

|| 3.14.20 ||

yām āśrityendriyārātīn durjayān itarāśramaiḥ |

vayaṁ jayema helābhir dasyūn durga-patir yathā ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : helābhir līlābhiḥ | jayemeti | sa-bhāryasyendriyāṇi prāyeṇetas tato na sarpantīti bhāvaḥ ||20||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : durga-patir iti | dasyavaḥ khalu durgam āśrityaiva janān luṇṭhanti | yas tu durga-patis tatra kathaṁ prabhavantīty arthaḥ | durgo’tra viṣaya-bhoga eva | tatra daivāt patitān brahmacaryādīn eva bhraṁśantīti, na tu gṛhasthān, teṣāṁ bhoga-viśeṣasya śāstra-vihitatvena duradṛṣṭānutpādanāt ||20||

|| 3.14.21 ||

na vayaṁ prabhavas tāṁ tvām anukartuṁ gṛheśvari |

apy āyuṣā vā kārtsnyena ye cānye guṇa-gṛdhnavaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tām anekopakāra-kartrīṁ tvāṁ kārtsnyenānukartuṁ pratyupakārais tvat-sadṛśā bhavituṁ na prabhavo na samarthāḥ | ye cānye guṇa-gṛdhnavo guṇa-priyāḥ, te’pi na samarthāḥ | sampūrṇenāpy āyuṣā | vā-śabdāj janmāntarair api na prabhava ity uktam ||21||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tad evaṁ striyam āśrityaiva gṛhastha eva sukhena viṣayam api bhuṅkte narake’pi na patati, na tu virakta ity atas tām prasiddhāṁ tvāṁ strī-jātim anukartuṁ pratyupakārais tādṛśī-bhavituṁ vayaṁ puruṣa-jātayo na prabhavaḥ | kārtsnyena yad āyus tenāpi sampūrṇenāpy āyuṣety arthaḥ | vā-śabdāj janmāntarair api ye cānye’tithayaḥ guṇa-gṛdhnavas tad-guṇa-grāhiṇaḥ, pakṣe tvām ity eka-vacanena vayam iti bahu-vacanena apy āyuṣety ādinā ca parihāsaḥ sūcitaḥ ||21||

athāpi kāmam etaṁ te prajātyai karavāṇy alam |

yathā māṁ nātirocanti muhūrtaṁ pratipālaya ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yady api tvad-anukaraṇam aśakyam | prajātyai putrotpattyai | nātirocanti na nindanti | pratipālaya pratīkṣasva ||22||
