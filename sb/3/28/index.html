(3.28)

aṣṭāviṁśo’dhyāyaḥ |

sabījayogalakṣaṇaṁ saguṇasya bhagavataḥ pṛthagavayavadhyānavarṇanaṁ ca |

|| 3.28.1 ||

śrī-bhagavān uvāca—

yogasya lakṣaṇaṁ vakṣye sabījasya nṛpātmaje |

mano yenaiva vidhinā prasannaṁ yāti sat-patham ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

sa-bījo vaiṣṇavo yogo nirbījas tṛṇya-daivataḥ |

bījaṁ viṣṇur hi jagataḥ śākhādyāś cānya-devatāḥ || iti kaurme ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

aṣṭāviṁśe tato’ṣṭāṅga-yogena dhyāna-śobhinā |

sarvopādhi-vinirmuktaṁ svarūpa-jñānam īryate ||

bhaktiṁ saṅkṣepataḥ procya sāṅkhyām ākhyāya vistṛtam |

athāha vaiṣṇavaṁ yogam aṣṭāṅgaṁ kapilo hariḥ ||

sabījasya sālambanasya | prasannaṁ sat ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : yenaiva vidhinā prakāreṇa tat-prakārakaṁ lakṣaṇam ity anvayaḥ ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

aṣṭāviṁśe yato yogī muktiṁ vinded ayatnataḥ ||

sva-bhaktim upadiśyaivaṁ sāṅkhyām uktvā tad-anvitam |

aṣṭāṅga-yogaṁ tan-miśram ārebhe vaktum iśarīraḥ ||

sabījasya sālambanasya ||1||

dvau bhāgau pūrayed annais toyenaikaṁ prapūrayet |

mārutasya pracārārthaṁ caturtham avaśeṣayet || iti smṛti-prasiddham |

viviktaṁ vijanaṁ kṣemaṁ nirbādhaṁ tasya sthānasya sevanam | yāvatārthaḥ prayojanaṁ tāvan mātrasya parigrahaḥ | āsanādīny aṅgāny āha tribhiḥ | sata āsanasya jayena sthairyam | sva-dhiṣṇyānāṁ prāṇa-sthānānāṁ mūlādhārādīnāṁ madhye ekasmin deśe manasā saha prāṇasya dhāraṇaṁ dhāraṇā | ātmano manasaḥ samādhānam ātmākāratā | anyaiś ca vrata-dānādibhiḥ | pathibhir upāyaiḥ ||2-7||

anyāsaṅgasya gauṇatvam | tac ca dvividham—(1) naṣṭa-prāyatvam (2) ābhāsa-mātratvaṁ ca | tayoḥ pūrvatra tasyāḥ prathamodayāvasthā | uttaratra prakaṭodayāvasthā | tasmāt prathamodaya-paryanta evāsamyag-āvirbhāvaḥ | prakaṭodayasya tu samyaktvam eva | yatra tv anyāsaṅga eva na vidyate, tatra darśita-prabhāva-nāmāna āvirbhāvā jñeyāḥ | tatra prakaṭodayam ārabhyaiva bhakty-ārabdhe’pavarge jīvan-muktāḥ | prāptāyāṁ bhagavat-pārṣadatāyāṁ paramam uktāḥ | nitya-pārṣadās tu nitya-muktā jñeyāḥ | tatrābhāsam āha—evaṁ harau iti |

dhautātmā puruṣaḥ kṛṣṇa-pāda-mūlaṁ na muñcati |

bhaktyā gṛhīta-caraṇaḥ parayā ca teṣāṁ

nāpaiṣi nātha hṛdayāmburuhāt sva-puṁsām || [bhā.pu. 3.9.5] iti |

āvirhotreṇa ca visṛjati hṛdayaṁ na yasya sākṣād [bhā.pu. 11.2.55] iti | ata eva pūrvatra sva-puṁsām ity atra sva- iti viśeṣaṇam |

yad vā, bhagavati pratilabdha-bhāvādi-rūpo’pi tac citta-baḍiśam | api-śabdāt bhagavantam api kaivalya-vāsanayā bhāvāder ābhāsitatvād viyuṅkte tyajatīty arthaḥ | baḍiśa-śabdena cedaṁ dhvanyate—gaṅgādi-mahā-tīrtha-sthānīye bhagavati baḍiśa-sthānīyena cittena grāhyatvān matsya-sthānīyasya sva-prakāśānanda-rūpe hāse tad-aṁśe sajātīyatayā vyaktasya nirviśeṣa-brahmaṇa upalambhāt siddhārthatayā kṛta-tat-tyāgasya tasya dhīvaratvam eva labdham | na tu tad-avagāha-mātra-sukhaja-sādhu-lokatvam | tataś ca baḍiśa-jīvika-sādṛśyena tasya nikarṣa eva jñāpitaḥ | na tu sva-sukha-nibhṛta [bhā.pu. 12.12.62] ity-ādau rucira-padena śrī-vyāsa-sūnor lobhanīyāṁ līlāṁ praśaṁsati, vākye tad-vidhasyeva tasyotkarṣaḥ pratipāditaḥ | sa yathā—

ye tu necchanty api paraṁ te svārtha-kuśalā narāḥ || [bhā.pu. 6.18.74]
