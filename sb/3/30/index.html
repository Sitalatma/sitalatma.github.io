SB 3.30

(3.30)

triṁśo’dhyāyaḥ

dehagehāsaktapuruṣāṇāṁ pāpādaghogati-kathanam |

|| 3.30.1 ||

kapila uvāca—

tasyaitasya jano nūnaṁ nāyaṁ vedoru-vikramam |

kālyamāno’pi balino vāyor iva ghanāvaliḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

triṁśe tu kāyakāntādi- lālanākula-cetasām |

kāmināṁ tāmasī pāpād adhogatir udīryate ||

kāla-prabhāva-varṇana-pūrvakaṁ vairāgyāya vicitra-karma-kṛtāṁ saṁsṛtim adhyāya-trayeṇa prapañcayati | tasyaitasya balinaḥ kālasya | balineti vā pāṭhaḥ | kālyamāno vicālyamāno’pi | vāyor vikramaṁ yathā megha-paṅktir na veda ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : balineti pāṭhe tena kālenaiveti śeṣaḥ ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

nirguṇāṁ saguṇāṁ bhaktiṁ jñāna-yogau ca sāttvikau |

hariḥ pañcabhir adhyāyair darśayāmāsa mātaram ||

adhyāya-tritayenātha krameṇa kila karmaṇām |

tāmasīṁ rājasīṁ cāpi sāttvikīṁ gatim aikṣayat ||

triṁśe tāruṇya-vṛddhatvam ṛti-nāraka-yantraṇāḥ |

prāha sāṁsārikāṇāṁ strī-putrādy-ākula-cetasām ||

atha hari-bhaktiṁ vinā vicitra-karma-kṛtāṁ sāṁsārikaṁ duḥkham adhyāya-trayeṇa prapañcayati | tasya kālasya balino balineti ca pāṭhaḥ ||1||

...

|| 3.30.2 ||

yaṁ yam artham upādatte duḥkhena sukha-hetave |

taṁ taṁ dhunoti bhagavān pumān chocati yat-kṛte ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : vikramam evāha—yaṁ yam arthaṁ duḥkhena prayāsenopādatte āpādayati, taṁ tam arthaṁ bhagavān kālo dhunoti vināśayati | yat-kṛte yan-nimittam ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : bhagavān kālaḥ ||2||

...

|| 3.30.3 ||

dhruvāṇi manyate mohād gṛha-kṣetra-vasūni ca ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : śoke hetuḥ, yat yasmāt sānubandhasya kalatrādi-sahitasya dehasya sambandhīni gṛhādīni | vasu dravyam | anukta-samuccayārthaś ca-kāraḥ ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : śoke ko hetuḥ | yad yasmāt sānubandhasya kalatrādi-sahitasya ||3||

...

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : durmatitvaṁ darśayan duḥkhaṁ prapañcayati—jantur iti caturdaśabhiḥ ||4||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : nirūḍha-mūlaṁ prasṛta-manorathaṁ hṛdayaṁ yasya | bahu manyate kṛtārtho’ham iti ślāghate ||6||

|| 3.30.8-9 ||

raho racitayālāpaiḥ śiśūnāṁ kala-bhāṣiṇām ||

gṛheṣu kūṭa-dharmeṣu duḥkha-tantreṣv atandritaḥ |

kurvan duḥkha-pratīkāraṁ sukhavan manyate gṛhī ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : durāśayatvam āha—ākṣipta ātmā indriyāṇi ca yasya | kayā ? asatīnāṁ puṁścalīnām api rahasi racitayā sambhogādi-rūpayā | madhura-bhāṣiṇāṁ śiśūnām ālāpaiś ca | sukhavan manyata ity uttareṇānvayaḥ ||8|| kūṭāḥ vitta-śāṭhyādi-bahulā dharmā yeṣu duḥkha-pradhāneṣu ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

————————————————————————————————————————

|| 3.30.10 ||

puṣṇāti yeṣāṁ poṣeṇa śeṣa-bhug yāty adhaḥ svayam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yeṣāṁ poṣaṇenādho yāti tān puṣṇāti | śeṣa-bhug iti bhogo’pi tasya durlabha ity arthaḥ ||10||

|| 3.30.14-15 ||

tatrāpy ajāta-nirvedo bhriyamāṇaḥ svayaṁ bhṛtaiḥ |

jarayopātta-vairūpyo maraṇābhimukho gṛhe ||

āste’vamatyopanyastaṁ gṛha-pāla ivāharan |

āmayāvy apradīptāgnir alpāhāro’lpa-ceṣṭitaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : bhriyamāṇaḥ puṣyamāṇaḥ ||14|| avamatyāvajñayopanyastaṁ samīpe prakṣiptam | gṛha-pālaḥ śvā | āharan bhuñjānaḥ | āmayāvī rogī ||15||

————————————————————————————————————————

|| 3.30.16 ||

vāyunotkramatottāraḥ kapha-saṁruddha-nāḍikaḥ |

|| 3.30.21-22 ||

|| 3.30.31 ||

ekaḥ prapadyate dhvāntaṁ hitvedaṁ sva-kalevaram |

kuśaletara-pātheyo bhūta-droheṇa yad bhṛtam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : pāpārjitaṁ dhanaṁ bhuñjate bahavaḥ, kartaina narakaṁ yātīty āha—eka iti | bhūta-droheṇa yad bhṛtaṁ tat sthūlaṁ sva-kalevaram ihaiva hitvā | kuśalād itarat pāpaṁ tad eva pātheyaṁ bhogyaṁ yasya ||31||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : kuśaletarat pāpam eva pātheyaṁ maraṇa-patha-bhogyaṁ yasya saḥ | bhūta-droheṇa yad bhūtaṁ puṣṭīkṛtaṁ tat sthūlaṁ kalevaraṁ hitvā ||31||

|| 3.30.32 ||

daivenāsāditaṁ tasya śamalaṁ niraye pumān |

bhuṅkte kuṭumba-poṣasya hṛta-vitta ivāturaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : nanu pāpam api vihāya gacchatu tatrāha—daiveneti | tasya kuṭumba-poṣaṇasya śamalaṁ pāpaṁ daivaneneśvareṇa prāpitaṁ bhuṅkte ||32||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : daivenety ukte’pi neśvarasya nairghṛṇyam īśvaras tu parjanyavad draṣṭavya iti nyāyāt ||32||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : punar manuṣya-śarīra-prāpti-prakāram āha—nara-lokasya manuṣya-dehasya prāpter adhastād arvāg yāvatyo yātanāḥ | ādi-śabdena śva-sūkarādi-yonayaś ca yāstāḥ sarvāḥ krameṇa saṁprāpya bhogena kṣīṇa-pāpaḥ śuciḥ sann atra punar naratvaṁ prāpnotīty arthaḥ ||34||
