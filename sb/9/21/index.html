SB 9.21

(9.21)

athaikaviṁśo’dhyāyaḥ

bharata-vaṁśānuvarṇane

|| 9.21.1 ||

śrī-śuka uvāca—

vitathasya sutān manyor bṛhatkṣatro jayas tataḥ |

mahāvīryo naro gargaḥ saṅkṛtis tu narātmajaḥ ||

śrīdharaḥ :

ekaviṁśe tu dauṣyanteḥ sutasyānvaya ucyate |

rantidevājamīḍhāder yatra kīrtiḥ pratanyate ||

bharatasyānvaye vitathe sati dattatvād vitatha-saṁjño bharadvājaḥ | sa ca brāhmaṇo’pi bharatasya dattaḥ putro jāta iti tad vaṁśo’nukramyate | vitathasya suto manyuḥ, tasmād bṛhat-kṣatra-jaya-mahāvīrya-nara-gargāḥ pañca putrā babhūvuḥ ||1||

krama-sandarbhaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanāthaḥ :

ekaviṁśe puru-vaṁśya-rantideva-kathocyate |

yasya tv audārya-dhairyābhyām tryadhīśās toṣam āyayuḥ ||

vitatho bharadvājaḥ, sa ca brāhmaṇo’pi bharatasya putras tasmān manyuḥ ||1||

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.2 ||

guruś ca rantidevaś ca saṅkṛteḥ pāṇḍu-nandana |

rantidevasya mahimā ihāmutra ca gīyate ||

na katamenāpi vyākhyātam.

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.3 ||

viyad-vittasya dadato labdhaṁ labdhaṁ bubhukṣataḥ |

niṣkiñcanasya dhīrasya sa-kuṭumbasya sīdataḥ ||

śrīdharaḥ : viyad-vittasya viyato gaganād ivodyamaṁ vinā daivād upasthitam eva vittaṁ bhogyaṁ yasya | yad vā, viyad vyayaṁ prāpnuvad vittaṁ bhogyaṁ yasya | tad evāha—bubhukṣato’pi sato labdhaṁ labdhaṁ dadataḥ | tat prapañcayati—niṣkiñcanasyety-ādi-sārdhaiḥ pañcadaśabhiḥ ||3||

krama-sandarbhaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanāthaḥ : viyato gaganād iva udyamaṁ vinā daivād upasthitam eva vittaṁ bhogyaṁ yasya tatrāpi labdhaṁ dadataḥ bubhukṣamāṇasyāpi ||3||

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.4 ||

vyatīyur aṣṭa-catvāriṁśad ahāny apibataḥ kila |

ghṛta-pāyasa-saṁyāvaṁ toyaṁ prātar upasthitam ||

śrīdharaḥ : apibato jala-pānam apy akurvataḥ | ghṛtādi-trayāṇāṁ dvandvaikyam | toyaṁ ca ||4||

krama-sandarbhaḥ : ghṛteti sārdhakam | tad-antaraṁ ghṛtādikam upasthitaṁ bhoktu-kāmasya ||4||

viśvanāthaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.5 ||

kṛcchra-prāpta-kuṭumbasya kṣut-tṛḍbhyāṁ jāta-vepathoḥ |

atithir brāhmaṇaḥ kāle bhoktu-kāmasya cāgamat ||

na katamenāpi vyākhyātam.

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.6 ||

tasmai saṁvyabhajat so’nnam ādṛtya śraddhayānvitaḥ |

hariṁ sarvatra sampaśyan sa bhuktvā prayayau dvijaḥ ||

śrīdharaḥ, viśvanāthaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

krama-sandarbhaḥ : annaṁ pūrvoktaṁ ghṛtādikam eva ||6||

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.7 ||

athānyo bhokṣyamāṇasya vibhaktasya mahīpateḥ |

vibhaktaṁ vyabhajat tasmai vṛṣalāya hariṁ smaran ||

śrīdharaḥ : athānyo vṛṣalo’tithir āgamat | vibhaktasya kuṭumbārtham annādikaṁ vibhakta-vataḥ ||7||

krama-sandarbhaḥ : pūrvaṁ hariṁ sarvatra sampaśyann ity uktam | adhunā ca tasya tāṁ karuṇā-vācam ity ucyate | tatra sarvatraiva harim antaryāmitayā sampaśyann iti cet, jīva-dṛṣṭy-abhāvena karuṇeyaṁ teṣu viruddhyate | tasmāt taṁ sarvatra parama-kāruṇikatayaiva sampaśyaṁs tad-bhāvena spṛśyamānaḥ karuṇāyata iti labhyate | ata eva brahma-śivābhyāṁ tādṛśa-guṇa-bhaktābhyāṁ saha tad-āvirbhāvānantaraṁ tad-bhaktir eva phalitā vakṣyate—vāsudeve bhagavati bhaktyā cakre manaḥ paraṁ [bhā.pu. 9.21.15] iti | kevala-jīva-kāruṇyaṁ khalu vighnāya syāt, bharatavat | tasmān na kāmaye [bhā.pu. 9.21.12] ity-ādinā yo mokṣānādaraḥ, so’pi tat-kāruṇya-guṇa-vibhāvana-maya-bhakti-kṛta eveti nāyuktaḥ syād iti jñeyam ||7||

viśvanāthaḥ : vibhaktasya vibhaktavato mahīpater anyo’tithir āgataḥ | tataś ca kuṭumbādy-arthaṁ vibhaktam evānnaṁ tasmai vyabhajat, vibhajya dadau ||7||

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.8 ||

yāte śūdre tam anyo’gād atithiḥ śvabhir āvṛtaḥ |

rājan me dīyatām annaṁ sa-gaṇāya bubhukṣate ||

śrīdharaḥ : sa-gaṇāya śva-yūtha-sahitāya ||8||

krama-sandarbhaḥ, viśvanāthaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.9 ||

sa ādṛtyāvaśiṣṭaṁ yad bahu-māna-puraskṛtam |

tac ca dattvā namaścakre śvabhyaḥ śva-pataye vibhuḥ ||

na katamenāpi vyākhyātam.

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.10 ||

pānīya-mātram uccheṣaṁ tac caika-paritarpaṇam |

pāsyataḥ pulkaso’bhyāgād apo dehy aśubhāya me ||

śrīdharaḥ : uccheṣam urvaritam | ekam eva tarpayatīti tathā ||10||

krama-sandarbhaḥ, viśvanāthaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.11 ||

tasya tāṁ karuṇāṁ vācaṁ niśamya vipula-śramām |

kṛpayā bhṛśa-santapta idam āhāmṛtaṁ vacaḥ ||

śrīdharaḥ, krama-sandarbhaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanāthaḥ : uccheṣam urvaritam ekam eva tṛptīkaroti, na tu dvāv iti svārthaḥ | vibhāgānarham iti bhāvaḥ | amṛtaṁ vaca iti tasya vāco’pi yena sa-śraddhaṁ karṇābhyām pīyate, so’pi na mriyate, tasya na dehenaiva siddhyati, kiṁ punas tat karmānutiṣṭheyur iti bhāvaḥ ||11||

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.12 ||

na kāmaye’haṁ gatim īśvarāt parām

aṣṭa-rddhi-yuktām apunar-bhavaṁ vā |

ārtiṁ prapadye’khila-deha-bhājām

antaḥ-sthito yena bhavanty aduḥkhāḥ ||

śrīdharaḥ : para-duḥkhāsahiṣṇutayā sarveṣāṁ duḥkhaṁ svayaṁ bhoktum āśāste—neti | aṇimādy-aṣṭa-samṛddhi-yuktāṁ gatim api [apunar-bhavaṁ] mokṣam api ca ahaṁ na kāmaye | tarhi kiṁ kāmayase ? tad āha—akhila-deha-bhājām ārtiṁ duḥkhaṁ tat-tad-bhoktṛ-rūpeṇa antaḥ-sthitaḥ sann ahaṁ prapadye prāpnuyām ity evaṁ kāmaye, yena tad-duḥkha-bhoktrā mayā hetu-bhūtena te sarve’duḥkhā bhavanti ||12||

krama-sandarbhaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanāthaḥ : antaḥ-sthitaḥ san ārtiṁ prapadye, tair bhoktavyaṁ duḥkham aham eva bhūñjeya sarva-jīvānām api samuditaṁ duḥkham aham eka eva bhoktum alam iti tat sva-duḥkhaṁ sahyaṁ para-duḥkha-darśanaṁ tv asahyam iti bhāvaḥ ||12||

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.13 ||

kṣut-tṛṭ-śramo gātra-paribhramaś ca

dainyaṁ klamaḥ śoka-viṣāda-mohāḥ |

sarve nivṛttāḥ kṛpaṇasya jantor

jijīviṣor jīva-jalārpaṇān me ||

śrīdharaḥ : nanu duḥkhaṁ kim iti prārthayase | para-duḥkha-nivṛttyaiva mama sarva-duḥkha-nivṛttir ity āha—kṣut-tṛḍ iti | kṛpaṇasya jantor jīvana-hetor jalasyārpaṇān me sarve kṣut-tṛḍ-ādayo nivṛttāḥ ||13||

krama-sandarbhaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanāthaḥ : nanv iti pipāsārto’si kiñcid avaśiṣṭaṁ jalaṁ svayaṁ ca pibety ata āha—kṣut tṛḍ iti | kṛpaṇasyāsya jantor jīvana-hetor jalasyārpaṇān mama kṣut-tṛḍ-ādayo vivṛttāḥ ||13||

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.14 ||

iti prabhāṣya pānīyaṁ mriyamāṇaḥ pipāsayā |

pulkasāyādadād dhīro nisarga-karuṇo nṛpaḥ ||

śrīdharaḥ : ity evaṁ prabhāṣya ||14||

krama-sandarbhaḥ : nisarga-karuṇa iti ca nisargeṇa śrī-bhagavat-karuṇā-sphurti-maya-svabhāvena karuṇaḥ ||14||

viśvanāthaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.15 ||

tasya tribhuvanādhīśāḥ phaladāḥ phalam icchatām

ātmānaṁ darśayāṁ cakrur māyā viṣṇu-vinirmitāḥ ||

śrīdharaḥ : tri-bhuvanādhīśā brahmādayaḥ | māyās tadīya-dhairya-parīkṣārthaṁ prathamaṁ māyayā vṛṣalādi-rūpeṇa pratītāḥ santa ity arthaḥ ||15||

krama-sandarbhaḥ : ata eva tasyeti | yataḥ sāmānyataḥ phalam icchatāṁ phaladāḥ, yata evātma-prabhṛtayo viṣṇunā bhagavatā svayam eva māyā māyika-vṛṣalādi-rūpeṇa nirmitāḥ pratyāyitā ity arthaḥ | atas te punar ātmānaṁ yathārtha-rūpaṁ darśayāñcakruḥ ||15||

viśvanāthaḥ : tri-bhuvanādhīśā viṣṇu-brahma-rudrāḥ, tasya dhairya-parīkṣārthaṁ prathamaṁ māyāḥ brāhmaṇa-vṛṣalāśva-patīn, tataḥ ātmānaṁ sva-svarūpaṁ ca ||15||

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.16 ||

sa vai tebhyo namaskṛtya niḥsaṅgo vigata-spṛhaḥ

vāsudeve bhagavati bhaktyā cakre manaḥ param ||

śrīdharaḥ : paraṁ kevalaṁ vāsudeve bhaktyā namaś cakre na tu tān kim api yācitavān ||16||

krama-sandarbhaḥ : sa vai sa tu | niḥsaṅgaḥ tad-darśanaṁ vinā phalāntara-bhoga-rahitaḥ, tatra vigata-spṛhaś ca | bhagavati tad-bhakteṣu ca sama-pūjyatva-dṛṣṭyā tebhyo namaskāraṁ viśeṣeṇaiva kṛtvāpi cakre paraṁ śreṣṭhaṁ yathā syāt tathā vāsudeve bhagavati manaś cakre | tatra hetuḥ—bhaktyā, tayor api tatra sa-prema-mahā-bhakti-dṛṣṭyety arthaḥ ||16||

viśvanāthaḥ : manaḥ-paraṁ śreṣṭhaṁ bhagavad-rūpa-guṇādi-dhyāyitvāt ||16||

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.17 ||

īśvarālambanaṁ cittaṁ kurvato’nanya-rādhasaḥ

māyā guṇa-mayī rājan svapnavat pratyalīyata ||

śrīdharaḥ : ananya-rādhasa īśvarāti-rikta-phalāntarānapekṣasya | pratyalīyatātmany eva līnā babhūva |

krama-sandarbhaḥ : ananya-rādhasa iti, ārtiṁ prapadye’khila-deha-bhājāṁ [bhā.pu. 9.21.11] iti ca mithaḥ pūrva-yuktyaiva saṅgacchate | pratyalīyata tato’ntarhitavatī, ṛte’rthaṁ yat pratīyeta [bhā.pu. 2.9.33] iti nyāyena ||17||

viśvanāthaḥ : tad evāha—īśvareti | ananya-rādhasaḥ anyad evānyārādhana-śūnyasya sva-pūrvaṁ svapne yathā svata eva līyate tathaivety arthaḥ ||17||

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.18 ||

tat-prasaṅgānubhāvena rantidevānuvartinaḥ

abhavan yoginaḥ sarve nārāyaṇa-parāyaṇāḥ ||

śrīdharaḥ, viśvanāthaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

krama-sandarbhaḥ : tādṛśīṁ bhagavad-bhaktiṁ kaimutyena praśaṁsati—tat-prasaṅgeti ||18||

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.19 ||

gargāc chinis tato gārgyaḥ kṣatrād brahma hy avartata

duritakṣayo mahāvīryāt tasya trayyāruṇiḥ kaviḥ ||

śrīdharaḥ : manyu-sutasya narasya vaṁśam uktvā tat sutasyaiva gargasya vaṁśam āha—gargād iti | mahā-vīryasya vaṁśam āha—duritakṣayas tasya trayyāruṇiḥ kaviḥ puṣkarāruṇiś ceti trayaḥ putrāḥ ||19||

krama-sandarbhaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanāthaḥ : narasya vaṁśam uktvā tad-bhrātṝṇāṁ gargādīnāṁ vaṁśam āha—gargād iti ||19||

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.20 ||

puṣkarāruṇir ity atra ye brāhmaṇa-gatiṁ gatāḥ

bṛhatkṣatrasya putro’bhūd dhastī yad-dhastināpuram ||

śrīdharaḥ : kathaṁ-bhūtāḥ ? ye atra kṣatra-vaṁśe brāhmaṇa-gatiṁ brāhmaṇa-rūpatāṁ gatās te | manyu-putrāṇāṁ pañcānāṁ jyeṣṭhasya vaṁśam āha—bṛhat-kṣatrasyeti | yad yena hastināpuraṁ kṛtam ity arthaḥ ||20||

krama-sandarbhaḥ, viśvanāthaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.21 ||

ajamīḍho dvimīḍhaś ca purumīḍhaś ca hastinaḥ

ajamīḍhasya vaṁśyāḥ syuḥ priyamedhādayo dvijāḥ ||

śrīdharaḥ : ajamīḍhādayas trayo hastinaḥ putrāḥ ||21||

krama-sandarbhaḥ, viśvanāthaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.22 ||

ajamīḍhād bṛhadiṣus tasya putro bṛhaddhanuḥ

bṛhatkāyas tatas tasya putra āsīj jayadrathaḥ ||

śrīdharaḥ : ajamīḍhād evānyaḥ putro bṛhadiṣur nāmābhavat ||22||

krama-sandarbhaḥ, viśvanāthaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.23 ||

tat-suto viśadas tasya syenajit samajāyata

rucirāśvo dṛḍhahanuḥ kāśyo vatsaś ca tat-sutāḥ ||

na katamenāpi vyākhyātam.

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.24 ||

rucirāśva-sutaḥ pāraḥ pṛthusenas tad-ātmajaḥ

pārasya tanayo nīpas tasya putra-śataṁ tv abhūt ||

śrīdharaḥ : pārasya tanayo nīpaḥ | nīpasya putra-śatam ||24||

krama-sandarbhaḥ, viśvanāthaḥ : na vyākhyātam.

—o)0(o—

|| 9.21.25 ||

sa kṛtvyāṁ śuka-kanyāyāṁ brahmadattam ajījanat |

yogī sa gavi bhāryāyāṁ viṣvaksenam adhāt sutam ||

śrīdharaḥ : sa eva kṛtvyāṁ kṛtvī-saṁjñāyāṁ śuka-kanyāyāṁ brahma-dattaṁ ca janayām āsa | tad uktaṁ harivaṁśādiṣu, “parāśara-kulotpannaḥ śuko nāma mahā-yaśāḥ | vyāsād araṇyāṁ saṁbhūto vidhūmo’gnir ivojjvalan | sa tasyāṁ pitṛ-kanyāyāṁ vīriṇyāṁ janayiṣyati | kṛṣṇaṁ gaura-prabhaṁ śaṁbhuṁ tathā bhūri-śrutaṁ jayam | kanyāṁ kīrti-matīṁ ṣaṣṭhīṁ yoginīṁ yoga-mātaram | brahma-dattasya jananīṁ mahiṣīm aṇuhasya ca |” iti | yady api śuka utpattyaiva vimukta-saṅgo nirgatas tathāpi virahāturaṁ vyāsam anuyāntaṁ dṛṣṭvā chāyā-śukaṁ nirmāya gatavāṁs tad abhiprāyeṇaiva gārhasthyādi-vyavahāra ity avirodhaḥ | sa brahma-datto yogī | gavi vāci sarasvatyām ||25||

krama-sandarbhaḥ : nīpasyaiva bahu-kāla-vyavadhānena vyūḍhāyāṁ śuka-kanyāyāṁ vaṁśāntaram āha—sa kṛtvyām iti | śuka-kanyāyām ity atra, na tu mama kanyāyām ity uktam iti svasmād anyo’sau śuka iti bodhayati | tac ca yuktam | brahma-vaivarta-varṇita-svayaṁ-śrī-bhagavan-mahānugraha-pātratvāt, tata eva viṣṇurātavan narākṛti-para-brahmaṇā dattatvena tad-brahmarātatva-sambhavāt tasyaiva śrī-bhāgavata-vaktṛtvaṁ sambhavati, nānyasya | kalpa-bheda-vyavasthayā bhāratādi-varṇitasyā’raṇi-sambhavasya kim uta chāyā-śukatvena kalpitasyeti | atra brahma-vaivartetihāso’yaṁ kiñcit kiñcit proddhriyate—

prāpte kaliyuge vyāsaś cakre yo jagataḥ kṛte |

mahābhāratam ākhyānaṁ sarva-vedārtha-saṅgraham || ity-ādi |

tathā—

brahmacarya-paribhraṁśe mātur vākyād upasthite |

svayaṁ pita-ṛṇa-nālaṁ dvipavat soḍhum akṣamaḥ ||

vicintya manasā cakre bhāryāṁ jābāli-kanyakām |

vīṭikākhyāṁ dadau tasmai so’pi vaikhānasāśramī ||

cakre tayā saha tapa ṛtu-gāmī sutecchayā |

atha garbho’bhavat tasmāt tasyāṁ sa vavṛdhe śanaiḥ ||

kalābhiḥ śukla-pakṣe vai himadyutir iva dvija |

dvādaśābdāni jātāni na prasūte sma sā tadā ||

kṛtṛṣṇa-dvaipāyana-proktaṁ vedā-vedārtha-saṅgraham |

purāṇaṁ bhārataṁ dharma-śāstraṁ yad aśṛṇod dvija ||

tat sarvaṁ prajñayā garbbhaś cakre hṛdi papāṭha ca |

garbha-saṁstho’pi kurute na tu niṣkramaṇe matim ||

sa ca garbhabharākrantā ity-ādi |

atha dvādaśame varṣe kṛṣṇa-dvaipāyano muniḥ |

upaviśya kṛta-snāno garbham āmantrya sāmabhiḥ ||

sambodhayāmāsa tadā vacobhir yukti-bhāvitaiḥ |

vyāsa uvāca—

yat tvaṁ madīya-gṛhiṇīm adhunā’tikālam

āviśya pīḍayasi garbhatayā yathārtham |

tad brūhi niṣkramasi nāma kathaṁ na mūḍha!

kiṁ mārayiṣyasi viruddha-karas tvam etām ||

garbha uvāca—

rakṣo’ham asmi sura-pakṣety-ādi,

bhrānto’haṁ teṣu sarveṣu ko’haṁ tvāṁ ca bravīmi kim |

sāmprataṁ mānuṣo bhutvā jaṭhare’syā avasthitaḥ ||

tad vyāsa! na kariṣyāmi garbhān niṣkramaṇaṁ bahiḥ |

nātmānaṁ veṣṭayiṣyāmi dīna-citto’jamāyayā || ity-ādi |

vyāsa uvāca—

yady evam asty abhimataṁ tava tarhi nindyān

niṣkramya tulya-narakād iha garbha-vāsāt |

samyak samāśraya mahodaya-hetum ekaṁ

śrī-prāṇanātham ubhayoḥ kuru vā vivekam ||

garbha uvāca—

jñānaṁ viveka-bhavam asti ca tāvad eva

vairāgyam anya-bhava-saṁsmaraṇaṁ ca tāvat |

yāvan na niṣkramati jantur ito nibaddho

devasya citta-nilayasya na māyayā syāt || ity-ādi |

vyāsa uvāca—

māyā bhaviṣyati na te puruṣottamasya

vaktraṁ pradarśaya nijaṁ mama vatsa! śīghram | ity-ādi |

garbha uvāca—

yady etya me sa bhavitā prati bhūr murārir

māyā-prahāṇa-viṣaye yadu-vaṁśanāthaḥ |

garbhaṁ vihāya tad ahaṁ bahir adya bhāvī

naivānyayā katham apīti viniścayo me || ity-ādi |

sūta uvāca—

itthaṁ tadīya-vacanaṁ dvija śuśruvān saḥ

vyāso’tiduḥkhita-manā prayayau tadānīm |

nārāyaṇaṁ vyavasito hṛdi tasya hetor

ānetum īṣad anivṛtta-matir bhavābdheḥ ||

tatra triloka-mahitena kṛta-praṇāmaḥ

kṛṣṇena viśva-suhṛdā dvija-daivatena |

prāptārhaṇo nikhilam ātma-hṛdistham asmai

vyāso nyavedayad asāv api tat prapede ||

kartavyam etad iha nāma kiyan madīyaṁ

gehaṁ pavitrayitum adya tavāgatasya |

tīrthasya jaṅgama-gatiṁ dadhato’ntaraṅga-

jaṁbāla-śodhana-paṭor iti cāha vācam ||

nārāyaṇāpaciti-bhājanam evam eṣa

bhūtva yayau dvija-varaḥ saha kṛṣṇa-yuktaḥ |

prāha praviśya punar eva sa kāndiśīkaḥ

kiñcit parānubhava-dhiṣṭhita-garbha-śaṅkī ||

vyāsa uvāca—

ayam asty āgato garbha! dvāravatyā gadādharaḥ |

gṛhītvainaṁ pratibhūvaṁ niṣkrāmaṁ kuru putraka ||

garbha uvāca—

tvaṁ brūhi mādhava! jagan-nigaḍopameyā

māyā’khilasya na vilaṅghyatamā tvadīyā |

badhnāti ṁāṁ na yadi garbham imaṁ vihāya

yāsyāmi samprati bahiḥ pratibhūs tvam atra ||

ā brahma viśvam akhilaṁ tava māyayaiva

baddhaṁ tvayaiva bhava-duḥkha-haras tvam eva | ity-ādi |

sūta uvāca—

itthaṁ tadīya-vacasā bhagavān pratītaḥ

prāha prahasya śanakair iti vācam īśaḥ |

yan māyayā na bhavitā tava samprayogo

yogāvṛtāṅga! kuru niṣkramaṇaṁ viśaṅkam ||gantāsi mokṣam adhunaiva mama prasādāt

jīvann avāpsyati mahodaya-saukhyam aṅga |yaṁ taṁ vadanti munayo bahu-duḥkha-yoga-

buddhir viyogam aviyoga-karaṁ mayā yat ||

vākyāvasānam anuviśya tatas tu garbhāt

tasyā jhaṭity atha vinirgata eva yogī |

sa dvādaśābda-mita-veṣa-vayāḥ praṇamya

vyāsaṁ hariṁ svajananīm atha sampratasthe ||

tataś ca pita-putra-samvādānantaram, śrī-bhagavān uvāca—

vyāsa tvadīya-tanayaḥ śukavan manojñaṁ

brūte vaco bhavatu tac chuka eva nāmnā |

harmye ca vatsyati tathāpi na māyayāyaṁ

spṛṣṭo yato mama kathā hṛdi māsya śokam ||

yāmo gṛhaṁ vayam api prakuruṣva vākyaṁ

yat tvām ayaṁ parama-bhāgavato’bhyuvāca | ity-ādi,

itthaṁ jagat-traya-guruḥ puruṣottamas taṁ

sambodhya bodha-nidhim āśu rathādhirūḍhaḥ |

āmantryabhārata-kaviṁ dvija-varyam uccais

tāṁ dvārakāṁ yadu-purīṁ drutam ājagāma || iti ||25||

viśvanāthaḥ : sa eva nīpaḥ punar api kṛtvī-saṁjñāyāṁ śukoka-nyāyāṁ śuko’yaṁ vyāsād araṇyāḥ saṁbhūto’nya eva | tad uktam harivaṁcādiṣu parāśara-kulotpannaḥ śuko nāma mahā-yaśāḥ vyāsād araṇyāṁ saṁbhūto vidhūmo’gnir iva jvalan | sa tasyāṁ pitṛ-kanyāyāṁ vīriṇyāṁ janayiṣyati | kṛṣṇaṁ gauraṁ prabhuṁ śambhuṁ, tathā bhūri-śrutaṁ jayam | kanyākm kīrtimatīṁ ṣaṣṭhīṁ yoginīṁ yoga-mātaram | brahmadattasya jananīṁ mahiṣī mahuhasya ca iti śrī-bhāgavata-vaktā śukas tu vyāsasya prathamaḥ putro’raṇi-sambhūtād anyaḥ | yad uktaṁ brahma-vaivarte—

prāpte kali-yuge vyāsaś cakre yo jagataḥ kṛte |

mahābhāratam ākhyānaṁ sarva-vedārtha-saṅgraham || ity-ādi |

tathā—

brahmacarya-paribhraṁśe mātur vākyād upasthite |

svayaṁ pita-ṛṇa-nālaṁ dvipavat soḍhum akṣamaḥ ||

vicintya manasā cakre bhāryāṁ jābāli-kanyakām |

vīṭikākhyāṁ dadau tasmai so’pi vaikhānasāśramī ||

tataś ca vyāsas tayā saha bahu-kālaṁ tapas tepe | tad-ante tasyāṁ vīryam ādhatta | sā ca garbhavatī ekādaśasu varṣeṣu vyatīteṣv api na prasūte sma |

atha dvādaśe varṣe vyāsaḥ kadācid garbhasthāṁ putram āha—bhoḥ putra ! kiṁ sva-mātaraṁ pīḍayasi ? garbhān niḥsareti |

sa cāha—garbhān niḥsṛtaṁ māyā mām abhibhāviṣyati, tasmād atraiva bhagavantaṁ dhyāpayāmīti |

vyāsa āha—māyā na tvām abhibhaviṣyatīti mad-vacanam eva pramāṇīkṛtya garbhān niḥsara, sva-mukhaṁ māṁ darśaya, mat-patnīṁ mā pīḍayeti |

sa āha—patnī-putrādy-āsaktatvena tvām api māyābhibhūtaṁ jānāmy atas tad-vacanaṁ na pramāṇīkaromi |

vyāsa āha—tarhi kasya vākyaṁ pramāṇīkuruṣe ?

sa āha—yasya māyeti |

vyāsa āha—tarhi tam atraivānayāmīty uktvā dvārakāṁ gatvā nivedita-sarva-vṛttāntaṁ bhagavantaṁ sva-parṇa-śālābhyantaram ānīya vyāsaḥ punar āha—bhoḥ putra ! āyāto’yaṁ bhagavān iti |

tataḥ sa āha—

tvāṁ brūhi, mādhava ! Jagan-nigaḍopamā

sā māyākhilasya na vilaṅghyatamā tvadīyā ||

badhnāti māṁ na yadi garbham imaṁ vihāya

yāsyāmi, samprati bahiḥ pratibhūs tvam atreti |

bhagavān āha—man-māyayā na bhavitā | tava samprayogo gantāsi mokṣam adhunaiva mama prasādād iti |

ato garbhān nisṛtya praṇamya bahu stuvānaṁ taṁ dṛṣṭvā bhagavān āha—vyāsa ! tvadīya-tanayaḥ śukavan manojñaṁ brūte | vaco bhavatu tac chuka eva nāmneti |

vyāsam āmantrya ratham āruhya dvārakāṁ pratasthe | śuko’pi pravavrāja vyāsas tam anujagāmeti | śrī-svāmi-caraṇās tu araṇī-sambhūtaḥ śuka eva virahāturaṁ pitaram anuvrajantam ālakṣya chāyā-śukaṁ nirmāya pravavrāja chāyā-śukasyaiva gārhasthya-vyavahāra ity āhuḥ | sa brahmadatto gavi sarasvatyām ||25||
