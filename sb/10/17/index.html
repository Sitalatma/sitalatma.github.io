SB 10.17

(10.17)

atha saptadaśo’dhyāyaḥ

kāliyasya yamunā-hrade nivāsasya kāraṇa-varṇanaṁ hradān nirgatena śrī-kṛṣṇena vrajaukasāṁ dāvānalād rakṣaṇam |

|| 10.17.1 ||

śrī-rājovāca—

nāgālayaṁ ramaṇakaṁ kathaṁ tatyāja kāliyaḥ |

kṛtaṁ kiṁ vā, suparṇasya tenaikenāsamañjasam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

nāgaṁ saptadaśe nāgā layaṁ taṁ nirayāpayat |

bandhūn sva-duḥkhataḥ śrāntān suptāṁs tatra davād apāt ||

asamañjasam apriyam ||1||

———————————————————————————————————————

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : taṁ nāgaṁ kāliyam | nirayāpayat niṣkāsitavān | tatra kālindī-taṭe | davāt vanāgneḥ |*| samañjasam priyam priye yogye samañjasam iti kośāt | tad bhinnam asamañjasam ||1||

———————————————————————————————————————

śrīnātha-cakravartī (caitanya-mata-mañjuṣā) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

sanātana-gosvāmī (bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : śrī-bhagavataḥ svasthatāṁ mahā-duṣṭe'pi kāruṇya-vistāraṇaṁ cākarṇya svasthaḥ prītamanāḥ san śrī-vṛndāvana-yamunāśrayena kāliyasya tasmin hṛade śrī-garuḍād bhayam apagatam, anyena vā kenāpi hetum iti tathā tādṛśa-mahā-tīrthe tasya duṣṭasya nivāse ko hetur iti ca vijñātuṁ kathāntare vautsukyena tat pūrva-vṛttaṁ pṛcchati—nāgeti | nāgānām ālayaṁ svabhāvataḥ sadā sarpa-varga-vasati-sthānam, na tu garuḍhasyety arthaḥ | ato bahavo nāgās tatra nivasanty eveti bhāvaḥ | tat kasmāt kāraṇād iti, śrī-vṛndāvana-yamunāśrayaṇārthaṁ kiṁ vānyena kenāpi hetunety arthaḥ |

nanu, yad bhayād iti tat sūcitam eva, tarhi śrī-garuḍāt sarveṣām eva nāgānāṁ sādhāraṇyena svato bhaye vartamāne'pi viśeṣatas tasyaikasya bhayo kiṁ kāraṇam iti pṛcchati kṛtam iti | vā-śabdaḥ kaṭākṣe, tat kathayeti śeṣaḥ ||1||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (laghu-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : abhīṣṭa-līlā-siddhyā śrī-bhagavatas tad abhudaya-darśanena śrī-vrajasya ca svasthatām ākarṇyaṁ svasthaḥ prītamaṇāḥ san kathā-madhya eva kathā-sauṣṭhavāya tat pūrva-vṛttaṁ pṛcchati—nāgeti | nāgānām ālayaṁ svabhāvataḥ sarpa-vargasya vasati-sthānaṁ na tu garuḍasyety arthaḥ | ato bahavo nāgas tatrānye nivasantyeveti bhāvaḥ |

nanu, yad bhayād iti tat sūcitam eva tatrāha kṛtaṁ kim iti vā-śabdaḥ kaṭākṣe ||1||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : śrī-bhagavato vrajasya ca svasthatām ākarṇya svasthaḥ kathā-madhyataḥ eva tat-sauṣṭhavāya pṛcchati—nāgeti ||1.5||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (bṛhat-krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

tārkṣyād bhītiḥ kāliyasya tārkṣye saubhari-śāpa-vāk |

kṛṣṇāptir goduhāndāvāt trāṇaṁ sapta-daśo’bhavat ||

———————————————————————————————————————

baladeva-vidyabhūṣaṇaḥ (vaiṣṇavānandinī) :

kāliyasya bhayaṁ tārkṣyāc chāpoktis tatra saubhareḥ |

hary-āptir goduhāṁ dāvādrakṣā saptadaśe’bhavat ||

asamañjasaṁ apriyam ||1||

|| 10.17.2 ||

śrī-śuka uvāca—

upahāryai sarpapjanair māsi māsīha yo baliḥ |

vānaspatyo mahābāho nāganāṁ prāṅ nirūpitaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : upahāryair bhakṣyaiḥ | sarpa-janaiḥ sarpāyatair janaiḥ | vānaspatyo vanas-pater mūle deyo nāgānāṁ tad-bādhā-parihārāya yo balir nirūpita upakalpitaḥ ||2||

———————————————————————————————————————

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : bali-rupahāraḥ baliḥ pūjopahārayoḥ iti viśvaḥ | he mahā-bāho iti | yathā mahā-rājasya parākrameṇa vairiṇo’pi rājāno baliṁ harantīti bhāvaḥ | sarpāyattā janāḥ sarpa-janāḥ | śāka-pārthivādir ayam | tad bādhā nāga-bādhā tan nivṛttaye | yad vā, vānaspatye’māyāṁ pratimāsaṁ sarpabhīnivṛtty arthaṁ sarve lokās tebhyo baliṁ dadatīty arthaḥ | vanaspati gataḥ somo yatra sa vānaspatyo darśaḥ

amāyām uḍurājo hi sadā yāti vanaspatim |

vānaspatyaṁ tatas tasyā nāma vai procyate budhaiḥ ||

iti kośāt | upahāryaiḥ sarpa-bhakṣya-bhūtais tat-pūjakair vā ||2||

———————————————————————————————————————

śrīnātha-cakravartī (caitanya-mata-mañjuṣā) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (laghu-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : upeti trikam | he mahābāho ! iti yathā bhavato mahā-rājasya parākrameṇa vairiṇe’pi rājāno balim upaharantīti bhāvaḥ ||2||

———————————————————————————————————————

sanātana-gosvāmī (bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : upeti trikam | upahāryair iti tair vyañcitam | yad vā, upahāryair bali-dāna-yogyaiḥ sarpāṇāṁ janaiḥ sevakaiḥ kṛtvā tad varety arthaḥ | iha nāgālaye vānaspatyaḥ phala-mūlādi-nirmito yo bahir nāgānāṁ nāgauḥ kartṛbhiḥ prāk prathamaṁ nirūpito dātuṁ niścitaḥ | he mahā-bāho, iti yathā bhavato mahā-rājasya parākrameṇa vairiṇo'pi rājāno bahim upaharantīti bhāvaḥ ||2||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ, bṛhat-krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : upahāryaiḥ bhakṣyatvenopahārīkriyamāṇaiḥ sarpa-rūpair janaiḥ vānaspatyaḥ vanaspate mule deyaḥ nāgānāṁ nāgair garuḍātsva-bādhā—parihārāya nirupitaḥ upakalpitaḥ ||2||

———————————————————————————————————————

baladeva-vidyabhūṣaṇaḥ (vaiṣṇavānandinī) : upahāryair garuḍasya bhakṣyaiḥ sarparūpair janai kartṛbhir yo valir nāgānāṁ garuḍād bādhāparihārāya prāk nirūpita āsīt | vānaspatyo vanaspater mūle deyaḥ ||2||

|| 10.17.3 ||

svaṁ svaṁ bhāgaṁ prayacchanti nāgāḥ parvaṇi parvaṇi |

gopīthāyātmanaḥ sarve suparṇāya mahātmane ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : te ca nāgāḥ svaṁ svaṁ bhāgaṁ janair dattaṁ suparṇāya prayacchanti tato bhītāḥ | gopīthāya rakṣaṇāya ||

———————————————————————————————————————

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : parvaṇi-parvaṇi darśedarśe’vasare’vasare vā

parva valībe mahe grantau prastāve lakṣaṇāntare |

darśa-pratipadoḥ sandhau viṣuvat-prabhṛtiṣv api || iti medinī |

tataḥ suparṇāt | mahātmane balavattarāya ||3||

———————————————————————————————————————

śrīnātha-cakravartī (caitanya-mata-mañjuṣā) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

sanātana-gosvāmī (bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : parvaṇi parvaṇi pratidarśanam, mahātmane aparicchinna-śaktaya ity arthaḥ, ayaṁ bhāga—pradāne hetuḥ | yad vā, mahāśayāya phalādi-bali-pradāna-mātreṇaiva sahaja-viriṣv api santuṣṭāyety arthaḥ ||3||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (laghu-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : parvaṇi parvaṇi prati pañcadaśyantaṁ mahātmane aparicchinna-śaktaya ity arthaḥ | ayaṁ bhāga-pradāne hetuḥ ||3||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ, bṛhat-krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tatra sarve nāgās taṁ svaṁ-svaṁ bhāgaṁ prayacchanti parvaṇi parvaṇi pratipañcadaśyantaṁ gopīthāya rakṣaṇāya kintu kāliyas tu taṁ na prayacchati pratyutānyair dattam api svayam eva babhuje ||3||

———————————————————————————————————————

baladeva-vidyabhūṣaṇaḥ (vaiṣṇavānandinī) : tatra sarve nāgāḥ parvaṇi parvaṇi pratipakṣa-daśyantaṁ, garuḍāya svaṁ svaṁ bhāgaṁ prayacchanti kim artham ? ātmano gopīthāya rakṣaṇāya ||3||

|| 10.17.4 ||

viṣa-vīrya-madāviṣṭaḥ kādraveyas tu kāliyaḥ |

kadarthī-kṛtya garuḍaṁ svayam taṁ bubhuje balim ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : viṣa-vīryābhyāṁ yo madas tenāviṣṭaḥ | kādraveyaḥ kadrū-putraḥ | garuḍa kadarthī-kṛtya avigaṇayya | yad vā, upahāryaiḥ suparṇa-bhakṣyaiḥ sarpā eva janās tair nāgānāṁ sambandhī yasminn ekaiko nāgo dīyate tathā-bhūto yo baliḥ suparṇāya nirūpitas tatra svaṁ svaṁ bhāgaṁ sarve nāgāḥ prayacchanti, kāliyas tu na prayacchati, kintv anyair dattam api taṁ baliṁ svayam eva bubhuje ||4||

———————————————————————————————————————

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : śāstrāntara-sammatyā kliṣṭa-kalpanayā ca pūrva-vyākhyānam asammatam iva mahatvāha—yad veti | purāṇtare—ekaikaṁ hi dadur-nāgaṁ nāgās tārkṣyāya rakṣayā ity ukteḥ tatra teṣu ||4||

———————————————————————————————————————

śrīnātha-cakravartī (caitanya-mata-mañjuṣā) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

sanātana-gosvāmī (bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : paścād viṣa-vīryābhyāṁ mahad āviṣṭaḥ san kadrāveya iti bhrātṛtvaṁ ca made hetvantaraṁ jñeyam, kadarthī-kṛtya tad anādareṇa tadīyebhyo balād-grahaṇenaiva vā ||4||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (laghu-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : paścāt viṣa-vīryābhyāṁ mad āviṣṭaḥ san kādraveya iti bhrātṛtvaṁ ca made hetvantaraṁ jñeyam | kadarthī-kṛtya tad anādareṇa tac chrutveti vakṣyamāṇāt tadīyebhyo balād grahaṇenaiva vā, kadarthī-kṛtya svayaṁ tu svayam eva ||4||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ, bṛhat-krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : kadarthī-kṛtya anādṛtya ||4||

sanātana-gosvāmī (bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : he rājann iti cakravartino bali-prada-rāja-viprati-pattyā kopo bhavatīti bhavatā jñāyata eveti bhāvaḥ | yad vā, rājamānaḥ kopena tejobhara-prākaṭyāt svato vā vidyotamānaḥ yato bhagavān sarva-śakti-yuktaḥ bhagavataḥ priyaś ca pārṣada-pravara ity arthaḥ | samyaṅ-māraṇodyotatayā samīpa eva vegenāgacchat | tatra hetuḥ—mahā-vego'paricchinna-java iti ||5||

|| 10.17.6 ||

tam āpatantaṁ tarasā viṣāyudhaḥ

pratyabhyayād utthita-naika-mastakaḥ |

dadbhiḥ suparṇaṁ vyadaśad dad-āyudhaḥ

karāla-jihvocchvasitogra-locanaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : viṣam evāyudhaṁ yasya saḥ | pratyabhyayād yoddhuṁ pratijagām | ucchrita-naika-mastaka unnamitāneka-phaṇaḥ | dad-āyudho dantāyudhaḥ | karāla-jihvaś cāsāv ucchvasitogra-locanaś ca ucchvasitāny ujjṛmbhitāny ugrāṇi locanāni yasya saḥ ||6||

|| 10.17.7 ||

taṁ tārkṣya-putraḥ sa nirasya manyumān

pracaṇḍa-vego madhusūdanāsanaḥ |

pakṣeṇa savyena hiraṇya-rociṣā

jaghāna kadrū-sutam ugra-vikramaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tārkṣya-putro garuḍaḥ | madhusūdanasyāsanaṁ yasmin saḥ ||7||

———————————————————————————————————————

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : tārkṣau garuḍa-kaśyapau ity amaraḥ | tam kāliyam | savyena dakṣiṇe vāmenavā savyaṁ dakṣiṇa-vāmayoḥ iti vaijayanti ||7||

———————————————————————————————————————

śrīnātha-cakravartī (caitanya-mata-mañjuṣā) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

eṣa ghoratamo vahnis tāvakān grasate hi naḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : he rāmeti | asyāpi tad dine sarvajñatva-darśanenāyam api kṛṣṇa-bhrātā devāviṣṭa ity anumānāt | tavakānity uktir dayotpādanārthā ||23||

———————————————————————————————————————

śrīnātha-cakravartī (caitanya-mata-mañjuṣā) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

sanātana-gosvāmī (bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : tat-prakāram evāha—kṛṣṇeti | vīpsā saṁbhrameṇa, sneha-bhara-svabhāvena vā mahad-bhāgam, samūhārthe aṇ bhaga-śabda-vācyam aiśvarya-vṛndaṁ yasya tat-sambodhanam, atas tavāśakyaṁ kiñcid api nāstīti bhāvaḥ | yad vā, mahān bhāgo bhāgyam asmād dṛśānāṁ yasmād iti tvat sākṣād asmākaṁ duḥaṁ nopayuktam iti bhāvaḥ amito'nanto vikramaḥ śauryaṁ yasyeti baladevaṁ prati sambodhanam, tava vīryeṇa dāvāgnir api nirvātīti bhāvaḥ | yad vā, śrī-bala-bhadraṁ parityajya kathaṁ mām eva śaraṇaṁ yāthety āśakyāhuḥ—rāmād api rāmeṇāpi vā, amito mātum aśakyo vikramo yasyaḥ | yad vā, ramayatīti rāmo'mito vikramo yasya saḥ | eṣa iti pratyakṣatvaṁ śīghratvaṁ vā bodhayati, ghoratamaḥ, apratikāryatvāt tvd antika-prāptatvād vā grasate niḥśeṣeṇa saṁharatīty arthaḥ | tāvakān iti kṛpā-jananārthaṁ grasanāyogyatva-bodhanārthaṁ vā, hi niścitam ||23||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (laghu-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : tat prakāram evāha—kṛṣṇeti | vikṣāsambhrameṇa sneha-bhara-svabhāvena vā mahān bhāgo bhāgyam asmād dṛśānāṁ yasmād iti tvat sākṣād asmākaṁ duḥkhaṁ nopayuktam iti bhāvaḥ | amitaḥ ananto vikramaḥ śauryaṁ yasyeti baladevaṁ prati sambodhanaṁ tava vīryeṇa dāvāgnir api nirvātīti bhāvaḥ evaṁ tadāpi teṣāṁ mahā-prabhāvatvajñānam eva jātaṁ na-tv aiśvarya-jñānam iti bhāvaḥ | eṣa iti pratyakṣatvaṁ śīghratvaṁ vā bodhayati ghoratamaḥ apratikāryatvāt yuṣmad antika-prāptatvād vā grasate niśśeṣeṇa saṁharatīty arthaḥ | tāvakān iti kṛpā-jananārthaṁ grasanāyogyatva-bodhanārthaṁ vā tava kamamakāv-eka-vacanam iti taddhita nimittakād ādeśa-sūtrādyad atraikasyaiva sambandhaḥ pratipādyate hi niścayaṁ tat khalu dvayor abheda-pratipādanārtham iti jñeyam ||23||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (bṛhat-krama-sandarbhaḥ) : vastutas tu śrī-kṛṣṇa-vigrahasyātmatvāt | tathā ca acchedyo’yam adāhyo’yam [gītā 2.24] ity-ādi | tena tad avigraha evātmā ||23.25||

———————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : he rāmeti tasyāpi tad dine sarvajñatva-darśanādayam api kṛṣṇa-bhrātā devāviṣṭa ity anumānāt ||23||

———————————————————————————————————————

baladeva-vidyabhūṣaṇaḥ (vaiṣṇavānandinī) : he rāmeti ! dhenuka-vadhena tasyāti-vikramāvagamāt ||23||

|| 10.17.24 ||

sudustarān naḥ svān pāhi kālāgneḥ suhṛdaḥ prabho |

na śaknumas tvac-caraṇaṁ santyaktum akuto-bhayam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : kālāgner mṛtyu-rūpād agneḥ | na mṛtyor bibhīmaḥ, kintu tvac-caraṇa-viyogād ity āhuḥ--na śaknuma iti ||24-25||

———————————————————————————————————————

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : mṛtyor nityatvāt tato na bhayam ity āhuḥ—neti | tarhi kutaḥ ? tatrāhuḥ—kiṁ tv iti | prabho ! he sarvaṁ kartuṁ samartha ! na kuto’pi bhayaṁ yasmāt, tam | ato nija-caraṇa-parityāga-bhayam asmākam āśu vināśayeti bhāvaḥ ||24||

———————————————————————————————————————

śrīnātha-cakravartī (caitanya-mata-mañjuṣā) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

sanātana-gosvāmī (bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : svān jñātīn ātmīyān vā, su śobhanaṁ hṛd yeṣāṁ, tān, sad-bhāvena tad-eka-niṣṭhān ity arthaḥ | prabho ! he sarvaṁ kartuṁ samartha ! na kuto'pi bhayaṁ yasmāt, tam ato nija-rakṣayāsmākaṁ bhayam āśu vināśayeti bhāvaḥ |

yad vā—nanu, mamāpy ayaṁ bhayaṅkara iti cet, tatrāhuḥ—na kuto'pi bhayaṁ yasyeti, sākṣād adhunaiva darśanād iti bhāvaḥ, ataḥ samyak maraṇenātyanta-viyuktatayā na tyaktuṁ śaknuma iti ||24||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (laghu-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : svān jñātīn ātmīyān vā | su śobhanaṁ hṛt yeṣāṁ, tān | sad-bhāvena tad-eka-niṣṭhān ity arthaḥ | prabho ! he sarvaṁ kartuṁ samarthaḥ ! na kuto’pi bhayaṁ yasmāt, tam ato nija-caraṇa-parityāga-bhayam asmākam āśu vināśayeti bhāvaḥ | ataḥ samyak kṣaṇam api viyuktatayā tyaktuṁ na śaknuma iti ||24||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : akuto-bhayam iti tvac-charaṇa-prāptiṁ vinā anyat sarvam evāsmākaṁ bhaya-hetur ity arthaḥ ||24||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (bṛhat-krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : kālo mṛtyus tad-rūpād agneḥ | mṛtyau sati tvac-caraṇena saha viyogo bhavet | sa tu duḥsaha ity āhuḥ—na śaknuma iti ||24||

———————————————————————————————————————

baladeva-vidyabhūṣaṇaḥ (vaiṣṇavānandinī) : kālāgner iti mṛtyu-rupād vahner ity arthaḥ | bhṛto sati tvac-caraṇābhyāṁ saha viyogaḥ syāt, sa ca dusaha ity āha—na śaknuma iti ||24||

|| 10.17.25 ||

itthaṁ sva-jana-vaiklavyaṁ nirīkṣya jagad-īśvaraḥ |

tam agnim apibat tīvram ananto'nanta-śakti-dhṛk ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : tam vanodbhūtam | ananta-śakti-dhṛg iti vahni-pāna-śaktir api śrī-kṛṣne’stīty āha | nanu, parama-sukumāraḥ kathaṁ tīvram agnim apibat ? tatrāha—ananta iti | tasya saṁhārikā śaktir evāpibat, tasmin śaktim ati tat-pānopacāra-mātram iti bhāvaḥ | dhṛju-gatau bhv-ādiḥ, tataḥ kvipi dhṛg iti rūpam ||25||

vimohān jñāta-bhagavat-tattvān | aho yena mahā-viṣaḥ kāliyo nirastas so’yaṁ sākṣād īśa eveti | tata īśatvād eva | anantaś cāsau vana-vahniś ceti ||*||

———————————————————————————————————————

śrīnātha-cakravartī (caitanya-mata-mañjuṣā) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

sanātana-gosvāmī (bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : ittham anena kākūkty-ādi-prakāreṇa | nirīkṣya sākṣād anubhūyeva | yad vā, ittham īdṛśa-dāvāgni-kṛtam ity arthaḥ | teṣāṁ vijñāpanāt pūrvam evālokya taṁ śuṣka-vanodbhūtaṁ sarvato vyāpakam ativṛddhaṁ cet ity arthaḥ, atas tīvraṁ duḥsaham ity apratikāryatvam uktam, tathā-bhūtam apibad iva niḥśeṣeṇa pānābhinayādinā nāśayāmāsety arthaḥ | tatra ca yuktir nānusandheyety āha—jagatāṁ jagat-sraṣṭṛ-brahmādīnām api īśvaraḥ sarva-śakti-prada ity arthaḥ | tasmād evāgner api śaktiḥ | yad vā, parameśvaratvena durvitarkyaṁ lilatvād iti bhāvaḥ | yad vā, devatānām agni-mukhāgnyor abhedena sva-sthāne śrī-mukha-madhye sthāpitavān ity arthaḥ | evam agner api sukham akarod iti jñeyam, yato jagad-īśvaraḥ sarva-deva-sukha-pradaś cety arthaḥ |

nanu, tarhi śrī-mukhāntar-agner vartamāṇatvenāsmādṛśāṁ manasi santoṣo na syād ity āśaṅkyāha—ananto'paricchinnaḥ | kasmin śrī-mukhāntar-bhāge'gnir gataḥ, tam api lakṣayituṁ kaścid api na śakta ity arthaḥ |

nanu, parama-snigdhataraiḥ śrī-nanda-yaśodādibhiḥ sākṣāt tad agni-pānaṁ kathaṁ soḍham ? kathaṁ vā prasahya na nivāritam ? tatrāha—ananta-śakti-dhṛk, tathā tat-pānaṁ cakre, yathā taiḥ kathañcil lakṣayituṁ na śaktam ity arthaḥ ||25||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (laghu-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : itthaṁ sva-premaika-mūlakāneka-kākūkty-ādi-prakārakaṁ nirīkṣya anubhūya taṁ tādṛśaṁ atas tīvraṁ duḥsahaṁ tathā-bhūtam apy apibat kāruṇya-maya-premāveśeneti bhāvaḥ |

nanu, bhavatu tad-āveśaḥ, tena kathaṁ tat-pānaṁ syād ity āśaṅkya gūḍham api tad-aiśvaryaṁ sva-samaye svayam eva vyaktī-bhavatīty abhipretya siddhāntayati—jagatām īśvaraḥ sarveṣu tat-tac-chakti-prada ity arthaḥ | tasmād evāgner api śakteḥ ko nāma vismayaḥ ? iti bhāvaḥ |

nanu, gopa-bālaka-rūpaḥ samantād bhavam agniṁ katham apibat ? tatrāha—anantaḥ tādṛśasyaiva vigrahasya vibhutvena svayam api samantāt prakāśamāna ity arthaḥ | na ca tan-mātra-śaktitvam api āścaryam ity āha—ananta-śakti-dhṛg iti | ata eva śrī-gopā api nivārayituṁ nāvasaraṁ labdhavanta ity arthaḥ ||25||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : apibat kṛpāveśena pānābhinayenaiva saṁhṛtavān | tatra hetuḥ--jagad-īśvaraḥ agny-ādīnāṁ sarveṣām eva tat-tac-chakti-pravartaka-nivartakaḥ samantād gatāgni-pāne yuktiḥ || anantaḥ sarvatra prakāśamāna-sva-vigrahaḥ tādṛśa-śaktyāvan adbhutatvam ananta-śakti-dhṛg iti ||25||

———————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (bṛhat-krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

———————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : sva-jana-vaiklavyaṁ dṛṣṭveti sva-jana-viṣayakas tat premaiva teṣāṁ rakṣaṇārthaṁ tamaiśvaryam anusandhāpayāmāseti bhāvaḥ |

nanu, parama-sukumāraḥ kathaṁ tīvram agnim apibat ? tatrāha—ananta-śakti-dhṛk tasya saṁhārikā śaktir evāpibat | tasmin śaktimati tat-pānopacāra-mātram iti bhāvaḥ ||25||

iti sārārtha-darśinyāṁ hārṣiṇyāṁ bhakta-cetasām |

daśame’smin sapta-daśaḥ saṅgataḥ saṅgataḥ satām ||*||

———————————————————————————————————————

baladeva-vidyabhūṣaṇaḥ (vaiṣṇavānandinī) : nanu, parama-sukumāraḥ kathaṁ tīvram agnim apibat ? tatrāha—ananta-śakti dhṛg iti | tam ayati tīṣau sati tasya duṣṭa-saṁhāriṇī śaktir eva tam api vadity arthaḥ ||25||

iti śrīmad-bhāgavate mahā-purāṇe brahma-sūtra-bhāṣye pāramahaṁsyaṁ saṁhitāyāṁ vaiyāsikyāṁ daśama-skandhe dāvāgni-mocanaṁ nāma saptadaśo’dhyāyaḥ |
