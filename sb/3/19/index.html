SB 3.19

(3.19)

ekonaviṁśo’dhyāyaḥ

brahmaṇaḥ prārthanām aṅgīkṛtya varāha-kṛto hiraṇyākṣa-vadhaḥ |

|| 3.19.1 ||

maitreya uvāca—

avadhārya viriñcasya nirvyalīkāmṛtaṁ vacaḥ |

prahasya prema-garbheṇa tad apāṅgena so’grahīt ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

ūnaviṁśe viriñcyādi- prārthitena mahā-mṛdhe |

varāheṇa hiraṇyākṣa- vadhaḥ ślāghyo’nuvarṇyate ||

nirvyalīkaṁ ca tad amṛtaṁ ca | pāṭhāntare niṣkapaṭābhiprāyaṁ ca tad ṛtaṁ ca | kālātmano’pi mama muhūrta-balam upadiśatīti prahasyāpāṅgālokanena svī-kṛtavān ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : nirvyalīkam akapaṭam amṛtaṁ priyaṁ ca | premaika-mayatvāt | pāṭhāntare ṛtam arthato’pi niṣkapaṭam ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

yuddhe tasyāsamarthasya māyāḥ sṛṣṭavato hariḥ |

svāstreṇa hṛtvā tam ahann ūnaviṁśe sva-pāṇinā ||

nirvyalīkaṁ niṣkapaṭam asmad-rāddha-varo’sura iti | amṛtaṁ na yāvad eṣa vardhetety-ādi-prema-mayatvād amṛta-tulyaṁ vacas tad-bhagavatāpyādareṇāsvāditaṁ tad ity āha—prahasyeti | aho mat-premnaḥ sarva-vismārakatā-sāmarthyaṁ mat-kālātmano’pi mama muhūrta-balam upadiśatīti prema-garbheṇaivāpāṅgena svīcakāra, satyaṁ, tvaṁ yathā diśasi, tathaiva kurvann imam adhunaiva hanmi | kintu mamāsya ca yuyutsā-sukhaṁ pūryatām ato’dya rātrāv avainaṁ haniṣyāmīti jñāpayāmāsety arthaḥ ||1||

...

|| 3.19.2 ||

tataḥ sapatnaṁ mukhataś carantam akuto-bhayam |

jaghānotpatya gadayā hanāv asuram akṣajaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : mukhataḥ abhimukhe | hanau kapolasyādho-bhage | akṣajo brahmaṇo ghrāṇendriyād āvirbhūtaḥ ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : akṣajaḥ yo’dhokṣajo’pi | samprati netrādiṣu prakaṭībhūta ity arthaḥ ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : mukhataḥ saṁmukha eva | hanau kapolasyādho-bhage | akṣajo brahmaṇo ghrāṇendriyād āvirbhūtaḥ ||2||

...

|| 3.19.3 ||

sā hatā tena gadayā vihatā bhagavat-karāt |

vighūrṇitāpatad reje tad adbhutam ivābhavat ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sā sva-gandayā tena hatā tato vihatā vicyuta satī vighūrṇitā bhūtvāpatad reje ca | tad bhagavat-karāt patanam | yad vā, tat padasyāvṛttyā reje ity anenāpi saṁbandhaḥ tad daitya-pauruṣaṁ reje iti ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tad bhagavat-kārāt patanam adbhutam ivābhavad itīva-śabdo bhagavad-icchayā tat-tat-sambhavān na vastuto’dbhūtam iti bodhayati ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : sā harer gadā tenāsureṇa vihatā vicyutā satī vighūrṇitā bhūtvāpatat | vireje ca | tat bhagavat-kārāt patanam ||3||

...

|| 3.19.4 ||

sa tadā labdha-tīrtho’pi na babādhe nirāyudham |

mānayan sa-mṛdhe dharmaṁ viṣvaksenaṁ prakopayan ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : dharmaḥ satyaḥ iti proktā dharmaś cāpi hareḥ priyaḥ iti brāhme ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : labdha-tīrtho labdhāvasaraḥ sa ca na babādhe na prāharat | sa mānayan babhūveti vākya-bhedāt sa ity asyāpaunar uktyam ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : sa-mṛdhe sa-yuddhe jane yo dharmas tam ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : labdha-tīrthaḥ labdhasaro’pi na babādhe na prāharat | tatra hetuḥ—mānayann iti | tena ca prakopayann iti—kim are mām api tvad-bāhu-balād eva patita-gadaṁ vyākulaṁ jānāsi yat sva-dhārmikatvaṁ prathayan na praharasi | paśya re paśya | kṣaṇa-mātreṇaiva tvat-prāṇān evāpaharāmīti bhagavāṁś cukopety arthaḥ ||4||

...

|| 3.19.5 ||

gadāyām apaviddhāyāṁ hāhā-kāre vinirgate |

mānayām āsa tad-dharmaṁ sunābhaṁ cāsmarad vibhuḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : sunābhaṁ ceti ca-kāreṇa pūrvaṁ gadām api sasmāreti gamyate ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : apaviddhāyāṁ patitāyāṁ, na caitat śālva-yuddhe sārṅgadhanuḥ-patanam iva paramatam āśaṅkanīyam | hiraṇyākṣasya pārṣadatvāt pārṣadasya ca bhagavat-tulya-balatvāt tulya-balatve ca gadā-patanasyāsambhavatvābhāvāt | tulya-balatvaṁ vinā ca yuddha-sukhasyānutpatter gadā-patanam idaṁ bhagavad-utsāha-vardhakatvād bhūṣaṇam eva, na tu dūṣaṇam | bhakta-sthāne bhagavatā premāmbudhinā sva-parābhavasya śataśo’ṅgīkṛtatvād ity etad-artha-vyañjakam utaṁra-śloke sva-pārṣada-mukheneti padam anusandheyam | sunābhaṁ ceti, ca-kārād gadām api pūrvaṁ sasmāraiveti gamyate ||5||

...

|| 3.19.6 ||

taṁ vyagra-cakraṁ diti-putrādhamena

sva-pārṣada-mukhyena viṣajjamānam |

citrā vāco’tad-vidāṁ khe-carāṇāṁ

tatra smāsan svasti te’muṁ jahīti ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : vyagraṁ sa-saṁbhramaṁ cakraṁ yasya | viṣajjamānaṁ viśeṣeṇa saṅgaṁ prāpnuvantaṁ prati | atad-vidāṁ tat-prabhāvam ajānatām | āsamantād āsan sma ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : bahir diti-putrādhamena antas tu sva-pārṣada-mukhyenety arthaḥ | ditijābhidheneti pāṭhe nāma-mātratas tad-rūpeṇa ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : vyagraṁ saṁbhrama-yuktaṁ hanta hanta bhagavān māṁ smarati smeti svayam evāgatya haste lagnaṁ cakraṁ yasya tam | atra diti-putrābhidhena diti-putrādhameneti pāṭha-trayam | viṣajjamānaṁ vīkṣyeti śeṣaḥ ||6||

...

|| 3.19.7 ||

sa taṁ niśāmyātta-rathāṅgam agrato

vyavasthitaṁ padma-palāśa-locanam |

vilokya cāmarṣa-pariplutendriyo

ruṣā sva-danta-cchadam ādaśac chvasan ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sa daityas tam ātta-cakraṁ niśāmya dṛṣṭvā agrato vyavasthitaṁ ca vilokya āmarṣeṇa krodhena pariplutāni kṣubhitāni indriyāṇi yasya saḥ | ādaśat daṣṭavān ||7||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : prathamam ātta-rathāṅgaṁ niśāmya dṛṣṭvā punar agrato vyavasthitaṁ ca vilokya | danta-cchadam adharam ||7||

...

|| 3.19.8 ||

karāla-daṁṣṭraś cakṣurbhyāṁ sañcakṣāṇo dahann iva |

abhiplutya sva-gadayā hato’sīty āhanad dharim ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : cakṣurbhyāṁ dahann iva sañcakṣāṇaḥ paśyan | iva iti vastutaḥ krodhābhāvam āha—hato’si jñāto’sīti vāstavo’rthaḥ ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : sañcakṣāṇaḥ paśyan hato’si | pakṣe, jñāto’si ||8||

...

|| 3.19.9 ||

padā savyena tāṁ sādho bhagavān yajña-sūkaraḥ |

līlayā miṣataḥ śatroḥ prāharad vāta-raṁhasam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā), viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : vāta-raṁhasaṁ vāyu-vegām ||9|

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

...

|| 3.19.10 ||

āha cāyudham ādhatsva ghaṭasva tvaṁ jigīṣasi |

ity uktaḥ sa tadā bhūyas tāḍayan vyanadad bhṛśam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : ghaṭasvodyamaṁ kuru | yatas tvaṁ jetum icchasi ||10||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ), viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : na vyākhyātam.

...

|| 3.19.11 ||

tāṁ sa āpatatīṁ vīkṣya bhagavān samavasthitaḥ |

jagrāha līlayā prāptāṁ garutmān iva pannagīm ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

yathecchayaiva sarve tu manasā dehato’pi vā |

kartuṁ śakto’pi śastrādyā līlaivānanta-śaktitaḥ || iti varāhe ||11||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā), jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tāṁ gadām ||11||

...

|| 3.19.12 ||

sva-pauruṣe pratihate hata-māno mahāsuraḥ |

naicchad gadāṁ dīyamānāṁ hariṇā vigata-prabhaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : hato māno garvo yasya ||12||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : daitya-mahābhaṭena hiraṇyākṣeṇa arpitaṁ kṣiptam | ajjñitam iti tārkṣya-patatrasya tyaktatva-mātrāṁśe tātparyam | na tu tadvad icchayaiva tat-siddham ity aṁśe’pi ||12||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : na vyākhyātam.

...

|| 3.19.13 ||

jagrāha tri-śikhaṁ śūlaṁ jvalaj-jvalana-lolupam |

yajñāya dhṛta-rūpāya viprāyābhicaran yathā ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : jvalanyo jvalanas tadval lolupaṁ grasana-vyagram | yajñāya viṣṇum ālakṣya | akārya-karatve dṛṣṭāntaḥ, vipram uddiśyābhicāraṁ kurvan yathā ||13||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : yajñāyeti | yajño viṣṇus tad-arthaṁ lakṣyīkṛtyety arthaḥ | viprārthaṁ vipram uddiśya abhicaran yathā havanīya-dravyaṁ gṛhṇāti tadvat ||13||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : jvalat jvālā-yuktaṁ jvalano vahnis tadval lolupaṁ grasana-vyagraṁ yajñāya mūrtimantaṁ yajñam iva hantum | atyantānaucitye dṛṣṭāntaḥ | viprāya hantum ||13||

...

|| 3.19.14 ||

tad ojasā daitya-mahā-bhaṭārpitaṁ

cakāsad antaḥ-kha udīrṇa-dīdhiti |

cakreṇa ciccheda niśāta-neminā

harir yathā tārkṣya-patatram ujjhitam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : antaḥ sve ākāśa-madhye cakāsat prakāśamānam | udīrṇā utkaṭā dīdhitir dīptir yasya | niśāta-neminā śita-dhāreṇa | harir indro yathā tārkṣyasya patatram ujjhitaṁ ciccheda | devān vinirjitya amṛta-kalaśaṁ nayatā garuḍena indra-prayukta-vajrasyāmoghasya mānaṁ dātuṁ piccham ekaṁ tyaktaṁ tad yathendraś ciccheda, chinnaṁ ca tad yathā sve pracakāśe tadvat prakāśamānam ity abhisandhiḥ ||14||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : cakāsad dīpyamānam antar yasya tathā khe ākāśe udīrṇā udgatā bahir api dīdhitayo yasya tat | yad vā, cakāsantyaḥ antaḥ-khe ākāśa-madhye udīrṇā dīdhitayo yasya tat | niśāta-neminā tīkṣṇa-dhāreṇa harir indro yathā tārkṣyasya garuḍasya patatraṁ pakṣam ujjhitaṁ tyaktaṁ ciccheda | devān jitvā amṛta-kalasaṁ nayatā garuḍena indra-prayukta-vajrasya amogha-rakṣaṇārthaṁ piccham ekaṁ tyaktam | tad yathā indraś ciccheda chinnaṁ ca yathā khe prakāśate, tadvat prakāśamānam ity api sambandhaḥ ||14||

...

|| 3.19.15 ||

vṛkṇe sva-śūle bahudhāriṇā hareḥ

pratyetya vistīrṇam uro vibhūtimat |

pravṛddha-roṣaḥ sa kaṭhora-muṣṭinā

nadan prahṛtyāntaradhīyatāsuraḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : arāḥ santy asyety ari cakraṁ, tena bahudhā vṛkṇe chinne sati praty etyābhimukham āgatya harer uro vakṣaḥ prahṛtya ||15||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : vṛkṇa ity-ādiṣu pūrva-pūrvavat krodhādhikaṁ tad-ābhāsa eva | prahārādikaṁ ca mahā-sāratvaṁ jñātvaiva ||15||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : vṛkne chinne uraḥ prahṛtya sākṣād yuddhe sva-sāmarthyābhāvam avadhārya māyāḥ srakṣyann antardadhau ||15||

...

|| 3.19.16 ||

tenettham āhataḥ kṣattar bhagavān ādi-sūkaraḥ |

nākampata manāk kvāpi srajā hata iva dvipaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā), viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : manāg īṣad api | kvāpy aṁśe ||16||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tathaivānantaram āha—tenettham iti | ādiḥ parama-kāraṇa-rūpaś cāsau śūkarākṛtiś ceti ||16||

...

|| 3.19.17 ||

athorudhāsṛjan māyāṁ yoga-māyeśvare harau |

yāṁ vilokya prajās trastā menire’syopasaṁyamam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : asya jagataḥ | upasaṁyamaṁ pralayam ||17||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : prajāḥ brahma-putrādayaḥ ||17||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : prathamam ātta-rathāṅgaṁ niśāmya dṛṣṭvā punar agrato vyavasthitaṁ ca vilokya | danta-cchadam adharam ||17||

...

|| 3.19.18 ||

pravavur vāyavaś caṇḍās tamaḥ pāṁsavam airayan |

digbhyo nipetur grāvāṇaḥ kṣepaṇaiḥ prahitā iva ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : pāṁsu-kṛtaṁ tamaś ca preritavantaḥ | kṣepaṇair yantraiḥ ||18||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : kṣepaṇair yantraiḥ ||18||

...

|| 3.19.19 ||

dyaur naṣṭa-bhagaṇābhraughaiḥ sa-vidyut-stanayitnubhiḥ |

varṣadbhiḥ pūya-keśāsṛg- viṇ-mūtrāsthīni cāsakṛt ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : naṣṭo bhagaṇo nakṣatra-samūho yasyām | anena daitya-balātirekād brahma-datta-muhūrtātikramo gamyate | ahni nakṣatrāṇām asaṁbhavāt ||19||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : stanayitnur atra meghajyotir-ākhyaḥ | kartari prayogāt ||18||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : naṣṭa-bhagaṇety anena yuddha-kutūhalinā bhagavatā brahma-datta-muhūrtasyātikramo gamyate ||19||

|| 3.19.20 ||

girayaḥ pratyadṛśyanta nānāyudha-muco’nagha |

dig-vāsaso yātudhānyaḥ śūlinyo mukta-mūrdhajāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : nānāyudhāni muñcantīti tathā yātudhānyaś ca pratyadṛśyanta ||20||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

|| 3.19.21 ||

bahubhir yakṣa-rakṣobhiḥ patty-aśva-ratha-kuñjaraiḥ |

ātatāyibhir utsṛṣṭā hiṁsrā vāco’tivaiśasāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : hiṁsrāc chindhi-bhindhīty evaṁ-bhūtā ativaiśasā atyugrā vāca utsṛṣṭā ity atraiva vākya-samāptiḥ ||21||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

|| 3.19.22 ||

prāduṣkṛtānāṁ māyānām āsurīṇāṁ vināśayat |

sudarśanāstraṁ bhagavān prāyuṅkta dayitaṁ tri-pāt ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : prāduṣkṛtānām iti prakaṭitā māyā vināśayat | “vināśanam” iti pāṭhe yathāśrutaiva ṣāṣṭhī | trīṇi savanāni pādā yasya | yajña-mūrtir ity arthaḥ | trayo asya pādāḥ iti śruteḥ ||22||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : vināśanaṁ vināśakaṁ "vināśayan" iti pāṭhe dvitīyārthe ṣaṣṭhyaḥ | trayas tapa-ādayaḥ pādā yasya sa tripāt dharma-mūrtiḥ | yad vā, trīṇi savanāni pādā yasya | trayo asya pādāḥ iti śruteḥ | yajña-mūrtir ity arthaḥ ||22||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā), viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : bhartur ādeśaṁ tvat-putrau bhagavān haniṣyatīty evaṁ-bhūtam ||23||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.
