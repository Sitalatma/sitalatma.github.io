SB 10.65

pañcaṣaṣṭitamo’dhyāyaḥ

balabhadrasya vraje gamanaṁ, kālindī-karṣaṇaṁ ca |

|| 10.65.1 ||

śrī-śuka uvāca—

balabhadraḥ kuru-śreṣṭha bhagavān ratham āsthitaḥ |

suhṛd-didṛkṣur utkaṇṭhaḥ prayayau nanda-gokulam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

pañca-ṣaṣṭitame rāmaś cakre gokulam āgataḥ |

ramamāṇas tu gopībhiḥ kālindyāḥ karṣaṇaṁ madāt ||

rām asya caritaṁ citraṁ kālindy-ākarṣaṇādi yat |

pauṇḍrakāntādi kṛṣṇasya pṛthag uktam ataḥ param ||1||

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : karṣaṇam ākarṣaṇam | citram adbhutam || balena bhad-ante lokān kalyāṇa-yutān karotīti balabhadraḥ |

[toṣaṇī] atha śrī-parīkṣitas tat-tal-līlānandam anubhavato balād anugacchatā śrī-gokula-vyākulatā-smaraṇena dūyamānaṁ mano jñātvā śrīman-munīndraḥ punas tad-āśvāsana-kathāṁ kathāntarāveśena tyaktām api pātayati—balabhadra iti | sambuddhyā ślāghate—he kuru-śreṣṭha ! iti | atīva-śuddha-kulodbhūtasya tava hṛdi śrī-vraja-līlaiva sphurati muhur iti bhāvaḥ | suhṛdaḥ śrī-vasudevādito’pi pitṛtvādimaya-śuddha-snehāṁśena śobhanaḥ, suhṛd yeṣāṁ tān nandādīn didṛkṣuḥ san yaś cira-viraheṇotsukaḥ nitya-tad-darśanotkaṇṭhaḥ tadā tu labdha-śrī-kṛṣṇānujña iti jñeyam | tad uktaṁ śrī-hari-vaṁśe—jagāmaiko vrajaṁ rāmaḥ kṛṣṇasyānumate sthitaḥ [ha.vaṁ 2.46.1] iti |

ata eva vakṣyati—kṛṣṇasya sandeśaiḥ sāntvayāmāsa [ha.vaṁ. 10.65.16] iti | śrī-kṛṣṇas tu śrī-vasudevādibhir anujñāta eva tena saha nāgacchat | ayaṁ śrī-nandādi-prema-baddhaḥ | punar atrānāgamiṣyatīty ananujñāta iti jñeyam | prakarṣeṇa gopa-veśādinā yayau | tad uktaṁ śrī-hari-vaṁśe—

sa praviṣṭas tu vegena taṁ vrajaṁ kṛṣṇa-pūrvajaḥ |

vanyena ramaṇīyena veṣeṇālaṅkṛtaḥ prabhuḥ || [ha.vaṁ. 2.46.3] iti |

ratha-sthitis tu śīghra-gamanārthaiva ||1||

sanātana-gosvāmī (bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : evaṁ śrī-kṛṣṇasya dīna-vātsalyam uktvā prasaṅgāc chrī-rāmasyāpi tad āha—bala- ity-ādinā yāvat-samāpti | suhṛdaḥ śrī-nanda-yaśodādīn didṛkṣuḥ san, yata utkaṇṭhaś cira-vicchedena nitya-darśanotsukaḥ, ataḥ śīghra-gamanārthaṁ tat-paricitaṁ ratham āsthita ārūḍhaṁ san, nandasya gopendrasya | kiṁ vā, nandayati jagad viśeṣataś ca sānujam ātmānaṁ harṣayatīti nandaṁ yad gokulaṁ tat prakarṣeṇa gopa-veśa-dhāraṇādinā yayau | tathā ca śrī-hari-vaṁśe—

sa praviṣṭas tu vegena taṁ vrajaṁ kṛṣṇa-pūrvajaḥ |

vanyena ramaṇīyena veṣeṇālaṅkṛtaḥ prabhuḥ || [ha.vaṁ. 2.46.3] iti |

yato bhagavān parama-dayāluḥ | kiṁ vā, sarvajño'nujābhiprāyaṁ jānann ity arthaḥ | tathā ca tatraiva jagāmaiko vrajaṁ rāmaḥ kṛṣṇasyānumate sthitaḥ [ha.vaṁ. 2.46.1] iti | ata evātrāpi vakṣyati kṛṣṇasya sandeśaḥ sāntvayāmāsa [bhā.pu. 10.65.16] iti | bala-bhadra iti buddhy-ādi-balādhikyena mahatām āśvāsanādau samarthatara iti bhāvaḥ | he kuru-śreṣṭha ! iti tad-arthaṁ tat-prayāṇena praharṣāt | yad vā, kuru-śreṣṭho bhavānivāsau śrī-kṛṣṇāgrajatayā yadu-śreṣṭho'nujasyāyāsya ity-ādi vākyāsatyatā-samādhānārthaṁ prayātum arhatyeveti bhāvaḥ ||1||

jīva-gosvāmī (laghu-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : atha śrī-parīkṣitas tat-tal-līlānandam anubhavato’pi balād anugacchatā śrī-gokula-vyākulatā-smaraṇena dūyamānaṁ mano vijñāya śrīman-muni-rājaḥ punas tad-āśvāsana-kathāṁ kathāntarāveśena tyaktām api pātayati—bala-bhadra ity-ādinā | sambodhanena taṁ lāte ca—he kuru-śreṣṭha ! iti | bala-bhadro bhagavān iti ca taṁ prati ślāghā-garbham | suhṛdaḥ śrī-vasudevādito’pi pitṛtvādimaya-śuddha-snehāṁśena śobhanaṁ hṛd yeṣāṁ tān śrī-nandādīn didṛkṣuḥ san prayayau | yaḥ pūrvata evotkaṇṭhaḥ cira-viraheṇa nitya-tad-darśanotsukaḥ tadā tu labdha-śrī-kṛṣṇānujña iti jñeyaṁ | tathā ca hari-vaṁśe jagāmaiko vraja rāmaḥ kṛṣṇasyānumate sthitaḥ [ha.vaṁ 2.46.1] iti | ata evātrāpi vakṣyati kṛṣṇasya sandeśaiḥ sāntvayāmāsa [bhā.pu. 10.65.16] iti etāvad-dinaṁ tad-anujñā ca sahaiva sva-gamanecchayeti lakṣyate | tac ca śrī-vasudevādīnāṁ śrī-nandādi-prema-baddho'sau tatraiva tiṣṭhed ity ananujñayaiveti pūrvavaj jñeyam | ata eva śrī-nandādiṣu nijāgamana-sandeśādi-gopanārtham ekākitvenaiva tat-prasthāpanam api evam antas tad-bhāvabhāvitatvena prayayāv iti gopa-veṣadhāraṇādi prakarṣeṇa yayāv ity arthaḥ | tathā ca tatraiva—

sa praviṣṭas tu vegena taṁ vrajaṁ kṛṣṇa-pūrvajaḥ |

vanyena ramaṇīyena veśenālaṅkṛtaḥ prabhuḥ || [ha.vaṁ. 2.46.3] iti |

iti ratha-sthitatvaṁ tu paramotkaṇṭhitatvāc chīghra-gamanārtham eva ||1||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : balabhadra ity atra kṛṣṇasyānumate sthita ity api śrī-hari-vaṁśāl labhyate | tathā hi—

kadācit tv atha kālasya smṛtvā gopeṣu sauhṛdam |

jagāmaiko vrajaṁ rāmaḥ kṛṣṇasyānumate sthitaḥ || [ha.vaṁ. 2.46.1] iti |

tena saha svāgamanasya sampraty ayogyatāyāṁ, tat-tad-vrajastha-sāntvanārthaṁ tam ekam eva śrī-kṛṣṇaḥ svayam anugatavān iti bhāvaḥ ||1-2||

jīva-gosvāmī (bṛhat-krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

pañcaṣaṣṭhitame rāmo goṣṭhaṁ gatvā sva-bandhubhiḥ |

militaḥ svīya-gopībhī reme kṛṣṇāṁ cakarṣa ca || ||

balabhadra iti | nanu, prema-mahodadhiḥ kṛṣṇaḥ kathaṁ vrajaṁ na yayau ? iti ced ucyate—

preyasīḥ prema-vikhyātāḥ pitarāv ativatsalau |

prema-vaśyaś ca kṛṣṇas tāṁs tyaktvā naḥ katham eṣyati |

iti matvaiva yadavaḥ pratyabadhnan harer gatau |

vraja-prema-pravardhi-sva-līlādhīnatvam īyuṣaḥ ||

nanu tarhi baladevo’pi sva-prāṇa-preṣṭha-bhrātaraṁ kṛṣṇaṁ tyaktvā ekākī eva gantuṁ nārhati | tatrāha—utkaṇṭhā atyutkaṇṭhā-culukita-dhairya-vivekādir ity arthaḥ ||1||

|| 10.65.2 ||

pariṣvaktaś cirotkaṇṭhair gopair gopībhir eva ca |

rāmo’bhivādya pitarāv āśīrbhir abhinanditaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : āśīrbhir abhinandito babhūva ||2||

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : pitarau yaśodā-nandau | pariṣvaktaḥ sarvāṅga-saṅgataṁ gāḍhaṁ bahu-vidham aśru-pulakādi-valitaṁ cāliṅgitaḥ, yataś cirotkaṇṭhaiḥ | pitarau tasya tayoś ca bhāvānusāreṇa tatra prathamam itas tato labdhair gopair melanam | vrajābhyantare tu gopībhir mātṛ-vayasyābhir dṛdhābhir api | eva ca ity anukta-samuccaye, purohitādibhir ity api jñeyam ||2||

sanātana-gosvāmī (bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : parito bahudhā gāḍhatayā sarvāṅga-sammilanādinā svata āliṅgataḥ, yataś cirotkaṇṭhaiḥ, eva api, gopībhir api, ca-kārād bhṛtya-purohitādibhiś ca, iti cirotkaṇṭhatayā parama-sambhramāt | ādau sāmānyena sarveṣāṁ pariṣvaṅgam uktvā, adhunā viśeṣato yathārha-vyavahāram āha—rāma iti sārdha-dvayena | pitarāv iti tadānīm api tayos tasminn api putra-buddhy-anuvṛtteḥ, tathā tasyāpi tau prati tādṛṅ-mater anapagamāt ||2||

jīva-gosvāmī (laghu-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : tad-bhāva-bhāvitatvam eva darśayati—pari ity-ādibhiḥ | pariṣvaktaḥ parīti sarvāṅga-saṅgataṁ gāḍhaṁ bahu-vidham aśru-pulakādi-valitaṁ cāliṅgitaḥ, yataś cirotkaṇṭhaiḥ pitarāv iti tasya tayoś ca bhāvānusāreṇoktam | tatra prathamam itas tato labdhair gopair milanam | vrajābhyantare tu gopībhir mātṛ-vayasyābhir dṛḍhābhir api | evaṁ cety anuktayāpi samuccayāt purohitādibhiś ceti jñeyam | tata uddiśya prathama-milanau pitarau śrī-yaśodā-nandāv abhivādyā sthito rāmas tābhyām api pariṣvaktaḥ sann āśīrbhir apy abhinandita ity arthaḥ ||2||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ, bṛhat-krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

nityānanda-svarūpo’pi prema-tapto vrajaukasām |

yayau kṛṣṇam api tyaktvā yas taṁ rāmaṁ muhuḥ stumaḥ || ||

gopībhiḥ mātṛ-vayasyābhiḥ | pitarāv iti tasya tayoś ca bhāvānusāreṇoktam abhinandito babhūva ||2||

|| 10.65.3 ||

ciraṁ naḥ pāhi dāśārha sānujo jagad-īśvaraḥ |

ity āropyāṅkam āliṅgya netraiḥ siṣicatur jalaiḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tad āha—ciram iti ||3||

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : tad āśīr abhinandanam [netrebhyaś cakṣur nāḍībhyo nirgatāni netrāṇi taiḥ kārakāt kriyā-yukte ity apādāna-kārakān nirgate'rthe aṇ, vaikalpika-ṇittvād-vṛddhā-bhāvaḥ] | netraṁ nāḍyāṁ taror mūle iti yādavaḥ |

[toṣaṇī] ciraṁ pāhi iti vāg-bhaṅgyā dīrghāyuṣṭvāpratihata-bala-vīryād āśiṣaḥ sūcitāḥ | jagad-īśvaraḥ sann ity anantaraṁ yadyapi sveṣām api dāśārhatvam asti, tathā pāṇḍava-kauravavat prācurya-prayoga-vivakṣayā yādaveṣv eva mukhyatvāt tac-chabda-pravṛttiḥ | vasudeva-putratvenaiva tathā sambodhitaḥ | yadyapi svīyo'pi putra-bhāvas tasminn adhikaḥ, tathāpi priya-bhrātṛ-putratām āśrityaiva svayaṁ putratvena pālitas saujanyottara-sambandham eva bahutaraṁ tenātmanaś caikyaṁ vyañjayitvā sva-putra eva viśiṣya darśitaḥ | ata eva sānuja iti sākṣāt-sva-putre tad-anujatvam uktam, tasyaiva prādhānya-vivakṣayā gauṇatvaṁ ca | ata eva na ity aviśeṣeṇāsmān yādavāṁś cety arthaḥ | athavotkaṇṭhayā śleṣeṇa samprati tayoḥ sveṣv audāsīnyaṁ darśitam, tad anucitam ity āha—na iti | yathā yādavān pāsi, tathāsmān pāhīty arthaḥ ||

[viśvanāthas tu]—sānujo'pi tvaṁ jagadīśvara iti sarvatra śūcyate, tad api vṛddhau sva-mātā-pitarāv āvāṁ kathaṁ na pālayasi ? iti bhāvaḥ ||3||

sanātana-gosvāmī (bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : ciraṁ pāhīti vāg-bhaṅgyā dīrghāyuṣṭvāpratihata-bala-vīryādy āśiṣaḥ sūcitā eva | na iti sarva-vraja-janāpekṣayā jagad-īśvaraḥ sann ity āśīr-antaram | sānuja iti tasyānāgamanena ātma-viṣayakaṁ nirdayatvaṁ matvā prārthanā, rakṣāyāṁ satyām api tat sambandha-rāhityenārakṣāyām eva paryavasānāt | dāśārha ! he yadu-kulodbhaveti sva-parityāgena yadūnām eva pālanād īrṣyayā paramārtyā vā sambodhanam | tajjair yāni jalāniḥ taiḥ | “netrair” iti vā pāṭhaḥ, netra-bhar ity arthaḥ | anyat tair vyākhyātam |

yad vā, āśīrbhiḥ—

dīrghāyur apratihata-bala-vīrya-samanvitaḥ |

putra-pautra-dhanaiśvaryair yukto vatsa bhavottamaḥ ||

ity-ādi-lakṣaṇābhiḥ, pitṛtvenādī sneha-bhareṇāśīrbhir abhinandya paścād dainyena prārthayate—ciram iti | dāśārha ! he yadu-kuleśvareti | tat-pālana-paro'pi jagadīśvaratvena jagad-antar-gatān asmān api pāhīty arthaḥ | anyat samānam ||3||

jīva-gosvāmī (laghu-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : ciraṁ pāhi iti vāg-bhaṅgyā dīrghāyuṣṭvāpratihata-bala-vīryādy-āśiṣaḥ sūcitāḥ | jagad-īśvaraḥ sann ity āśīḥ | anantaraṁ yadyapi sveṣām api dāśārhatvaṁ vartate, tathāpi pāṇḍava-kauravavat prācurya-prayogāvivakṣayā yādaveṣv eva mukhyatvāt tac-chabda-pravṛttir iti śrī-vasudeva-putratvena tathā sambodhitaṁ | yadyapi cokta-rītyā svīyo’pi putra-bhāvas tasmin adhikaḥ, tathāpi sva-priya-bhrātṛ-śrī-vasudeva-putratām avalambyaiva svayaṁ putratvenāsau pālita iti saujanyāt tat-sambandham eva bahutaraṁ matvā tena ātmanaś caikyaṁ vyañjayitvā sva-putra-bhāva eva viśiṣya darśitaḥ | ata eva sānuja ity anena sākṣāt sva-putre’pi tad-anujatvam uktam | tasyeya prādhānya-vivakṣayā gauṇatvaṁ ca, ata eva na ity aviśeṣeṇa yādavān asmāṁś cety arthaḥ | athavotkaṇṭhayā śleṣeṇa samprati tayoḥ sveṣaudāsīnyaṁ vyañjitaṁ tat tv anucitam ity āha—na iti | yathā yādavān pāsi, tadvad apy asmān pāhīty arthaḥ ||3||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : sānujas tvaṁ jagad-īśvaraḥ san ciraṁ sarvadaiva asmān pāhi ity āśiṣaṁ dade | tatra sānuja iti jyeṣṭhatva-mānena | tathā svasya tasmin putra-bhāvādhikye'pi dāśārha iti śrī-vasudevena saha svābheda-vivakṣayā sā cātisnehena atha tad-dinānantare dine ity arthaḥ ||3-4||

jīva-gosvāmī (bṛhat-krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : sānujo’pi tvaṁ jagadīśvara iti sarvatra śrūyase | tad api vṛddhau sva-mātā-pitarāv āvāṁ na pālayasi katham ity uktvā prathamaṁ nandas tato yaśodā ca balād aṅkam āropya sa-cumbanam āliṅgya netraiḥ netrajaiḥ ||3||

|| 10.65.4-6 ||

gopa-vṛddhāṁś ca vidhivad yaviṣṭhair abhivanditaḥ |

yathā-vayo yathā-sakhyaṁ yathā-sambandham ātmanaḥ ||

samupetyātha gopālān hāsya-hasta-grahādibhiḥ |

viśrāntaṁ sukham āsīnaṁ papracchuḥ paryupāgatāḥ ||

pṛṣṭāś cānāmayaṁ sveṣu prema-gadgadayā girā |

kṛṣṇe kamala-patrākṣe sannyastākhila-rādhasaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : gopa-vṛddhāṁś ca vidhivad abhivandya yaviṣṭhair abhivandito babhūvety arthaḥ | api ca yathā-vaya iti || te sarve sveṣu yādaveṣu taṁ rāmam anāmayam ārogyaṁ papracchuḥ | te ca tena pṛṣṭā iti | kṛṣṇe kṛṣṇa-prāpty-arthaṁ saṁnyastākhila-rādhasas tyakta-sarva-viṣayāḥ ||4-6||

vaṁśīdharaḥ (bhāvārtha-dīpikā-prakāśaḥ) : yaviṣṭhaḥ kaniṣṭhaḥ | ity artha iti śeṣaḥ | yathocitaṁ kartavyam āha—api ca iti | ātmanaḥ vaya-ādy anatikrameṇa gopālān samprāpyeti sārḍhenānvayaḥ | tatrādināśleṣa-grahaḥ ||4| atha gopāḥ papracchuḥ | "gopālāḥ" iti pāṭho'pi sādhīyān | te ca gopā | tena rāmeṇa ||5|| kṛṣṇa iti nirmitte saptamī | yad vā, kṛṣṇe saṁnyastāḥ samarpitā akhila-rādhasaḥ sarva-sampadaḥ | rādhas tejasi hiṁsāyāṁ viṣaye siddhi-sampado iti dharaṇiḥ ||6||

sanātana-gosvāmī (bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī) : atha anantaraṁ gopālānāṁ vaya-ādy-anatikrameṇa hāsyādibhiḥ samyag upetya militvā viśrāntaṁ vigata-vartma-śramaṁ santaṁ paścāt sukham āsīnam | kiṁ vā, hāsyādibhyo viśrāntam uparatam | yad vā, cira-pravāsa-śrama-rahitam, ata eva sūkham āsīnam | ādi-śabdāt priyoktyādayaḥ | paritaḥ sarvata upa samīpe āgatāḥ | ātmano yathā-sambandham iti śrī-nanda-yaśodayor bhrātrādi-sambodhanenaivātmanaḥ pitṛvya-mātuleyatvādi-sambandhānusāreṇety arthaḥ | iti pūrvavad eva sarvo vyavahāro dyotitaḥ | tathā ca tatraiva śrī-hari-vaṁśe—

sa tān abhāṣata prītyā yathā-pūrvam arindama |

gopīs tenaiva vidhinā yathā-nyāyaṁ yathā-vayaḥ ||

tathaiva prāha tān sarvān tathaiva pariharṣayan |

tathaiva saha gopībhir yojayan madhurāḥ kathāḥ || [ha.vaṁ. 2.46.4-5] iti |

yad vā, nanu, kalyāṇyo yuṣmābhir batai-tādṛśārttipradaṁ tasmin sauhārdaṁ kim iti kṛtaṁ tatra tādṛśokti-pratihatatvād ity āha—kathaṁ nv iti ||11-12||
