SB 3.7

(3.7)

atha saptamo’dhyāyaḥ

nirguṇasya bhagavato guṇa-kriyādi-saṅgaḥ katham iti praśnasya maitreya-dvārā samādhānaṁ śrutvā vidurasya punar vividhāḥ praśnāḥ |

|| 3.7.1 ||

śrī-śuka uvāca—

evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ maitreyaṁ dvaipāyana-suto budhaḥ |

prīṇayann iva bhāratyā viduraḥ pratyabhāṣata ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

saptame saṁśaya-cchedi pratinandya muner vacaḥ |

punaḥ kṣattrā kṛtā nānā praśnāḥ samyag udīritāḥ ||

atha te bhagaval-līlā yoga-māyopabṛṁhitāḥ[bhā.pu. 3.5.22] ity-ādinā māyā-guṇair līlayā bhagavān sṛṣṭy-ādi karotīty evaṁ bruvāṇaṁ maitreyaṁ bhāratyā prārthanā-rūpayā prīṇayann ivety abhiprāyājñānenākṣepāt ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : iveti vākyālaṅkāre ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

saptame bhagavaj-jīva-gatānyat saṁśaya-cchidā |

bhakti-jijñāsayānyo’pi kṣattuḥ praśnāḥ prakīrtitāḥ ||

evaṁ—

sā vā etasya sandraṣṭuḥ śaktiḥ sad-asad-ātmikā |

māyā-nāma mahā-bhāga yayedaṁ nirmame vibhuḥ |

kāla-vṛttyā tu māyāyāṁ guṇa-mayyām adhokṣajaḥ |

puruṣeṇātma-bhūtena vīryam ādhatta vīryavān || [bhā.pu. 3.5.25-26]

ity-ādi bruvāṇaṁ bhāratyā brahmaṁs tvayā saṁsāra-kūpād uddhṛta evāham iti madhurayā vācā prīṇayan iveti vidura-mano-gatākṣepasya sahasaiva maitreyeṇāvagatatvān maitreyaṁ prīṇayitum aśaknuvann ity arthaḥ ||1||

...

|| 3.7.2 ||

vidura uvāca—

brahman kathaṁ bhagavataś cin-mātrasyāvikāriṇaḥ |

līlayā cāpi yujyeran nirguṇasya guṇāḥ kriyāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : nirvikārasya kriyā, nirguṇasya ca guṇāḥ katham ? līlayety uktiḥ prayojanābhāvaṁ pariharati, na vastu-virodham iti bhāvaḥ ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : atha svābhāvika-māyā-śaktyā parameśvaro viśva-sṛṣṭy-ādikaṁ karoti, jīvaś ca tatra muhyatīty uktam | tatra sandehaṁ praśnottarābhyāṁ pariharati—brahmann ity-ādinā | he brahman ! cin-mātrasya cinmātra-svarūpasya svataḥ svarūpa-śaktyā bhagavataḥ śrī-vaikuṇṭhādi-gata-tādṛśaiśvaryādi-yuktasya, ata eva nirguṇasya prākṛta-guṇāspṛṣṭasya tata eva avikāriṇas tādṛk-svarūpa-śakti-vilāsa-bhūtānāṁ kriyāṇām anantānām api sadodita-varānanta-vidha-prakāśe tasmin nitya-siddhatvāt tat-tat-kriyāvirbhāva-kartus tasyāvasthāntara-prāptatvābhāvāt prākṛta-kartur iva na vikārāpattir iti |

nirvikārasya ca kathaṁ sattvādayaḥ prākṛta-guṇāḥ ?kathaṁ vā tad-āsaṅga-hetukāḥ sthity-ādayaḥ kriyāś ca yujyeran ? tataś ca cin-mātra-vastu-virodhād eva te ca tāś ca na yujyante | bhagavattve tu svaira-ceṣṭayāpi na yujyeran ity āha—līlayā vāpi iti | atrāvikāritva-nirguṇatvābhyāṁ saha cin-mātra-bhagavattvaṁ cety ubhayam api svīkṛtyaiva pūrva-pakṣiṇā pṛṣṭam |

tataś ca tasya cinmātra-svarūpasya bhavatu bhagavattvaṁ, tan-mātre’smākaṁ na sandehaḥ, kintu tasya katham itara-guṇādi-svīkāro yujyate ? ity eva pṛcchyate iti vākyārthaḥ ||2|| [paramātma-sandarbha 85]

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : cin-mātrasya guṇāḥ sattvādayaḥ kriyāḥ sṛṣṭy-ādayaś ca kathaṁ ? atra cid-rūpasya cinmayasyety-ādikam aprayujya cin-mātrasyety avadhāraṇa-vāci-mātra-śabda-prayogād acid-gandhasyāpi tatra rāhityaṁ bodhitaṁ | tataś ca sā vā etasyety anena sattvādi-guṇa-mayyā māyāyā bhagavac-chaktitvenoktatvāc chakteś ca sva-bhinnatvāsambhavāt | vastuto guṇās tadīyā eveti, tasya cinmātratve kathaṁ sattvādi-jaḍa-guṇavattvaṁ ? sattvādi-jaḍa-guṇavattve ca kathaṁ cin-mātratvam ? ity ākṣepa ekaḥ | tathā vikāro hi kāla-hetuko guṇa-dharma eveti tasya tu cinmātratvād evāvikāritve kathaṁ vikāra-vyañjaka-kriyāvattvaṁ ?kriyāvattve ca katham avikāritvam? iti dvitīyaḥ |

nanv evam asambhavanto’pi guṇāḥ kriyāś ca tasya svaira-līlatvāt sambhaveyuḥ ? tatrāha—līlayā veti nahi līlayety uktyā virodhaḥ parihartuṁ śakyate, tasya tādṛśa-līlatve guṇavattvasya duṣpariharatvād iti bhāvaḥ | atra bhagavata iti viśeṣyaṁ bhagavān eva cin-mātrā, na tu bhaga iti vācyaṁ, yato bhagasyāpi bhagavattvam aprākṛtatvaṁ viṣṇu-purāṇe dṛṣṭaṁ, yathā—

jñāna-śakti-balaiśvarya-vīrya-tejāṁsy aśeṣataḥ |

bhagavac-chabda-vācyāni vinā heyair guṇādibhiḥ || [vi.pu. 6.5.79] iti |

ata eva deha-dehi-vibhāgo’yaṁ neśvare vidyate kvacid ity uktam, ata eva bhaga-śabda-vācyānāṁ ṣaḍ-aiśvaryāṇām api cin-mātratvam eva, na tu sattvādi-guṇa-pariṇāmatvam iti bhagavattvaṁ svarūpa-lakṣaṇam eva | bhagavattvasya māyikatve taṭastha-lakṣaṇatvam iti vyācakṣāṇā bhrāntā eva | indrasya māyeyam iti indro māyayā vṛṣabho bhavatīty ukte indrasya vṛṣa-rūpatvam eva sva-māyā-kṛtaṁ pratīyate, na tv indratvaṁ yathā tathaiva seyaṁ bhagavato māyeti bhagavān eva māyayā viśvaṁ bhavatīty uktyā bhagavato viśva-rūpatvam eva māyikaṁ syān, na tu bhagavattvam iti yukteś ca ||2||

|| 3.7.3 ||

krīḍāyāṁ udyamo’rbhasya kāmaś cikrīḍiṣānyataḥ |

svatas-tṛptasya ca kathaṁ nivṛttasya sadānyataḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : arbhakaval līlāpi na yujyate vaiṣamyād ity āha—udyamayati pravartayatīty udyamaḥ | arbhakasya krīḍāyāṁ pravṛtti-hetuḥ kāmo’sti | anyatas tu vastv-antareṇa bālāntara-pravartanena vā tasya krīḍecchā bhavati | īśvarasya tu svatas tṛptasya kathaṁ kāmaḥ ? anyataḥ sadā nivṛttasya cāsaṅgād advitīyasya katham anyataś cikrīḍiṣā ? ity arthaḥ ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (paramātma-sandarbha 86) : tataś cinmātratve bhagavattve ca tasya tucchā guṇāḥ kriyāś ca na sambhavanty eveti dviguṇībhūyaiva praśnaḥ | kiṁ cārbhaka-līlāpi na yujyate, vaiṣamyāt ity āha—krīḍāyām iti |

udyamayati pravartayati ity udyamaḥ | arbhakasya krīḍāyāṁ pravṛtti-hetuḥ kāmo’sti | anyatas tu vastv-antareṇa bālāntara-pravartanena vā tasya krīḍecchā bhavati | bhagavatas tu svataḥ svenātmanā svarūpa-vaibhavena ca tṛptasya ata eva anyataḥsadā nivṛttasya ca katham anyato jīvāj jagataś ca nimittāt cikrīḍiṣā iti | na ca tasya te guṇās tāḥ kriyāś ca na vidyante ity apalapanīyam ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tataś ca cin-mātratve bhagavattve ca tasya tucchā guṇāḥ kriyāś ca na sambhavanty eveti dviguṇī-bhūyaiva praśnaḥ | līlayā vāpi kathaṁ yujyeran ? iti viśadayati—krīḍāyām iti |

avikāritvādi-tat-tad-utkarṣa-hīnasya arbhakasyacikrīḍiṣā-rūpaḥ kāmaḥ, tābhyām anyasya krīḍāyāṁ udyame hetur bhavati | bhagavatas tu svenātmanā svarūpa-vaibhavena ca tṛptasya, ata eva anyataḥ sadānivṛttasya ca katham asāv anyato jīvāj jagataś ca nimittāt cikrīḍiṣā sambhavet ? ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : kiṁ ca, līlayety uktyā virodha-parihāro māstu, līlaiva tāvad īśvare hetv-abhāvān na ghaṭata iti sa-dṛṣṭāntam āha—krīḍāyām iti | udyamayati pravartayatīty udyamaḥ kāma eva arbhakasyasvābhāvikaḥ krīḍāyāṁ pravṛtti-hetur asti | tathā anyato bālāntara-pravartanam api cikrīḍiṣāsti | parameśvarasya ātmārāmatvāt kathaṁ kāmaḥ, īśvarāntarābhāvāt ? katham anyataś cikrīḍiṣā ? ity āha—svatas tṛptasyety-ādi | anyato nivṛttasya anya-rahitasya ||3||

|| 3.7.4 ||

asrākṣīd bhagavān viśvaṁ guṇa-mayy ātma-māyayā |

tayā saṁsthāpayaty etad bhūyaḥ pratyapidhāsyati ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yac coktaṁ smaran viśva-sṛjām īśo vijñāpitam adhokṣaje[bhā.pu. 3.6.1] ity-ādināvidyopādher jīvasya bhogārtham īśvaraḥ sṛṣṭy-ādi karotīti | tad apy ākṣeptum anuvadati—

asrākṣīd iti | guṇa-mayī ātmano jīvasya kartṛtva-bhoktṛtvādi-mohotpādikā yā māyā, tayā sṛṣṭavān | tad uktaṁ prathame—

yayā saṁmohito jīva ātmānaṁ tri-guṇātmakam |

paro’pi manute’narthaṁ tat-kṛtaṁ cābhipadyate || [bhā.pu. 1.7.5] ity-ādinā |

atra ca, ato bhagavato māyā māyinām api mohinī[bhā.pu. 3.6.39] iti | saṁsthāpayati pālayati | pratyapidhāsyati prātilomyena tirohitaṁ kariṣyati | pāṭhāntare prātilomyenātmany abhito dhārayiṣyati ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na ca tasya te guṇās tāḥ kriyāś ca na vidyanta ity apalapanīyaṁ, tatraiva prasiddher ity āha—asrākṣīd iti | guṇa-mayyā traiguṇya-vyañjinyā ātmāśritayā māyayāsaṁsthāpayati pālayati pratyapidhāsyati prātilomyena tirohitaṁ kariṣyati ||4|| [paramātma-sandarbha 87]

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : na ca tasya te guṇāḥ kriyāś ca na santīty apalapanīyaṁ tathaiva prasiddher ity āha—asrākṣīd iti | pratyapidhāsyati prātilomyena tirohitaṁ kariṣyati ||4||

|| 3.7.5 ||

deśataḥ kālato yo’sāv avasthātaḥ svato’nyataḥ |

aviluptāvabodhātmā sa yujyetājayā katham ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : durbhaga-kleśa-śarīra-sthatvāt tasyāpi bhāvyam | na ca tad yujyate | seyaṁ bhagavato māyā, ayaṁ hi bhagavan-mahimā | tasya kārpaṇyaṁ bandhanādi na yujyata iti yad uktaṁ tan nyāyyam eva | durbhagādi śarīra-sthasyāpi tad-doṣāsparśa eva tan-mahimety arthaḥ |

kathaṁ deha-paro devo lipyeta sa hi bandhanaiḥ |

kathaṁ na duḥkhī sa bhaved duḥkhī ced īśvaraḥ kutaḥ ||

mahimā paramasyaiṣa yad dehastho na bādhyate |

yad duḥkhī na sa īśāno māyeti mahimocyate ||

pradhānaṁ maya ity āhuḥ prādhānyān mayatā bhavet || iti bhāllaveya-śrutiḥ |

avilupta-bodha-rūpatvān nāsau prakṛti-dehavān |

na sṛṣṭy-ādikaṁ bhrāntir bhrānti-vādā hi dānavāḥ ||

ato bhrānty-ādi-sambandho nāsya kvacana yujyate |

bhrāntyā jīvasya saṁsāra īśa-jñānād vilīyate |

bhrāntir dehādy-abhimatir īśa-jñānād vinaśyati || iti brahmāṇḍe ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : etac ca jīvasyāvidyāśrayatve ghaṭeta, na tu tat saṁbhavatīty āha—deśata iti | yo’sau deśādibhir aviluptāvabodha ātmā jīvaḥ, brahma-svarūpatvāt | sa katham ajayā’vidyayā yujyeta ? tatra deśato dīpa-prabhāyā iva lopo nāsti, sarva-gatatvāt | na kālataḥ, vidyuta iva nityatvāt | nāvasthātaḥ, smṛtivad avikriyatvāt | na svataḥ, svapnavat satyatvāt | nānyataḥ, ghaṭādivad advitīyatvāt | evam etair yasya bodho na lupyate, sa katham ajayā yujyeta ? ajā cātrāvidyaiva, na māyā, tasyā avabodhena virodhābhāvāt ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : jīvasya kathaṁ māyā-mohitatvaṁ ghaṭeta ? ity ākṣepāntaram āha—deśata iti | yo’sau deśādibhir aviluptāvabodha ātmā jīvaḥ sa kathamajayā avidyayā yujyeta? tatra deśa-vyavadhānato deśa-gata-doṣato vā cakṣuḥ prakāśa iva,kālato vidyud iva,avasthātaḥ smṛtir iva, svataḥ śukti-rajatam iva, anyato ghaṭādi-vastv iva, na tasyāvabodho lupyate avyāhata-svarūpa-bhūta-jñānāśrayatvād evety arthaḥ ||5|| [paramātma-sandarbha 87]

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : jīvasya ca kathaṁ māyā-mohitatvaṁ ghaṭetety ākṣepāntaram āha—deśata iti | yo’sau nitya-jñānatvāt deśādibhir aviluptāvabodha ātmā jīvaḥ, sa katham ajayā avidyayā yujyeta ajā-yogotthas tasyāvabodha-lopaḥ kathaṁ syāt? ity arthaḥ | tatra deśata uṣara-deśata uptaṁ bījam iva, kālato vidyud iva, avasthātaḥ smṛtir iva, svataḥ svapna iva, anyato ghaṭādir iva yasya cid-vastutvād avabodho na lupto bhavitum arhati, sa katham avidyayā vilupta-vibodhaḥ kriyate? ity arthaḥ ||5||

|| 3.7.6 ||

bhagavān eka evaiṣa sarva-kṣetreṣv avasthitaḥ |

amuṣya durbhagatvaṁ vā kleśo vā karmabhiḥ kutaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : kiṁ ca, brahma-rūpatvād eva jīvasya saṁsāro’pi na vidyata ity ākṣipati | sarva-kṣetreṣv avasthito bhoktāpi vastuto bhagavān eva, cid-rūpatvena tad-vyatirekābhāvāt | evaṁ ca saty amuṣya jīvasya durbhagatvam ānandādi-bhraṁśaḥ karmabhir hetu-bhūtaiḥ kleśo vā kutaḥ ? karma-saṁbandhābhāvāt | anyatheśvarasyāpi tat-prasaṅgaḥ syād iti bhāvaḥ ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tatraiva virodhāntaram āha—bhagavān eka eveti | eṣa eka eva bhagavān paramātmāpi sarva-kṣetreṣu sarvasya jīvasya kṣetreṣu deheṣv avasthitaḥ | tatra sati kathamamuṣya jīvasya durbhagatvaṁ svarūpa-bhūta-jñānādi-lopaḥ karmabhiḥ kleśaś ca tasya vā kuto nāsti ? na hy ekasmin jalādau sthitayor vastunoḥ kasyacit tat-saṁsargaḥ, kasyacin neti yujyata ity arthaḥ ||6|| [paramātma-sandarbha 89]

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : na cāyam anyāyo’rājaka dvairājyayor iva sambhāvanīyo yad ajā niraparādhaṁ jīvaṁ parābhaved ity āha—sarveṣām api jīvānāṁ kṣetreṣu deheṣu bhagavān eva avasthitaḥ antaryāmi-rūpeṇa svayam eva sthitaḥ, na tu rājeva sva-rājyeṣu sva-pratinidhi-puruṣa-dvārety arthaḥ | eka eveti dvairājya-śaṅkāpi parihṛtā | ata eva tasmin bhagavati sarva-pratipālake atraiva tiṣṭhaty api amuṣya jīvasya durbhagatvam ānandādi-bhraṁśo vā karmabhir āvidyakaiḥ kleśo vā kutaḥ ||6||

|| 3.7.8 ||

śrī-śuka uvāca—

sa itthaṁ coditaḥ kṣattrā tattva-jijñāsunā muniḥ |

pratyāha bhagavac-cittaḥ smayann iva gata-smayaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : codita ākṣiptaḥ smayann iva prauḍhim āviṣkurvann iva | vastutas tu gata-smayaḥ ||8||

|| 3.7.9 ||

maitreya uvāca—

seyaṁ bhagavato māyā yan nayena virudhyate |

īśvarasya vimuktasya kārpaṇyaṁ uta bandhanam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : bhagavato’cintya-śakter īśvarasya seyaṁ māyā nayena tarkeṇa virudhyata iti yat | tarkaṁ virodham evānuvadati—vimuktasya eva puṁso’vidyā-bandhanaṁ kārpaṇyaṁ ceti ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tatra kevalaṁ cin-mātratvaṁ jīve’pi na sambhavatīti bhagavattvam evāṅgīkṛtya śrī-maitreya uvāca—seyam iti | yayā viśva-sṛṣṭy-ādikaṁ bhavati, seyaṁbhagavato’cintya-svarūpa-śakter māyākhyā śaktiḥ, yat yā nayena tarkeṇa virudhyate tarkātītatayā seyam apy acintyety arthaḥ | yadyapy evaṁ dvayor apy acintyatvaṁ, tathāpi bhagavato māyety anena vyaktatvāt svarūpa-śakter antaraṅgatvād bahiraṅgāyā māyāyā guṇaiḥ sattvādibhis tat-kāryaiḥ sthāpanādibhiś ca nāsau spṛśyata ity arthaḥ |

tantreṇa cāyam arthaḥ—yat yathā māyayā yena bhagavatā saha na viruddhyate, nāsau virodha-viṣayī-kriyata iti | yat yā yena bhagavatā na virudhyate, na sarvathā nirviṣayīkriyata iti ca |

sama-viṣama-matīnām [bhā.pu. 6.9.37] iti tu gadyaṁ tathāpy uccāvaca-buddhīnāṁ tathā sphurasīti pratipatty-arthaṁ jñeyam |
