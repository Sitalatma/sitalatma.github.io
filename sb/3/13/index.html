(3.13)

atha trayodaśo’dhyāyaḥ

brahmaṇo nāsā-randhrād āvirbhūtasya bhagavato yajña-varāhasya saṁkṣipta-caritam, ṛṣibhiḥ kṛtaṁ bhagavataḥ stavanaṁ ca |

|| 3.13.1 ||

śrī-śuka uvāca—

niśamya vācaṁ vadato muneḥ puṇyatamāṁ nṛpa |

bhūyaḥ papraccha kauravyo vāsudeva-kathādṛtaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

trayodaśe sisṛkṣāyāṁ manor ākasmikāplutām |

dharām uddhartum udbhūtāt kroḍād daityānusūdanam ||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : ādṛtir asyāstīty ādṛtaḥ sādara ity arthaḥ | arśa āditvādac ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

trayodaśe manor vācā dhyāyato brahmaṇo nasaḥ |

udbhūya gāṁ gataḥ kroḍo gām uddadhre dvijaiḥ stutaḥ ||

ādṛtir asyāstīty arśa ādyac ādṛtaḥ sādara ity arthaḥ | yad vā, kathayaiva kartryā mām asau viduraḥ śṛṇotu, man-mādhuryam etat kartṛkāsvādāt sārthakaṁ bhavatv ity ādṛtaḥ | yad vā, kathāyām ādṛtaḥ vaktṛbhiḥ śrotṛbhiś cety arthaḥ ||1||

...

pratilabhya priyāṁ patnīṁ kiṁ cakāra tato mune ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : svayambhuvaḥ putras tad-dehāṁśatvāt ||2||

|| 3.13.4 ||

nanv añjasā sūribhir īḍito’rthaḥ |

tat-tad-guṇānuśravaṇaṁ mukunda-

pādāravindaṁ hṛdayeṣu yeṣām ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : atas tasya caritaṁ śrotavyam ity āha—suciraṁ śramo yasmiṁs, tasya puṁsāṁ śrutasyāñjasā mukhyatvenāyam evārthaḥ | īḍitaḥ stutaḥ | nanu mukunda-pādāravindaṁ yeṣāṁ hṛdayeṣv asti, teṣāṁ bhāgavatānāṁ guṇānuśravaṇam iti yat ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

sanātana gosvāmī (dig-darṣinī) : [bṛ.bhā. 2.7.14] teṣāṁ māhātmya-śravaṇam eva parama-phalam iti śrī-vidureṇoktaṁ tṛtīya-skandhe—śrutasya iti | asyārthaḥ—suciraṁ śramo yasmin tasya puṁsāṁ śrutasya śāstrasya tad-abhyāsasya tad-utpanna-jñānasya vā ayam evārthaḥ | nanu niścitam īḍitaḥ stutaḥ ko'sau ? mukunda-pādāravindaṁ yeṣāṁ hṛdayeṣv asti, teṣāṁ teṣāṁ bhāgavatānām, yad vā, yeṣāṁ hṛdayeṣv asti, teṣāṁ tasya mukunda-pādāravinda-hṛdaya-rūpasyānirvacanīyasya vā guṇasya māhātmyasya svabhāvasya vā anuśravaṇaṁ vāraṁ vāraṁ śravaṇam iti ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : -kathā-śravaṇaṁ vinā viduṣām api vidyā viphalā bhavatīty āha—suciraṁ śramo yasmin, tasya guru-mukhāt śrutasya śāstrādy-adhyayanasyety arthaḥ | ayam evārthaḥ prayojanam | īḍitaḥ stutaḥ tad-anyas tu nindita ity arthaḥ | sa ca kaḥ ? yeṣāṁ yeṣāṁ hṛdayeṣu kṛṣṇa-pāda-padmaṁ vartate, teṣāṁ teṣāṁ guṇānuśravaṇa-kīrtanādīty arthaḥ ||4||

|| 3.13.5 ||

sahasra-śīrṣṇaś caraṇopadhānam |

praṇīyamāno munir abhyacaṣṭa ||

————————————————————————————————————————
