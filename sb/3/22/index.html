SB 3.22

(3.22)

atha dvāviṁśo’dhyāyaḥ

manu-kardama-saṁvādaḥ, kardama-devahūti-vivāhaḥ, manor brahmavartāya prasthānaṁ ca |

|| 3.22.1 ||

maitreya uvāca

evam āviṣkṛtāśeṣa- guṇa-karmodayo munim |

savrīḍa iva taṁ samrāḍ upāratam uvāca ha ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

dvā-viṁśe kardamāyādād yathādiṣṭaṁ hi viṣṇunā |

manur duhitaraṁ deva- hūtim ity upavarṇyate ||

evam āviṣkṛto’bhiṣṭuto’śeṣāṇāṁ guṇānāṁ karmaṇāṁ codaya utkarṣo yasya sa samrāṇ manuḥ | sa-vrīḍa iva sva-kīrti-śravaṇāt, pratyākhyāna-śaṅkayā vā taṁ munim uvāca | upārataṁ nivṛtti-niratam ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) :

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

dvāviṁśe priya-bhāṣī svāṁ jñāta-tattvāya kanyakām |

kardamāya nṛpo dattvā svaṁ dhāmāgād itīryate ||

āviṣkṛto’śeṣāṇāṁ guṇānāṁ karmaṇāṁ ca udayo utkarṣo yasya saḥ | vacanād uparataṁ nivṛtti-nirataṁ vā munim | sa-vrīḍa iveti sva-kīrti-śravaṇāt paramārtha-parāyaṇe tasmin vyavahāra-vārtāyā vaktavyatvāc ca ||1||

...

|| 3.22.2-3 ||

manur uvāca—

brahmāsṛjat sva-mukhato yuṣmān ātma-parīpsayā |

chandomayas tapo-vidyā- yoga-yuktān alampaṭān ||

tat-trāṇāyāsṛjac cāsmān doḥ-sahasrāt sahasra-pāt |

hṛdayaṁ tasya hi brahma kṣatram aṅgaṁ pracakṣate ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : madīyā kanyā tvayā pariṇeyeti vijñāpayiṣyan yuṣmad asmat-saṁbandhas tāvad īśvareṇa pūrvam eva ghaṭita ity āha—brahmeti sārdhābhyām | ātmanaḥ parīpsayā paryāptum icchayā | chando-mayasyātmanaḥ paryāptiḥ pālanaṁ veda-pravartanaṁ tasyecchayā | yuṣmān brāhmaṇān | tat-trāṇāya brāhmaṇa-pālanāya | brahma brāhmaṇa-jātiḥ | kṣatraṁ kṣatriya-jātiḥ ||2-3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : brahmā virāḍ-rūpaḥ sva-mukhata iti jāti-mātrāpekṣayoktam | evaṁ doḥ-sahasrād iti ca hṛdayaṁ jñāna-śakti-prādhānyāt, aṅgaṁ kriya-śakti-prādhānyāt ||2-3||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : sahasā sva-kanyā-pradāna-prasaṅgasya tat-pratyākhyāna-śaṅkayā prathamaṁ brāhmaṇa-kṣatriyayoḥ kam api sambandhaṁ sa-nyāyam āha—brahmeti sārdhābhyām | ātmanaś chando-mayasya svasya parīpsayā veda-pravartanena pālanecchayā yuṣmān brāhmaṇān asṛjat | paryāptiḥ pālanam ||2||

brāhmaṇān ke pālayeyur ity ata āha—tad iti | doḥ-sahasrād iti parameśvareṇaikyāt | nanu kṣatriyān api kaḥ pālayet ? tatrāha—brahma brāhmaṇaḥ | aṅgaṁ bhujaḥ | ayam arthaḥ—loke hi hṛdaye prahāra āpatite bhujābhyāṁ hṛdayasya gopanaṁ dṛśyate | bhuje ca prahāra āpatite dehaṁ kuñcitīkṛtya hṛdaya-madhye eva bhujo gopyate iti hṛdayasya pālako bhujaḥ, bhujasya pālakaṁ hṛdayam ||3||

|| 3.22.4 ||

rakṣati smāvyayo devaḥ sa yaḥ sad-asad-ātmakaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : evaṁ sa deva eva rakṣati sma | ko’sau ? yaḥ sad-asad-ātmakaḥ sarvātmakaḥ | tathāpy avyayo nirvikāraḥ ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

|| 3.22.19 ||

ato bhajiṣye samayena sādhvīṁ
