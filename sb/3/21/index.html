(3.21)

athaikaviṁśo’dhyāyaḥ

manu-vaṁśa-varṇanaṁ prastutya kardama-tapasyā, bhagavad-vara-pradānam, kardamasyāśrame svāyambhuva-manor āgamanaṁ ca |

|| 3.21.1 ||

vidura uvāca——

svāyambhuvasya ca manor aṁśaḥ parama-sammataḥ |

kathyatāṁ bhagavan yatra maithunenaidhire prajāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

ekaviṁśe tapo-vidyā-toṣitena tu viṣṇunā |

kardamasya manoḥ putryā vivāha-ghaṭanocyate ||

edhire edhāñcakrire ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

ekaviṁśe tapas-tuṣṭaḥ kardamena stuto hariḥ |

vivāha-ghaṭanām āha manus tatrājagāma ca ||

edhire edhāñcakrire ||1||

...

|| 3.21.2 ||

priyavratottānapādau sutau svāyambhuvasya vai |

yathā dharmaṁ jugupatuḥ sapta-dvīpavatīṁ mahīm ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : dharmaṁ mahīṁ ca yathā jugupatuḥ rarakṣatuḥ, tan me vadeti tṛtīyena anvayaḥ ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : dharmān anatikrameṇa yathā jugupatus tan me vadeti tṛtīyena anvayaḥ ||2||

...

|| 3.21.3 ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tasya manoḥ ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : devahūtir iti kvacit pāṭhaḥ ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : devahūtīti devahūtir ity arthaḥ ||3||

|| 3.21.4 ||

sasarja katidhā vīryaṁ tan me śuśrūṣave vada ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yoga-lakṣaṇair yamādibhir yuktāyām | katidhā vīryaṁ sasarja ? kati putrān utpādayāmāsety arthaḥ ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : yoga-lakṣaṇair yama-niyamādibhiḥ | katidhā vīryaṁ sasarja ? katy apatyān utpādayāmāsety arthaḥ ||4||

|| 3.21.5 ||

yathā sasarja bhūtāni labdhvā bhāryāṁ ca mānavīm ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : mānavīṁ manoḥ kanyām ākūtiṁ ca prasūtiṁ ca bhāryāṁ labdhvā yathā bhūtāni sasarja, tac ca vadeti ca-kārasyārthaḥ ||5||

|| 3.21.6 ||

maitreya uvāca—

prajāḥ sṛjeti bhagavān kardamo brahmaṇoditaḥ |

sarasvatyāṁ tapas tepe sahasrāṇāṁ samā daśa ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sahasrāṇāṁ samā daśa daśa-sahasrāṇi saṁvatsarān ity arthaḥ ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (bhakti-sandarbha 225): tad evam īdṛśena karmaṇā miśrā sa-kāmā bhaktir yathā—prajā iti | atra tad-darśana-jāta-bhagavad-aśru-pāta-liṅgena niṣkāmasyāpy asya brahmādeśa-gauraveṇaiva kāmanā jñeyā ||6-7||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : prajā iti | daśānāṁ samānāṁ sahasrāṇi iti yadi syāt, tadā sambandhasyobhayāśrayatve sahasrāṇām ity-ādy api syād iti bhāvaḥ | sahasrāṇāṁ tu bahutvam ārṣam ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : samā varṣāṇi daśa-sahasrāṇi iti arthaḥ ||6||

|| 3.21.7 ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tatas tasmiṁs tapasi | kriyā-yogena pūjā-prakāreṇa | saṁprapede siṣeve | prapannebhyo bhaktebhyo vara-dātāram ||7||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tatas tapaś-caraṇānantaraṁ tāvatāpi tapasā darśanam aprāpyety arthaḥ | samādhiś cittaikāgryaṁ, kriyā-yogena pūjā-prakāreṇa | prapannebhyo bhaktebhyo varāṇāṁ dāśuṣaṁ dātāram ||7||

|| 3.21.8 ||

tāvat prasanno bhagavān puṣkarākṣaḥ kṛte yuge |

darśayāmāsa taṁ kṣattaḥ śābdaṁ brahma dadhad vapuḥ ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : śabda-viṣayaṁ brahma ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : śabdaika-vedyaṁ yad brahma tan-mayaṁ vapur dadhat, taṁ praty ātmānaṁ darśayāmāsa ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tāvad iti | yāvatā kālena samyak prapede, tāvatety arthaḥ | śābdaṁ śabda-brahma-pratipādyaṁ yat paraṁ brahma tat-svarūpaṁ dadhat prakāśayat ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tāvad iti | yāvat samprapede iti pūjāyāṁ satyāṁ tat-prasāde vilambābhāvaḥ sūcitaḥ | śābdaṁ śabdaika-vedyaṁ yad brahma, tan-mayaṁ vapur dadhad iti śrī-svāmi-caraṇāḥ | sac-cid-ānanda-mayam ākāraṁ dadhat prakaṭayan kardama-datta-gandha-mālya-naivedyādy-upacāraiḥ pūṣann iti vā ||8||

|| 3.21.9 ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sa kardamas taṁ sve’vasthitaṁ dṛṣṭvā mūrdhnā kṣitāv apatat gīrbhiś cābhyagṛṇād (12) iti caturṇām anvayaḥ | padmotpale dina-rātri-vikāse | sitānāṁ padmānām utpalānāṁ ca srak yasya tam | snigdhā nīlāś ca ye’lakās teṣāṁ vrāto vaktrābje yasya ||9||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : sa tam iti catuṣkam | snigdhaś ca nīlaś ca alaka-vrāto yatra tādṛśaṁ vaktrābjaṁ yasya ||10||

|| 3.21.13 ||

ṛṣir uvāca—

juṣṭaṁ batādyākhila-sattva-rāśeḥ

sāṁsiddhyam akṣṇos tava darśanān naḥ |

yad darśanaṁ janmabhir īḍya sadbhir

āśāsate yogino rūḍha-yogāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

tvām ṛte paramānandaṁ dhig anya-vara-kāmukam |

athāpi kṛpaṇaṁ mānugṛhāṇa vara-dānataḥ ||

bateti harṣe | he īḍya ! no’smābhiḥ samagra-sattva-nidhes tava darśanād adyākṣṇoḥ sāṁsiddhyaṁ sāphalyaṁ juṣṭaṁ sevitam | tvad-darśanam eva mahā-phalam ity upapādayati | yasya tava darśanaṁ sadbhir uttarottaram āpādita-prakarṣair janmabhī rūḍho virūḍho yogo yais te’pi ||13||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : ṭīkāyāṁ kardama-vākya-vṛndārthaṁ saṅkṣepeṇāha—tvām iti | anugṛhāṇeti | kārpaṇya-doṣa-nāśārthaṁ varaṁ dattvā paścād anugṛhāṇety arthaḥ, prīti-svabhāvātmā ity uktatvāt (12) | bhava-sindhu-potam (14) ity anena lokānāṁ tādṛśopakartari tasmin prītir eva samuciteti sūcitam ||13-14||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : aho mad-bhāgyasya prabhoḥ kāruṇyasya ca paramā kāṣṭhā, yad acireṇaiva darśanaṁ labdhavān asmity āha—juṣṭam iti | bata iti vismaye | tava darśanān no’smākam akṣṇoḥ sāṁsiddhyaṁ mamākṣiṇī adyaiva samyak siddhe saphale jāte, itaḥ-pūrvam api siddhe akṣiṇī apy anakṣiṇī evety āstām iti bhāvaḥ | tava kīdṛśasya ? akhila-sattvānām atinikṛṣṭa-sattvānām ity arthaḥ | rāśeḥ rāśi-rūpasyety arthaḥ | naitad anurūpaṁ mayā sādhanaṁ ca kṛtam ity āha—yad iti | he īḍya ! sadbhir uttarottara-śreṣṭhair bahubhir api janmabhī rūḍho yogo yais te’pi āśāsate eva, na tu labhante ||13||

|| 3.21.14 ||

ye māyayā te hata-medhasas tvat-

pādāravindaṁ bhava-sindhu-potam |

upāsate kāma-lavāya teṣāṁ

rāsīśa kāmān niraye’pi ye syuḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sa-kāma-bhaktān vigarhayann āha—ya iti | he īśa ! ye niraye’pi syuḥ, teṣāṁ kāmānāṁ lavāya ye tvan-māyayā naṣṭa-buddhayaḥ, ta evopāsate | tvaṁ tu teṣāṁ tān kāmān api rāsi dadāsi ||14||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : hanta hanta ! evaṁ kṛpā-samudram api tvāṁ sakāmatayā bhajantīty aho mauḍhyaṁ lokānāṁ ! ity āha—ye tava māyayā hata-buddhayo bhavanti, te eva nānye kāma-lavāya kāma-leśam eva prāptum upāsate | tvaṁ tu teṣāṁ kāmān bahūn eva akāmitān api rāsi dadāsi | anyathā bhakti-sukhānabhijñās te tvad-bhaktim api tyaktuṁ naiva vilamberann iti bhāvaḥ | bhakter atyāge tu kāle te’pi niṣkāmā bhaveyur evety āśayena dadāsi, na tu tad-dāna eva tātparyam | yatas te kalatra-putra-kuṭumbaiśvaryādyāḥ kāmā niraye nārakyām api yonau syuḥ, kintu svarga-narakayoḥ karma-janyatva-niyamanāt tvad-dattānāṁ tu tādṛśānām api bhogānāṁ karma-janyatvābhāvāt bandhakatvābhāvena, naiva vastutaḥ | svarga-naraka-śabda-vyapadeśas tad api tat-tulyatvāt tathoktir iti jñeyam ||14||

|| 3.21.15 ||

tathā sa cāhaṁ parivoḍhu-kāmaḥ

samāna-śīlāṁ gṛhamedha-dhenum |

upeyivān mūlam aśeṣa-mūlaṁ

durāśayaḥ kāma-dughāṅghripasya ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yaḥ sa-kāmān nindāmi, so’ham api tādṛśa evety āha—tatheti | gṛha-medho gṛhāśramas tatra dhenuṁ tri-varga-dogdhrīṁ bhāryāṁ parivoḍhu-kāmaḥ pariṇetum icchan kāma-dughāṅghripasya kalpa-drumasya tava mūlam aṅghrim upeyivān upāgato’smi | nanu kāmādy-artham anyat kim apy upāsyatām ? na, yato’śeṣasya puruṣārthasya mūlam etad eva ||15||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : aśeṣa-mūlam iti,

svargāpavargayoḥ puṁsāṁ rasāyāṁ bhuvi sampadām |

sarvāsām api siddhīnāṁ mūlaṁ tac-caraṇārcanam || [bhā.pu. 10.81.19] iti ||15||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : yo’haṁ sa-kāmān nindāmi, sa cāham eva sakāmeṣu mukhya ity āha—tatheti | gṛha-medho gṛhāśramas tatra dhenuṁ tri-varga-dogdhrīṁ bhāryāṁ pariṇetum icchan | dhenu-padena svasya nirbuddhitvād vṛṣatvam uktam | kiṁ ca, sakāmeṣv api madhye ahaṁ durāśayaḥ durabhiprāyatvād iti nirbuddhir ity arthaḥ, yataḥ parivoḍhu-kāma eva kāma-dughāṅghripasya tava mūlam aṅghrim upeyivān | aśeṣāḥ sarve eva padārthā mūle yasya tat, svargāpavargayoḥ puṁsām [bhā.pu. 10.81.19] ity-ādi-ukteḥ ||15||

|| 3.21.16 ||

prajāpates te vacasādhīśa tantryā

lokaḥ kilāyaṁ kāma-hato’nubaddhaḥ |

ahaṁ ca lokānugato vahāmi

baliṁ ca śuklānimiṣāya tubhyam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : nanu tarhi mukty-artham eva kiṁ na bhajasi anadhikārāt ? ity āha—he adhīśa ! yas tvaṁ prajāpatiḥ, tasya tava vacasā tantryāyaṁ kāma-hato lokaḥ paśuvad baddhaḥ | he śukla ! śuddha-dharma-mūrte ! ahaṁ ca kila lokānugataḥ, atas tubhyaṁ baliṁ vahāmi, karma-mayīṁ tvad-ājñām anuvarte | animiṣāya kālātmane | tad-arthaṁ bhāryāṁ cecchāmīti ca-kārasyārthaḥ | na kevalaṁ lokānugato baliṁ vahāmi, kintu ṛṇa-trayāpākaraṇārtham iti kilety uktam ||16||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tarhi prītyaiva kevalayā kiṁ bhajasi ? tatrāha—prajeti | te tavādhīno yaḥ prajāpatir [gītā 3.10] ity-ādi-śrī-gītoktaḥ, so’pi tvad-ājñānusāreṇaiva tathā vaktīti bhāvaḥ |

nanu kāma-hato’nubaddho bhavatu nāma, tvaṁ na kāma-hataḥ | kathaṁ nāradādivat tan nātikrāmasi ? tatrāha—lokānugataḥ, teṣu lokeṣu pratiṣṭhā-lipsur ity arthaḥ | pratiṣṭhā-tyāgas tu duṣkara iti bhāvaḥ | tasmād animiṣāya kālātmakatvāt sarva-pravartakāya tubhyaṁ baliṁ pūjopahāraṁ ca vahāmi samarpayāmi | duṣphale labdhe’py āyatyāṁ tādṛśa-durvāsanā nivartyatām iti vicintyeti bhāvaḥ | yadyapy asau cāmala-svabhāvāt guru-lokataḥ saṅkocenaiva tādṛśa-nija-duḥkham apy aṅgīkaroti, na tu pratiṣṭhādīcchayā, tathāpi paramāścarya-śiromaṇiḥ svasmin tat-tad-doṣatvenaiva paśyatīti jñeyam ||16||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : nanv evaṁ ced asti vivekaḥ, tarhi niṣkāma eva kathaṁ na bhajasi ? ity ata āha—prajāpater brahmaṇas tava tvadīyasya "prajāḥ sṛja" iti vacasā tantryā, loko marīcy-ādiḥ |

saha-yajñāḥ prajāḥ sṛṣṭvā purovāca prajāpatiḥ |

anena prasaviṣyadhvam eṣa vo’stv iṣṭa-kāma-dhuk || [gītā 3.10] iti gītokteś ca |

so’pi tvad-ājñānusāreṇaiva vaktīti na tasyāpi doṣa iti te pada-dhvaniḥ |

nanu kāma-hato loko’nubaddho bhavatu nāma, tvaṁ tv akāma-hataḥ kathaṁ sanakādivat tan nātikramasi ? tatrāha—ahaṁ ca lokānugataḥ tad-anugāmi-buddhiḥ, na tu nāradādi-sadṛśībhavituṁ samartha ity arthaḥ | atas tantryā baddho vṛṣa iva baliṁ vahāmi, karma-mayīṁ tvad-ājñām anuvarte, tad-artham ekāṁ bhāryām icchāmīty arthaḥ | ca-kārāt tvad-bhaktiṁ ca karomy āyatyāṁ tvat-prāpty-artham iti bhāvaḥ | he śukla ! animiṣāya kāla-rūpaṁ tvāṁ prasādayitum ity arthaḥ ||16||

|| 3.21.17 ||

lokāṁś ca lokānugatān paśūṁś ca

hitvā śritās te caraṇātapatram |

parasparaṁ tvad-guṇa-vāda-sīdhu-

pīyūṣa-niryāpita-deha-dharmāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : animiṣāyety anena kālātmakāt tvatto bhītaḥ karma karomīty uktam | etat tu bhayaṁ tvad-bhaktānāṁ nāstīty āha dvābhyām | lokān kāmābhibhūtāṁs tān anusṛtān paśūṁś ca | viveke saty api punaḥ karma-jaḍān mādṛśān hitvā anādṛtya ye tava caraṇa-rūpam ātapatraṁ śritāḥ, tān evāha—tvad-guṇānāṁ vādaḥ kathā, tad eva sīdhu madirā, saṁsāra-vismārakatvāt | pīyūṣaṁ ruci-karatvāt | tena niryāpitā vilāpitā deha-dharmāḥ kṣut-pipāsādayo yaiḥ, eṣām ity uttareṇānvayaḥ ||17||
