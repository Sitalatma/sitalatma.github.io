(3.10)

daśamo’dhyāyaḥ

daśa-vidha-sṛṣṭi-varṇanam |

|| 3.10.1 ||

vidura uvāca—

antarhite bhagavati brahmā loka-pitāmahaḥ |

prajāḥ sasarja katidhā daihikīr mānasīr vibhuḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

daśame kāla-saṁpraśna- prāptiṁ vaktuṁ tad udbhavaḥ |

prākṛtādi-vibhāgena sargas tu daśadhocyate ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

kālasya lakṣaṇaṁ sāmānyato daśama ucyate |

sargo daśa-vidhaś cātaḥ prākṛtādi-vibhedavān ||1||

...

|| 3.10.2 ||

ye ca me bhagavan pṛṣṭās tvayy arthā bahu-vittama |

tān vadasvānupūrvyeṇa chindhi naḥ sarva-saṁśayān ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : me mayā | vadasva vada | bhāsayasveti vā ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : vadasva tvaṁ sarvathā jānāsīty ato vadety arthaḥ ||2||

...

|| 3.10.3 ||

sūta uvāca—

evaṁ sañcoditas tena kṣattrā kauṣāravir muniḥ |

prītaḥ pratyāha tān praśnān hṛdi-sthān atha bhārgava ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sañcoditaḥ prārthitaḥ | hṛdi-sthān evāha, na tu te praśnās tena vismṛtā ity arthaḥ ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : sūta uvāceti evam ity-ādikā saṅgatiḥ | svayam eva sūtena kriyate, na śrī-śuka-kṛtānūdyata iti bodhayati | maitreya uvācety ādikaṁ tu śrī-śuka-vākyānukaraṇam | śuka uvāceti bhaṛateti ca pāṭhau na bahu-sammatau ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : bhārgavety ādinā jñāne sāmarthyaṁ dyotitam | hṛdi-sthān iti na te praśān tena vismṛtā ity arthaḥ ||3||

|| 3.10.4 ||

maitreya uvāca—

viriñco’pi tathā cakre divyaṁ varṣa-śataṁ tapaḥ |

ātmany ātmānam āveśya yathāha bhagavān ajaḥ ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : ātmani parameśvare mana āveśya ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : ātmani śrī-nārāyaṇe ātmānaṁ mana āveśya ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : ātmani śrī-nārāyaṇe ātmānaṁ manaḥ ||4||

|| 3.10.5-6 ||

tad vilokyābja-sambhūto vāyunā yad-adhiṣṭhitaḥ |

padmam ambhaś ca tat-kāla- kṛta-vīryeṇa kampitam ||

tapasā hy edhamānena vidyayā cātma-saṁsthayā |

vivṛddha-vijñāna-balo nyapād vāyuṁ sahāmbhasā ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : abja-saṁbhūto brahmā yad adhiṣṭhāya sthitaḥ, kartari ktaḥ | tat padmam ambhaś ca vilokya | kathaṁ-bhūtaṁ padmam ambhaś ca ? tena pralaya-kālena kṛtaṁ vīryaṁ yasya tena vāyunā kampitaṁ, nyapād ity uttareṇānvayaḥ ||5|| vivṛddhaṁ vijñānaṁ balaṁ ca yasya | nyapāt sarvaṁ pītavān ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tad iti yugmakam ||5|| nyapād iti yāvat pralaye vardhate sma tāvad iti bhāvaḥ ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : abja-saṁbhūto brahmā yat padmam adhiṣṭhita iti kartari ktaḥ | tad eva padmam ambhaś ca vāyunā kampitaṁ vīkṣya nyapād ity uttareṇānvayaḥ | vāyunā kathambhūtena ? tat-kālena pralaya-samayena kṛtaṁ vīryaṁ yasya tena nyapāt nāśayāmāsety arthaḥ | pralaya-kāle yāvat-pramāṇam ambho vāyuś cāvardhata tāvat-pramāṇam eveti jñeyam ||5||

|| 3.10.7 ||

tad vilokya viyad-vyāpi puṣkaraṁ yad-adhiṣṭhitam |

anena lokān prāg-līnān kalpitāsmīty acintayat ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : puṣkaraṁ padmam | prāg-līnān trīl lokān kalpitāsmi srakṣyāmi ||7||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tataś ca puṣkaraṁ padmaṁ viyad-vyāpi satya-loka-paryantam ucchritaṁ kalpitāsmi srakṣyāmi ||7||

|| 3.10.8 ||

padma-kośaṁ tadāviśya bhagavat-karma-coditaḥ |

ekaṁ vyabhāṅkṣīd urudhā tridhā bhāvyaṁ dvi-saptadhā ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : bhagavatā svayaṁ karaṇīye karmaṇi codito niyuktaḥ saṁs tadā padma-kośaṁ praviśya tam ekam eva tridhā loka-rūpeṇa vyabhāṅkṣīd vibabhāja | ekena kamala-mukulena kathaṁ loka-traya-sṛṣṭir ity asaṁbhāvanāṁ vārayituṁ tasya viśālatām āha—dvi-saptadhā caturdaśa-loka-rūpeṇa | urudhā tato’pi bahu-prakāreṇa | bhāvyaṁ bhāvayituṁ yogyam | ato na tena tri-lokī-karaṇaṁ citram ity arthaḥ ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tataś ca āviśya praviśya bhagavatā karmaṇi jagat-sarge niyuktaḥ | tam ekam eva tridhā loka-traya-rūpeṇa vyabhāṅkṣīt vibabhāja | ekena kamala-koṣeṇa kathaṁ loka-traya-sṛṣṭir ity asambhāvanāṁ vārayituṁ tasya viśālatām āha—dvi-saptadhā caturdaśa-loka-rūpeṇa, urudhā tato’pi bahu-prakāreṇa, bhāvyaṁ bhāvayituṁ yogyam | ato na tena trilokī-karaṇaṁ citram iti svāmi-caraṇāḥ | prathamaṁ sa nālena kamala-koṣeṇa tridhā, tataś caturdaśadhā, tataḥ sūrya-candrādi-loka-bhedena urudhā vyabhāṅkṣīt | kīdṛśaṁ ? bhāvyaṁ dhyeyaṁ vairājopāsakair ity anye ||8||

|| 3.10.9 ||

etāvāñ jīva-lokasya saṁsthā-bhedaḥ samāhṛtaḥ |

dharmasya hy animittasya vipākaḥ parameṣṭhy asau ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : animittasya brahmārpaṇa-buddhyā kṛtasya | a iti brahmeti śruteḥ ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tri-lokī-rūpeṇaiva vibhāge hetum āha—etāvāṁs tri-lokī-rūpo jīva-lokasya jīvānāṁ bhoga-sthānasya praty ahaṁ sṛjyasya saṁsthā-bhedo racanāviśeṣa uktaḥ | nanu parameṣṭhino’pi jīvatvāviśeṣād brahma-lokasyāpi kim iti praty ahaṁ sṛṣṭir na bhavati tatrāha—hi yasmād nimittasya niṣkāmasya dharmasya vipākaḥ phala-rūpo’sau | upalakṣaṇam etat satya-lokasya mahaḥ prabhṛti-lokānāṁ tad vāsināṁ ca | trai-lokyasya kāmya-karma-phalatvāt pratikalpam utpatti-vināśau bhavataḥ | mahaḥ prabhṛtīnām upāsanāsamucita-niṣkāma-dharma-phalatvād dvi-parārdha-paryantaṁ na nāśaḥ | tatra-sthānāṁ ca tataḥ paraṁ prāyeṇa muktir iti bhāvaḥ ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : etāvān dvisaptadhā paryantaḥ śrī-brahmā tu sarvato viśiṣṭatvāt tat-sraṣṭety āha—dharmasyeti | animittasya svabhāvenaiva kriyamāṇasya, na tu gṛhasthavat tat-tat-saṅkalpenapīty arthaḥ | vipākaḥ parākāṣṭhā-prāpta-pariṇāmaḥ | asāv iti | brahmāntaraṁ vyavacchinatti, brahmaṇo’pi bhayaṁ matta[bhā.pu. 11.10.30] ity-ādy-ukteḥ ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : jīva-lokasya vyaṣṭi-virāṭ-samūhasya saṁsthā-bhedaḥ pratibimbocita-sthāna-racanā-viśeṣa uktaḥ | nanu katham etāvatāṁ jīvānām ekenaiva brahmaṇā saṁsthā-nirmāṇaṁ ? ity ata āha—hi yasmād asau animittasya niṣkāmasya dharmasya tapo-vidyādeḥ vipākaḥ paripāka-mūrtir eva tapo-balena kim aśakyam iti bhāvaḥ ||9||

|| 3.10.10 ||

vidura uvāca—

yathāttha bahu-rūpasya harer adbhuta-karmaṇaḥ |

kālākhyaṁ lakṣaṇaṁ brahman yathā varṇaya naḥ prabho ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : lakṣaṇaṁ lakṣyam ātmā ca svarūpam iti kathyate ity abhidhānam ||10||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : kāla-bhedena loka-sṛṣṭi-bhedaṁ śrutvā tam eva kālaṁ jijñāsuḥ pṛcchati | yat kālākhyaṁ lakṣaṇaṁ svarūpam āttha abravīḥ | kathaṁ kālaḥ kalpate ? kiṁ vā tasya sūkṣmaṁ sthūlaṁ ca rūpaṁ ? iti yathāvad varṇayety arthaḥ ||10||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : kālākhyayāsadita-karma-tantra iti guṇena kālānugateneti kālena so’jaḥ puruṣāyuṣeti tat-kāla-kṛta-vīryeṇety ādibhiḥ pralaya-sṛṣṭy-ādikaṁ puruṣa-prayatnādikaṁ ca kālenaiva siddhyatīti vismṛtaḥ kālaṁ pṛcchati, yat kālākhyaṁ lakṣaṇaṁ svarūpam āttha abravīḥ, tad yathāvad eva varṇaya ||10||

...

|| 3.10.11 ||

maitreya uvāca—

guṇa-vyatikarākāro nirviśeṣo’pratiṣṭhitaḥ |

puruṣas tad-upādānam ātmānaṁ līlayāsṛjat ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : guṇa-vyatikaram ākaroti tad dṛṣṭvā apratiṣṭhito’nyatra | sa bhagavaḥ kasmin pratiṣṭhita iti sva-mahimni iti śrutiḥ | tad upādānaṁ guṇa-vyatikaropādāna-kartāraṁ, sṛṣṭy-ādy-arthatvena tasya puruṣasya brahmā-viṣṇu-maheśvarā iti trīṇi rūpāṇy ātmanā sṛṣṭāni ||11||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tatra sāmānyataḥ kālasya svarūpam atrocyate, uttarādhyāye tu viśeṣataḥ | guṇānāṁ vyatikaro mahad-ādi-pariṇāmas tenaivākriyate yaḥ, sa kāla iti śeṣaḥ | vakṣyate caikādaśe—guṇa-vyatikaraḥ kālaḥ svabhāvaḥ sūtram eva ca [bhā.pu. 11.22.13] iti | svatas tu nirviśeṣaḥ | apratiṣṭhitaḥ kvāpy aparyavasitaḥ, ādy-anta-śūnya ity arthaḥ | etad eva darśayitum īśvaraḥ sṛṣṭy-ādi tena nimitta-bhūtena karotīty āha—puruṣa iti | upādīyate nimittatayā svīkriyate ity upādānam | sa kāla upādānaṁ nimittaṁ yasmin, tam ātmānam eva viśva-rūpeṇāsṛjat | sva-vyatirekeṇa sṛjyasyābhāvāt | etac ca vastu-kathana-mātram | kālena nimittena cāsṛjad ity etāvad eva vivakṣitam ||11||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : guṇānāṁ vyatikarāt mahad-ādi-pariṇāmād eva ākāraḥ svarūpa-jñānaṁ yasya saḥ | svatas tu nirviśeṣaḥ apratiṣṭhitaḥ kvāpy aparyavasitaḥ ādy-anta-śūnya ity arthaḥ | yena nimitta-bhūtena sṛṣṭy-ādikaṁ bhavati, sa kāla ity āha—puruṣa iti | upādīyate nimittatayā svīkriyate ity upādānam | sa kāla eva upādānaṁ nimitta-kāraṇaṁ yasmin, tam ātmānam ātma-śakti-kāryatvād ātma-śabda-vācyaṁ viśvam asṛjat ||11||

|| 3.10.12 ||

viśvaṁ vai brahma-tan-mātraṁ saṁsthitaṁ viṣṇu-māyayā |

īśvareṇa paricchinnaṁ kālenāvyakta-mūrtinā ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : brahma nirmātṛkam | māyayā sāmarthyena | tatra yat saṁhatrīśvarākhyg rūpg tat kālākhyam | kal chedena iti dhātoḥ | atha trayī vāva prakṛtiḥ sattvaṁ rajas tama iti tāṁ nārāyaṇaṁ paryapaśyad ananya-pratiṣṭhitaḥ tvaṁ vā etam āhuḥ puruṣa iti pūrṇo hy eṣa bhavati satre dhāva bhūvaiṣāṁ guṇānām upādānāya viṣṇur vāva sattvasya rajaso brahmeśāno nāma tamasaḥ sa āviveśa | brahmā brahmāṇaṁ nāma caturmukhaḥ | īśa īśānaṁ nāma pañca-mukhaṁ yo vā īśa īśānam āviveśa | taṁ vā enaṁ kāla ity ācakṣate kāla ity ācakṣate iti saukarāyaṇa-śrutiḥ ||12||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sva-vyatirikta-sṛjyābhāvaṁ darśayan kālasya sṛṣṭi-nimittatāṁ darśayati—viśvam iti | viṣṇu-māyayā saṁsthitaṁ saṁhṛtaṁ brahma-tan-mātraṁ sad viśvam | īśvareṇa kartrā kālena nimittena paricchinnaṁ pṛthak prakāśitam | avyaktā mūrtiḥ svarūpaṁ yasyeti svato nirviśeṣatā darśitā ||12||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : kālam ācaṣṭe—yat tu lakṣaṇaṁ jñāpakaṁ guṇa-vyatikareṇa kriyā-rūpaṁ tat-pariṇāmādi paurvyāparyānusandhānenākriyate ākāra-bhedair lakṣyate yaḥ, sa kāla ity arthaḥ | viṣṇor īśvarasya māyayā sṛṣṭy-ādi-śaktyā saha līnaṁ brahmaṇi viṣṇāv eva tādātmyopapannaṁ sat, īśvareṇa tat-prabhāva-rūpeṇa kālena paricchinnaṁ pṛthak kṛtam ity arthaḥ ||12||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : yo viśvaṁ paricchinatti, sa kāla ity āha—viśvam iti | viṣṇor māyayā śaktyā samyak sthitam idaṁ viśvaṁ brahma-tan-mātraṁ kāraṇaṁ yasya tat īśvareṇa īśvara-prabhāva-rūpeṇa kālena paricchinnam etāvad iti pariccheda-viṣayīkṛtam avyaktā mūrtir yasya iti svato nirviśeṣatā darśitā ||12||

|| 3.10.13 ||

yathedānīṁ tathāgre ca paścād apy etad īdṛśam |

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

sṛṣṭiś ca pralayaś caiva saṁsāro muktir eva ca |

devarṣi-prabhṛtayo lokā lokā bhūr-ādayas tathā ||

anādy-ananta-kālīnāḥ sarvadaika-prakārakāḥ |

jagat-pravāhaḥ satyo’yaṁ naiva mithyā kathañcana ||

ye tv etad anyathā brūyuḥ sarva-hantāra eva te |

devair brahmādibhiḥ śaptā ṛṣībhir mānuṣādibhiḥ ||

setihāsais tathā vedaiḥ sarve yānty avaraṁ tamaḥ |

sarva-brahmatva-vettāro jīva-brahmatasya-vedinaḥ ||

anya-sāmya-vido viṣṇor viṣṇu-dveṣṭāra eva ca |

sarve yānti tamo ghoraṁ na caiṣām utthitiḥ kvacit || iti skānde ||13||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : apratiṣṭhitatvaṁ darśayituṁ tat-kārya-viśva-pravāhasyāpratiṣṭhām āha—yathedānīm asti tathāgre pūrvam apy āsīt paścād api bhaviṣyati ||13||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : yathety ardhakam | īdṛśaṁ pūrvokta-prakāreṇa pralayādy-ātmakaṁ sarga iti prākṛto vaikṛtas tv iti | tatra kecit prākṛtā īśvara-sṛṣṭāḥ, kecic ca vaikṛtā brahma-sṛṣṭā ity arthaḥ | ubhayātmakas tu vakṣyate ||13||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : nanv ādy-antayor asattvāsad eva viśvam ity ato’satyasya viśvasya kiṁ paricchinatvād yukteti cen, maivaṁ vācyam ity āha—yatheti | agre pūrva-mahā-pralayasyādāv api etad viśvam āsīd eva, paścād uttara-pralayānte’pi bhaviṣyaty evety ape’sya na mithyā-bhūtatvaṁ, kintu naśvaratvam eveti bhāvaḥ ||13||

|| 3.10.14 ||

sargo nava-vidhas tasya prākṛto vaikṛtas tu yaḥ |

kāla-dravya-guṇair asya tri-vidhaḥ pratisaṅkramaḥ ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

tāmasasya padārthasya sattvaṁ hi laya-kāraṇam |

sāttvikasya tamaś caiva tayor api rajaḥ kvacit ||

guṇato’yaṁ layaṁ prokto dravyatas tu virodhinā |

kālataḥ kāla-saṅkhyāko layaḥ sarvasya vastunaḥ || iti brahma-tarke ||14||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : evaṁ sāmānyataḥ kālaṁ nirūpya viśeṣato nirūpayiṣyaṁs tan nimittasya sargasya pūrvoktān eva bhedān anuvadati—sarga iti | yas tu prākṛto vaikṛtaś ca sa daśamaḥ | tan nimittam eva tri-vidhaṁ pralayam āha—kālenaiva kevalena nityaḥ pralayaḥ | dravyeṇa saṅkarṣaṇāgny-ādinā naimittikaḥ | guṇaiḥ sva-sva-kāryaṁ grasadbhiḥ prākṛtikaḥ ||14||

————————————————————————————————————————
