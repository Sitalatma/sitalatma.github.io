(3.1)

atha prathamo’dhyāyaḥ

dhṛtarāṣṭraṁ parityajya gatasya vidurasyoddhavena saha samāgamaḥ |

|| 3.1.1 ||

śrī-śuka uvāca—

evam etat purā pṛṣṭo maitreyo bhagavān kila |

kṣattrā vanaṁ praviṣṭena tyaktvā sva-gṛham ṛddhimat ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

yuddha-kāle tu viduras tīrtha-yātrāṁ gato’pi san |

prāya āste gajapure pāṇḍavānāṁ vyapekṣayā || iti skānde ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : śrī-gaṇeśāya namaḥ | śrī-gopāla-kṛṣṇāya namaḥ |

tṛtīye tu trayastriṁśad-adhyāyaiḥ sarga-varṇanam |

īśekṣayā guṇa-kṣobhāt sargo brahmāṇād asambhavaḥ ||

tatra tu prathame’dhyāye bandhūn hitvā gatāyuṣaḥ |

nirgatasyoddhavenādau samvādaḥ kṣattur ucyate ||

bhagavad brahma-saṁproktaṁ saṅkṣepta varṇitaṁ purā |

prāha bhāgavataṁ śeṣa-proktaṁ vistarataḥ punaḥ ||

dvedhā hi śrīmad-bhāgavata-sampradāya-pravṛttiḥ | ekataḥ saṅkṣepataḥ śrī-nārāyaṇa-brahma-nāradādi-dvāreṇa, anyatas tu vistarataḥ śeṣa-sanat-kumāra-sāṅkhyāyanādi-dvāreṇa | tatra dvitīye śrī-nārāyaṇa-brahma-saṁvādena saṅkṣepato’ham evāsam ity-ādi catuḥ-ślokyā śrī-bhāgavataṁ nirūpitam | tad eva brahma-nārada-saṁvādena daśa-lakṣaṇatayā kiñcid vistareṇoktam | tad eva śeṣoktam ativistarato vaktuṁ tṛtīyādy-ārambhaḥ |

tatra tṛtīye prathamaṁ kṣattur maitreya-saṅgamaś caturbhir adhyāyaiḥ, tato’ṣṭabhiḥ sa-visargaḥ sarga-prapañcaḥ | tato visarga-prastāvena saptabhir varāhāvatāraḥ | tata ekena visarga-samāhāraḥ | tat-prasaṅgena caturbhiḥ kapilāvatāraḥ| tato navabhiḥ kapilākhyānam |iti trayastriṁśatādhyāyais tṛtīya-skandha-pravṛttiḥ | tatra dvitīya-skandhānte—

parimāṇaṁ ca kālasya kalpa-lakṣaṇa-vigraham |

yathā purastād vyākhyāsye pādmaṁ kalpam atho śṛṇu ||[bhā.pu. 2.10.47]

iti pratijñātam arthaṁ vistareṇa nirūpayitum itihāsaṁ prastauti bhagavān śukaḥ, evam iti dvābhyām | ṛddhimat sarva-sampadbhiḥ sampūrṇam ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : śrī-śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-candrāya namaḥ |

tṛtīya-krama-sandarbhe sandarbhāṇāṁ samāhṛtiḥ |

kriyate yan-nideśena sa me’nanya-gater gatiḥ ||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

praṇamya śrī-guruṁ bhūyaḥ śrīkṛṣṇaṁ karuṇārṇavam |

lokanāthaṁ jagac-cakṣuḥ śrī-śukaṁ taṁ upāśraye ||

gopa-rāmā-jana-prāṇa-preyase’tiprabhuṣṇave |

tadīya-priya-dāsyāya māṁ madīyam ahaṁ dade ||

tryadhika-triṁśad-adhyāyyā tṛtīye sarga ucyate |

tattvaiḥ samaṣṭi-sṛṣṭir yaḥ pradhānāt puruṣekṣitāt ||

pradarśya bhagavad-brahma-nāradādyaiḥ pravartitam |

śeṣa-kumārādyaiś ca darśyate ||

viduroddhava-saṁvādaś caturbhiḥ punar aṣṭabhiḥ |

sa-visargaḥ sarga-vidhiḥ saptabhiḥ kroḍa-kīrtanam ||

tato visarga-saṅkṣepa ekena kapilodayaḥ |

caturbhir navabhiś ceti tṛtīya-skandha-saṅgrahaḥ ||

tatra tu pratham’dṛśayatie kṣattā hitvāgrajaṁ gataḥ |

tīrthaṁ tatra śrutāniṣṭo’pṛcchan militaṁ uddhavam ||

pūrva-skandhe’ṣṭame’dhyāye rājñāṁ kṛteṣu bahuṣu praśneṣu dvi-tra-praśnānāṁ uttaraṁ dattvā mahā-munir manasi parāmamarśa—yathānena rājñāṁ sampraty ahaṁ pṛṣṭas tathaiva pūrvaṁ vidureṇāpi maitreyaḥ pṛṣṭa iti | tataś ca tat-prastāvenaivotthāpitena rājñaḥ sarva-praśnottaraṁ dāsyāmīti niścityāha—evam iti | ṛddhimat sarva-sampadbhiḥ pūrṇam ||1||
