SB 3.17

(3.17)

saptadaśo’dhyāyaḥ

hiraṇyākṣa-hiraṇyakaśipvor janma, tadānīm utpāta-darśanam, hiraṇyākṣa-digvijayaś ca |

|| 3.17.1 ||

maitreya uvāca—

niśamyātma-bhuvā gītaṁ kāraṇaṁ śaṅkayojjhitāḥ |

tataḥ sarve nyavartanta tridivāya divaukasaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

tataḥ saptadaśe janma tayor loka-bhayaṅ-karam |

hiraṇyākṣa-prabhāvaś ca varṇyate dig-jaye’dbhutaḥ ||

kṣemaṁ vidhāsyati [3.16.37] iti brahma-vacanānantaraṁ | śaṅkayā tyaktāḥ ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : kāraṇaṁ tasyopadravasyotpattau pratīkāre ca hetum | śaṅkayā sandehena ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

tayor janmany ariṣṭāṇi spaṣṭaṁ saptadaśe’bhavan |

upāhasac ca dig-jetā hiraṇyākṣaḥ pracetasam ||

śaṅkayā ujjhitā bhagavaty eva viśvāsena iti bhāvaḥ ||1||

...

|| 3.17.2 ||

ditis tu bhartur ādeśād apatya-pariśaṅkinī |

pūrṇe varṣa-śate sādhvī putrau prasuṣuve yamau ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : bhartur ādeśāt lokān sa-pālāṁs trīṁś caṇḍi muhur ākrandayiṣyataḥ [bhā.pu. 3.14.39] iti vākyāt | svāpatyābhyāṁ pariśaṅkinī devopadravaṁ śaṅkamānā ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : apatya-pariśaṅkinī iti | śaṅkamānā surārdanāt [bhā.pu. 3.15.1] iti pūrvoktatvāt tasmāt tatra bhaya-yuktā ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : bhartur ādeśāt "lokān krandayiṣyati" iti tad-vākyāt apatyābhyāṁ sarva-lokopadravam apatyaṁ prati viṣṇu-hastato vadhād vā śaṅkamānā ||2||

...

|| 3.17.3 ||

utpātā bahavas tatra nipetur jāyamānayoḥ |

divi bhuvy antarikṣe ca lokasyoru-bhayāvahāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tatra tadā nipetur udbabhūvuḥ | uru bhayam āsamantād vahantīti ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : nipetur udbabhūvuḥ ||3||

...

|| 3.17.4 ||

sahācalā bhuvaś celur diśaḥ sarvāḥ prajajvaluḥ |

solkāś cāśanayaḥ petuḥ ketavaś cārti-hetavaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : acalaiḥ sahitā bhuvaḥ pradeśāḥ | ketavaś codayāṁ cakrur iti śeṣaḥ ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : ketavaś ca udagur iti śeṣaḥ ||4||

...

|| 3.17.5 ||

vavau vāyuḥ suduḥsparśaḥ phūt-kārān īrayan muhuḥ |

unmūlayan naga-patīn vātyānīko rajo-dhvajaḥ ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

phaṭkāraś caiva phutkārās tathā kilakilādayaḥ |

anukāra-śabdā vijñeyā ye cānye tādṛśā matāḥ || ity abhidhānam ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : phūt-kārān iti tīvra-vāyu-śabdānukaraṇam | naga-patīn mahā-vṛkṣān | vātyā eva anīkaṁ yasya | raja eva dhvajo yasya ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : phūt-kārān iti tīvra-vāyu-śabdānukaraṇam | vātyā eva anīkaṁ senā yasya saḥ ||5||

...

|| 3.17.6 ||

uddhasat-taḍid-ambhoda- ghaṭayā naṣṭa-bhā-gaṇe |

vyomni praviṣṭa-tamasā na sma vyādṛśyate padam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : uccair hasantya iva taḍito yeṣu teṣām ambudānāṁ ghaṭayā samūhena naṣṭo bhā-gaṇaḥ sūryādi-prabhā-samūho yasmin | padaṁ sthānaṁ na vyādṛśyate sma īṣad api nādṛśyata ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : uccair hasantya iva taḍito yeṣu teṣām ambudānāṁ ghaṭayā naṣṭo bhā-gaṇaḥ sūryādi-prabhā yasmiṁs tasmin vyomni | padaṁ sthānam ||6||

...

|| 3.17.7 ||

cukrośa vimanā vārdhir udūrmiḥ kṣubhitodaraḥ |

sodapānāś ca saritaś cukṣubhuḥ śuṣka-paṅkajāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : vārdhiḥ samudro vimanā iva | udgatā ūrmayo yasmāt | kṣubhitā udara-sthā makarādayo yasmin | udapānair vāpī-kūpādibhiḥ sahitāḥ | śuṣkāṇi paṅkajāni yāsu ||7||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : vārdhiḥ samudraḥ | sodapānāḥ sa-kūpāḥ ||7||

...

|| 3.17.8 ||

muhuḥ paridhayo’bhūvan sarāhvoḥ śaśi-sūryayoḥ |

nirghātā ratha-nirhrādā vivarebhyaḥ prajajñire ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : paridhayaḥ pariveṣāḥ | sa-rāhvo rāhu-grastayoḥ | nirghātā nirabhra-garjitāni | ratha-nirhrāda-tulyā dhvanayaḥ | vivarebhyo giri-guhābhyaḥ ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : nirghātā nirabhra-garjitāni | ratha-nirhrāda-tulyā dhvanayaḥ | vivarebhyo giri-guhābhyaḥ ||8||

...

|| 3.17.9 ||

antar-grāmeṣu mukhato vamantyo vahnim ulbaṇam |

sṛgālolūka-ṭaṅkāraiḥ praṇedur aśivaṁ śivāḥ ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : ṭaṅkāro’py anukāra-śabdaḥ | śṛgālās tatra pumāṁsaḥ |

api sambhāvanā-praśna-garhāśaṅkā-samuccaye |

nāśas tatra śṛgālānāṁ śivānāṁ cānyathā svaraḥ || ity āgneye ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sṛgālānām ulūkānāṁ ca ṭaṅkārair dhvanibhiḥ saha | śivāḥ sṛgālyaḥ ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : ṭaṅkārair dhvanibhiḥ saha | śivāḥ sṛgāla-striyaḥ ||9||

...

|| 3.17.10 ||

saṅgītavad rodanavad unnamayya śirodharām |

vyamuñcan vividhā vāco grāma-siṁhās tatas tataḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā), viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : grāma-siṁhāḥ śvānaḥ ||10||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

...

|| 3.17.11 ||

kharāś ca karkaśaiḥ kṣattaḥ khurair ghnanto dharā-talam |

khārkāra-rabhasā mattāḥ paryadhāvan varūthaśaḥ ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : khārkāro’py ānukāra-śabdaḥ ||11||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : karkaśais tīkṣṇaiḥ | he kṣattaḥ ! khārkāro gardabha-jāti-śabdas tasmin rabhasaḥ saṁbhramo yeṣām | varūthaśaḥ saṅghaśaḥ ||11||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : he kṣattaḥ ! khārkāro gardabha-śabdānukaraṇaṁ, tenaiva rabhaso harṣo yeṣāṁ te ||11||

...

|| 3.17.12 ||

rudanto rāsabha-trastā nīḍād udapatan khagāḥ |

ghoṣe’raṇye ca paśavaḥ śakṛn-mūtram akurvata ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : rāsabha-śabdāt trastāḥ sarvataḥ krośantaḥ ||12||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : rāsabhāt gardabha-śabdāt trastāḥ ||12||

...

|| 3.17.13 ||

gāvo’trasann asṛg-dohās toyadāḥ pūya-varṣiṇaḥ |

vyarudan deva-liṅgāni drumāḥ petur vinānilam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : gāvo’trasan trastāḥ | asṛg dohāś ca babhūvuḥ | deva-liṅgāni vyarudan, pratimānām aśru-srāva āsīd ity arthaḥ ||13||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : asṛg-dohā rudhira-dugdhāḥ | deva-liṅgāni deva-pratimāḥ ||13||

...

|| 3.17.14 ||

grahān puṇyatamān anye bha-gaṇāṁś cāpi dīpitāḥ |

aticerur vakra-gatyā yuyudhuś ca parasparam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : puṇyatamān guru-budhādīn bha-gaṇān bahūni nakṣatrāṇi ca, anye krūra-grahā maṅgalādayo’ticerur atikramya jagmur vakra-gatyā pratyāvṛtya yuyudhuś ca ||14||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : puṇyatamān bṛhaspati-śukrādīn anye krūra-grahā maṅgalādayo’tikramya jagmur vakra-gatyā pratyāvṛtya yuyudhuḥ ||14||

...

|| 3.17.15 ||

dṛṣṭvānyāṁś ca mahotpātān atat-tattva-vidaḥ prajāḥ |

brahma-putrān ṛte bhītā menire viśva-samplavam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : brahma-putrān ṛte vinā, teṣāṁ sva-śāpādi-jñānāt ||15||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : dṛṣṭveti | brahma-putrān sanakādīn ṛte brahma-sṛṣṭāḥ | sarvā eva prajā atat-tattva-vido bhītā viśva-samplavaṁ menire | devās tu brahma-vākyāt tattva-vido’bhītā āsan, na tu viśva-samplavaṁ menire ity arthaḥ ||15||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : brahma-putrān sanakādīn, teṣāṁ sva-śāpādi-jñānāt ||15||

...

|| 3.17.16 ||

tāv ādi-daityau sahasā vyajyamānātma-pauruṣau |

vavṛdhāte’śma-sāreṇa kāyenādri-patī iva ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : vyajyamānam ātma-pauruṣaṁ pūrva-siddhaṁ sva-pauruṣaṁ yayoḥ ||16||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tāv iti | atrānayor aṇimādi-śaktyā gūḍha-sthitau ṭīkā-sammatiś ca ||16||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : brahma-śāpe’pi bhagavad-bhaktānāṁ sarvādhikyam iti bodhayituṁ tayor akṛtair atisukṛtaiḥ trailokyādhipatya-bala-prabhāva-bhoga-yogaiśvaryādīni kṛtair api bahubhir duṣkṛtair naraka-yātanā-gandha-mātrābhāvaṁ ca vaktum āha—tāv iti ||16||

...

|| 3.17.17 ||

divi-spṛśau hema-kirīṭa-koṭibhir

niruddha-kāṣṭhau sphurad-aṅgadā-bhujau |

gāṁ kampayantau caraṇaiḥ pade pade

kaṭyā sukāñcyārkam atītya tasthatuḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : niruddhā vyāptāḥ kāṣṭhā diśo yābhyām | sphuranty aṅgadāni yeṣu te bhujā yayoḥ | aṅgadeti ṭāb-antatvam ārṣam | śobhanā kāñcī yasyāṁ tayā kaṭyā ||17||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : somapā-śabdavad aṅgadā-śabdo’pi bhavet ||17||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : pañcaṣa-dina-vayastve eva tāv evam abhūtām ity āha—divīti | kāṣthā diśaḥ | aṅgadeti ṭāb-antatvam ārṣam ||17||

...

|| 3.17.18 ||

prajāpatir nāma tayor akārṣīd

yaḥ prāk sva-dehād yamayor ajāyata |

taṁ vai hiraṇyakaśipuṁ viduḥ prajā

yaṁ taṁ hiraṇyākṣam asūta sāgrataḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yamayor madhye yaḥ sva-dehāt prathamam ajāyata, taṁ yathā hiraṇyakaśipuṁ viduḥ | sā ditiḥ prathamaṁ yam asūta, taṁ hiraṇyākṣaṁ yathā vidus tathā nāma kṛtavān ity arthaḥ | ayaṁ bhāvaḥ—yadā hi garbhādhāna-samaye yoni-puṣpaṁ viśad-vīryaṁ dvidhā vibhaktam ādi-paścād-bhāvena praviśati, tadā yamau bhavataḥ | tayoś ca pitṛtaḥ praveśa-krama-viparyayeṇa mātṛtaḥ prasūtiḥ |

yadā viśed dvidhā-bhūtaṁ bījaṁ puṣpaṁ parikṣarat |

dvau tadā bhavato garbhau sūtir veśa-viparyayāt || iti piṇḍa-siddhi-smaraṇāt |

ataḥ sva-dehāt pūrvaṁ yo jātas tasya hiraṇyakaśipur iti ditiḥ prathamaṁ yam asūta tasya hiraṇyākṣa iti nāma kṛtavān iti ||18||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : daśame’hni nāma-karaṇam āha—prajāpatiḥ kaśyapaḥ | yamayor madhye sva-dehād yaḥ prathamam ajāyata, taṁ hiraṇyakaśipuṁ yathā viduḥ, sā ditiḥ prathamaṁ yam asūta, taṁ hiraṇyākṣaṁ yathā vidus tathā tayor nāma akārṣīd iti | yathā-tathayor adhyāhāreṇānvayaḥ |

yadā viśed dvidhā-bhūtaṁ bījaṁ puṣpaṁ parikṣarat |

dvau tadā bhavato garbhau sūtir veśa-viparyayāt || iti piṇḍa-siddhi-smaraṇāt |

pitṛ-krameṇa hiraṇyakaśipur jyeṣṭhaḥ pitrā kaśyapena tasyaivādāv ahitatvāt | tathā mātṛ-krameṇa hiraṇyākṣo jyeṣṭhaḥ mātrā dityā tasyaivādau prasūtatvāt | evaṁ kramād vaividhyena dvayor api jyeṣṭhatve pituḥ prādhānyāt hiraṇyakaśipur eva jyeṣṭhatvena vyāhṛtaḥ ||18||

|| 3.17.21-22 ||

taṁ vīkṣya duḥsaha-javaṁ raṇat-kāñcana-nūpuram |

vaijayantyā srajā juṣṭam aṁsa-nyasta-mahā-gadam ||

mano-vīrya-varotsiktam asṛṇyam akuto-bhayam |

bhītā nililyire devās tārkṣya-trastā ivāhayaḥ ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

na devānāṁ prajāpānāṁ vijetā varato vinā |

balena vidyayā vāpi na samastat patīn vinā |

|| 3.17.25 ||

tasmin praviṣṭe varuṇasya sainikā

yādo-gaṇāḥ sanna-dhiyaḥ sa-sādhvasāḥ |

ahanyamānā api tasya varcasā

pradharṣitā dūrataraṁ pradudruvuḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sannā avasannā dhīr yeṣām | varcasā tejasā pragharṣitā abhibhūtāḥ santaḥ ||25||

|| 3.17.26 ||

sa varṣa-pūgān udadhau mahā-balaś

caran mahormīñ chvasaneritān muhuḥ |

maurvyābhijaghne gadayā vibhāvarīm

āsedivāṁs tāta purīṁ pracetasaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : mahormīn abhijaghne | maurvyā mārayatīti maurvaṁ kārṣṇāyasaṁ tan-mayyā | yad vā, mūrvā nāma tṛṇa-viśeṣaḥ, tan-maya-rajjvā dṛḍha-nibaddhayety arthaḥ | vibhāvarī-saṁjñām āsedivān prāptaḥ ||26||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : varṣa-pūgān varṣa-samūhān vyāpya mahormīm eva gadayā jaghne | calan capalaḥ maurvaṁ kārṣṇāyasaṁ murvā-nāma-tṛṇa-viśeṣo vā, tan-mayyā | aho jala-madhye kācit purī vartate, tad imāṁ praviśāmīti vibhāvarī-saṁjñāṁ prāptaḥ ||26||

|| 3.17.27 ||

tatropalabhyāsura-loka-pālakaṁ

yādo-gaṇānām ṛṣabhaṁ pracetasam |

smayan pralabdhuṁ praṇipatya nīcavaj

jagāda me dehy adhirāja saṁyugam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : asurāṇāṁ lokaḥ pātālaṁ, tat-pālakam | pralabdhum upahasituṁ praṇipatya ||27||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : asura-lokāḥ dānava-prāya-janāḥ | taṁ gṛhītvānayad bhṛtyo varuṇasyāsuro’ntikaṁ [bhā.pu. 10.28.2] iti daśamāt ||27||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : asura-lokasya pātālasya pālakam | pralabdhum upahasituṁ praṇipatya ||27||

|| 3.17.28 ||

tvaṁ loka-pālo’dhipatir bṛhac-chravā

vīryāpaho durmada-vīra-māninām |

vijitya loke’khila-daitya-dānavān

yad rājasūyena purāyajat prabho ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yad yasmād rājasūye bhavān ayajat ||28||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : ayajad bhavān iti pṛthag-vākyaṁ vijityeti viparīta-lakṣaṇā, tena tadānīṁ daityānām abhāvāt sarvair api rājasūyaḥ kartuṁ śakyate sma | tena samprati māṁ jitvā punar api rājasūyaṁ kurv iti bhāvaḥ | yad vā, kṛto’pi rājasūyas tadaiva te siddhyati, yadi māṁ jayasīti bhāvaḥ ||28||

|| 3.17.29 ||

sa evam utsikta-madena vidviṣā

dṛḍhaṁ pralabdho bhagavān apāṁ patiḥ |

roṣaṁ samutthaṁ śamayan svayā dhiyā

vyavocad aṅgopaśamaṁ gatā vayam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : upaśamaṁ yuddhādi-kautukād uparamam ||29||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : svayā dhiyā śamayann iti | atra me roṣo na phalavān bhaviṣyatīti parāmārśenety arthaḥ | upaśamaṁ gatāḥ vayaṁ samprati pravayaso’bhūmety arthaḥ | tena yauvanāvasthāyāṁ yadi tvāṁ prāpsyaṁ tadā tvādṛśānāṁ sahasram apy ajeṣyam iti bhāvaḥ | yad vā, upaśamaṁ sannyāsam | tena sannyāsaṁ cen nākariṣyaṁ tadādhunāpi tvām ajeṣyam iti bhāvaḥ ||29||

|| 3.17.30 ||

paśyāmi nānyaṁ puruṣāt purātanād

yaḥ saṁyuge tvāṁ raṇa-mārga-kovidam |

ārādhayiṣyaty asurarṣabhehi taṁ

manasvino yaṁ gṛṇate bhavādṛśāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yuddha-mārga-nipuṇaṁ tvāṁ yas toṣayiṣyati tam ihi gaccha | gṛṇate stuvanti ||30||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tarhi me raṇa-kaṇḍūyātaḥ kathaṁ nistāraḥ ? tatrāha—paśyāmīti | tasmā he asurarṣabha ! tvam ihi gaccha | manasvinaḥ śūrā gṛṇate raṇa-kaṇḍūyāpahartṛtvena stuvanti ||30||

|| 3.17.31 ||

taṁ vīram ārād abhipadya vismayaḥ

śayiṣyase vīra-śaye śvabhir vṛtaḥ |

yas tvad-vidhānām asatāṁ praśāntaye

rūpāṇi dhatte sad-anugrahecchayā ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : ārāc chīghram | vismayo naṣṭa-garvaḥ | vīraśaye raṇāṅgaṇe | rūpāṇi varāhādy-avatārān ||31||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : nanu taṁ katibhir dinair lapsye ? iti tatrāha—tam iti | ārāt pañcabhir eva dinair ity arthaḥ | vismayo vigata-garvaḥ san śayiṣyase, samprati tu garva-roga-pīḍito raṇa-kaṇḍūyodvegena tvaṁ na śeṣe iti bhāvaḥ | vīra-śaye vīrāḥ śerate yatra tatra raṇājire iti tava śayyāpi samucitā bhaviṣyatīty arthaḥ | tadā caraṇādy-aṅga-yantraṇa-kāriṇaḥ paricārakā api bahavo bhaviṣyantīty āha—śvabhir iti |

nanv evaṁ cet, tasyākāraṁ jñāpaya | yathādhunaiva brahmāṇḍasyāntar-bahir api anivṣya taṁ niṣkāśya tena sahāhaṁ yotsye, kathaṁ sa māṁ hanyād iti yūyaṁ paśyatha | iti tatrāha—ya iti | praśāntaye nāśāya rūpāṇi varāha-nṛsiṁhādyākārān dhatte iti sāmānyato jānāmi, tvāṁ tu kena rūpeṇa vadhiṣyatīti viśeṣaṁ tu na jānāmīty arthaḥ ||31||
