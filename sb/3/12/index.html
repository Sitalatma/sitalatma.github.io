(3.12)

atha dvādaśo’dhyāyaḥ

sanakāde rudrasya marīcy-ādīnāṁ sarasvatyā vedādīnāṁ cotpatti-varṇanam, brahmaṇo dehāt svāyambhuva-manoḥ śatarūpāyāś ca janma |

|| 3.12.1 ||

maitreya uvāca—

iti te varṇitaḥ kṣattaḥ kālākhyaḥ paramātmanaḥ |

mahimā veda-garbho’tha yathāsrākṣīn nibodha me ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

dvādaśe tu kumārādi-manaḥ-sargāsamedhanāt |

kāya-dvaidhena yaunas tu manu-sargo’nuvarṇyate ||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

sanakādi-marīcy-ādi-dharādharmādi-sṛg-vidhiḥ |

dvādaśe sarga-vṛddhy-arthaṁ mithunaṁ dehato’sṛjat ||

mahimā prabhāvaḥ ||1||

...

|| 3.12.2 ||

sasarjāgre’ndha-tāmisram atha tāmisram ādi-kṛt |

mahā-mohaṁ ca mohaṁ ca tamaś cājñāna-vṛttayaḥ ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

tamas tu śārvaraṁ proktaṁ mohaś caiva viparyayaḥ |

tad-āgraho mahā-mohas tāmisraḥ krodha ucyate |

maraṇaṁ tv andha-tāmisram avidyā pañca-parvikā || iti bhārate |

tamo’jñānaṁ viparyāso moho’nye tu tad-āgrahā iti hari-vaṁśe ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : agra iti | brahmā sva-sṛṣṭau prathamam avidyā-vṛttīḥ sasarja | tatra tamo nāma svarūpāprakāśaḥ | moho dehādy-ahaṁ-buddhiḥ | mahā-moho bhogecchā | tāmisraṁ tat-pratighāte krodhaḥ | andha-tāmisraṁ tan-nāśe’ham eva mṛto’smīti buddhiḥ | tad evoktaṁ vaiṣṇave—

tamo’viveko mohaḥ syād antaḥ-kraṇa-vibhramaḥ |

mahā-mohas tu vijñeyo grāmya-bhoga-sukhaiṣaṇā ||

maraṇaṁ hy andha-tāmisraṁ tāmisraḥ krodha ucyate |

avidyā pañca-parvaiṣā prādurbhūtā mahātmanaḥ || iti |

pātañjale’py eta evoktāḥ—avidyāsmitā-rāga-dveṣābhiniveśāḥ pañca kleśāḥ iti | śrī-viṣṇusvāmi-proktā vā, ajñāna-viparyāsa-bheda-bhaya-śokāḥ | tad uktaṁ svādṛg-uttha-viparyāsa ity-ādi ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : avidyā-vṛttayaḥ pūrva-siddhā eva, sṛṣṭy-ārambhe brahmataḥ sakāśāt tama-ādi-rūpeṇāvirbabhūvur ity āha—sasarjeti | tatra tamo nāma jīvasya svarūpāprakāśaḥ | moho dehādāv aham-āropaḥ | mahā-moho bhoktavya-viṣayeṣu mamatvāropaḥ | tāmisraḥ bhoga-pratighāte saty antaḥ-karaṇa-dharmasya krodhasya svīkāraḥ | tataś cāndha-tāmisraḥ krodha-tan-mayī-bhāva-rūpā mūrcchaiva maraṇam | ete jīvasyāsanto’py avidyayā sṛṣṭāḥ | yathoktaṁ vaiṣṇave—

tamo’viveko mohaḥ syād antaḥ-kraṇa-vibhramaḥ |

mahā-mohas tu vijñeyo grāmya-bhoga-sukhaiṣaṇā ||

maraṇaṁ hy andha-tāmisraṁ tāmisraḥ krodha ucyate |

avidyā pañca-parvaiṣā prādurbhūtā mahātmanaḥ || iti |

pātañjale’py eta evoktāḥ—avidyāsmitā-rāga-dveṣābhiniveśāḥ pañca kleśāḥ iti | śrī-viṣṇusvāmi-proktā vā, ajñāna-viparyāsa-bheda-bhaya-śokāḥ | vastutas tv avidyāyā āvaraṇa-vikṣepāv eva dvau dharmau | tāv eva avidyāsmitā-śabdātma|bhāv ajñāna-viparyāsa-śabdābhyāṁ cocyate | rāga-dveṣābhiniveśās tv antaḥkaraṇa-dharmā api vikṣepāṁśa-prādhānyād vikṣepa-prapañcatayaivocyante iti jñeyam ||2||

|| 3.12.4 ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yadyapi pratikalpaṁ sanakādi-sṛṣṭir nāsti, tathāpi brāhma-sargatvād ihocyate | vastutas tu mukhya-sargādaya eva pratikalpaṁ bhavanti, sanakādayas tu brāhma-kalpa-sṛṣṭā evānuvartante ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : sanakaṁ ceti | sāṅkhya-yoga-vairāgya-tapāṁsīti catvāry eva vidyāyāś catasro vṛttayaḥ | tāsām eva sanakādi-catuṣṭaya-rūpeṇāvirbhāvaḥ | kiṁ ca, bhaktyā vinā vidyāyā vaiphalyāt tad-vṛttiṣu tapa-ādiṣv api bhaktir guṇī-bhūtā satī tiṣṭhed iti sanakādayo’pi bhaktimanta eva dṛṣṭāḥ | mukhya-bhaktyer āvirbhāvas tu nārada-rūpeṇāgre vakṣyate ||4||

|| 3.12.5 ||

tān babhāṣe svabhūḥ putrān prajāḥ sṛjata putrakāḥ |

tan naicchan mokṣa-dharmāṇo vāsudeva-parāyaṇāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : svabhūr brahmā ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tān iti anicchāyāṁ hetur mokṣa-dharmāṇa iti | nanu prajāpatīnām api mokṣa-dharmitvaṁ dṛśyate | tatrāha—vāsudeva-parāyaṇāḥ | devarṣi-bhūtāpta-nṛṇāṁ pitṝṇām ity-ādibhyaḥ ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tan naicchann iti jñāna-yogaṁ khalv āvidyaka-karma-yoga-nirāsaka eva syād iti dyotitam ||5||

|| 3.12.6 ||

so’vadhyātaḥ sutair evaṁ pratyākhyātānuśāsanaiḥ |

krodhaṁ durviṣahaṁ jātaṁ niyantum upacakrame ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : avadhyāto’vajñātaḥ | pratyākhyātam anaṅgī-kṛtam anuśāsanaṁ yaiḥ ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : avadhyāto’vajñātaḥ | niyantum upacakrama iti brahmaṇo rājasa-svabhāvatvaṁ vyaktam | evaṁ tama-ādi-sanakādyoḥ sṛṣṭau tāmasa-sāttvika-bhāvodayau tasya pūrvaṁ jñeyau ||6||

|| 3.12.7 ||

dhiyā nigṛhyamāṇo’pi bhruvor madhyāt prajāpateḥ |

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : hṛdīti | hṛd-ādīnām ahaṅkāra-kāryatvād ahaṅkārādhiṣṭhātuḥ rudrasya tat-tat-sthānaucityāt | krodha-pakṣe tu hṛt-krodhasya janma-sthānam eva sa tatra pravṛtto netra-pāṇi-pādendriyeṣv api tiṣṭhati tat-tad-vyāpāra-sūcyaḥ | tathaivāsuṣu prāṇeṣv api nāsāśvāsādhiya-sūcyaḥ | tathā bahir-ākāśasya pracaṇḍa-ghora-siṁhādi-śabdair vāyv-agni-jalānāṁ śoṣakatva-dāhakatva-plāvakatvaiḥ mahyāḥ sva-vikārair mudgarādibhis tathā teṣāṁ saṅghātaiḥ siṁha-vyāghra-sarpādibhiḥ | sūrya-candrayos tāpa-himābhyāṁ mārakatvam astīti teṣv api krodhas tiṣṭhati | tapa ity upalakṣaṇaṁ sāṅkhya-yoga-vairāgyāṇām api teṣām api bhakti-pālyatvābhāve krodha-sthānatvaṁ dṛṣṭaṁ tapo-dīpita-manyava ity-ādi-vākyaiḥ | ata evoktam, āruhya kṛcchreṇa paraṁ padaṁ tataḥ patanty adho’nādṛtya yuṣmad-aṅghrayaḥ [bhā.pu. 10.2.32]iti ||11||

|| 3.12.19 ||

tapasaiva paraṁ jyotir bhagavantam adhokṣajam |

sarva-bhūta-guhāvāsam añjasā vindate pumān ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

kālato balataś caiva jñānānandādikair api |

sarvair guṇair viṣṇur eva śreṣṭhas tad-avamā ramā ||

anantāṁśena kālāt tv asamatasyāś caturmukhaḥ |

avaro bahulāṁśena tat-samo vāyur ucyate ||

niyamād vāyur evaiko brahmatvaṁ yāti nāparaḥ |

tasmāt samānatām uktau vāyutve kiñcid ūnatā ||

daśavarṣaṁ tu tat paścāj jananaṁ tat-striyor api |

ānandādis tad-daśāṁśaḥ kālaḥ saṁvatsarāt paraḥ ||

yāvat paścāj janis tāvat pūrvaṁ ced akṣayo bhavet |

brahma-vāyos tu te devau tad-deśāṁśaḥ sukhādikaḥ ||

śeṣasya garuḍasyāpi kālo divya-sahasrakaḥ |

śeṣa-rudrau brahma-vāyu yathā tadvat parasparam ||

tad-devyas tad-daśāṁśā syus tatas tv indrādayo matāḥ |

evam uktau ca pūrvaṁ ca nānyathā kvacid iṣyate |

anyathoktir yatra ca syāt tan-mohārthaṁ bhaviṣyati |

pūrvāpara-viparyāso bahu-rūpatva-hetutaḥ ||

iti viṣṇu-kṛta-tattva-viveke | athāta ānandasya mīmāṁsā devāsurebhyo maghavān pradhāna ity-ādi ca |

indrādyāḥ sanakādyāś ca dakṣādyā ye’pi cāpare |

ṛṣayo manavo devās tad-vaśā ye ca kecana ||

rāmāyā avarāḥ sarve guṇaiḥ sarvair nisaṁśayaḥ |

tat-samo na bhaviṣyo vā na bhūto’dyatano’pi vā ||

ṛte hariṁ brahma-vāyu śeṣavīndrān sa-bhāryakān |

śaṅkaraṁ ceti vettavyam anyan mohārtham ucyate || iti viṣṇu-kṛta-tattva-viveke ||19||
