SB 3.15

(3.15)

atha pañcadaśo’dhyāyaḥ

vaikuṇṭha-dhāma-varṇanam, tatra gatānāṁ sanakādīnāṁ jaya-vijayāv uddiśya śāpa-dānaṁ bhagavato darśanaṁ ca |

|| 3.15.1 ||

maitreya uvāca—

prājāpatyaṁ tu tat tejaḥ para-tejo-hanaṁ ditiḥ |

dadhāra varṣāṇi śataṁ śaṅkamānā surārdanāt ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

hata-prabhaiḥ suraiḥ pṛṣṭaḥ prāha pañcadaśe vidhiḥ |

tad-bījaṁ vipra-śāpādi vaikuṇṭhe viṣṇu-bhṛtyayoḥ ||

tad evaṁ devānāṁ brahmaṇaś ca saṁvāda-prastāvam uktvedānīṁ taṁ saṁvādaṁ vaktum āha—prājāpatyaṁ kāśyapaṁ tejo vīryaṁ pareṣāṁ tejo hantīti tathā ārṣaḥ | sva-putrābhyāṁ kariṣyate yat surāṇām ardanaṁ pīḍanaṁ tasmāc chaṅkamānā ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : śaṅkamāneti, garbhe prasūte nūnam asurārdano haniṣyatīti | ṭīkāyām ārṣa iti tejoghnam iti vaktavye tejo-hanam iti prayoga ārṣaḥ | amanuṣya-kartṛka ceti ṭaki tejo-ghnam ity eva syāt ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

suraiḥ pṛṣṭo vidhis tattvaṁ vaktuṁ diti-tanūjayoḥ |

śāpaṁ pañcadaśe viṣṇu-pārṣador viprato’bhyadhāt ||

tato diter garbhasya kā vārtety apekṣāyām āha—prājāpatyaṁ prajāpateḥ kaśyapasya sambandhi | pareṣāṁ tejo hantīti tathā tat pacādy ac | surāṇām ardanaṁ pīḍanaṁ tasmāt śaṅkamānā | asurārdanād viṣṇor iti vā tad-dhastato mṛtyu-śravaṇāt ||1||

...

|| 3.15.2 ||

loke tenāhatāloke loka-pālā hataujasaḥ |

nyavedayan viśva-sṛje dhvānta-vyatikaraṁ diśām ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tena garbha-tejasā hatāloke nirasta-sūryādi-prakāśe | hataujaso hata-prabhāvāḥ | dhvāntena vyatikaraṁ saṅkaram ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : dhvāntena vyatikaram āsaṅgam ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tena garbha-tejasā hatāloke āhatāloke iti ca pāṭhaḥ | vyatikaraṁ vyāptim ||2||

...

|| 3.15.3 ||

devā ūcuḥ—

tama etad vibho vettha saṁvignā yad vayaṁ bhṛśam |

na hy avyaktaṁ bhagavataḥ kālenāspṛṣṭa-vartmanaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : vettha jānāsi kiṁ vicārayasi | yady ato vayaṁ saṁvignā bhītāḥ | avyaktam ajñātam | na spṛṣṭaṁ vartma jñāna-pracāro yasya ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : atra devā ūcur iti kvacit | tama etad ity-ādinā parameśvarābhedena brahmāṇaṁ stuvanti ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : vettha jānāsy eva, yato bhagavataḥ sarvajñasya tava kālena alupta-jñāna-mārgasya ||3||

...

|| 3.15.4 ||

deva-deva jagad-dhātar lokanātha-śikhāmaṇe |

pareṣām apareṣāṁ tvaṁ bhūtānām asi bhāva-vit ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : brahmāṇaṁ parameśvaratvena stuvantaḥ prārthayante—deva-deveti saptabhiḥ | loka-nāthānāṁ śikhāmaṇe | bhāva-vid abhiprāyajño’si | kenābhiprāyeṇa diter garbho vardhata iti jānāsīty arthaḥ ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : loka-pāla iti puṇyāraṇyaḥ ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : lokanāthānām indrādīnāṁ śikhāmaṇe ||4||

...

|| 3.15.5 ||

namo vijñāna-vīryāya māyayedam upeyuṣe |

gṛhīta-guṇa-bhedāya namas te’vyakta-yonaye ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : vijñānaṁ vīryaṁ balaṁ yasya | idaṁ brahma-deham upeyuṣe prāptavate | gṛhīto guṇa-bhedo rajo-guṇo yena | vyaktasya prapañcasya yonaye kāraṇāya | na vyaktā kenāpi pramāṇena vijñātā yonir yasyeti vā ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : nama ity-ādinā parameśvarābhedena stutiḥ ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : idaṁ brahma-deham upeyuṣe prāptavate | guṇa-bhedo rajaḥ | avyaktaḥ parameśvara eva yoniḥ kāraṇaṁ yasya tasmai ||5||

...

|| 3.15.6-7 ||

ye tvānanyena bhāvena bhāvayanty ātma-bhāvanam |

ātmani prota-bhuvanaṁ paraṁ sad-asad-ātmakam ||

teṣāṁ supakva-yogānāṁ jita-śvāsendriyātmanām |

labdha-yuṣmat-prasādānāṁ na kutaścit parābhavaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sa-kāmatayā pratikṣaṇaṁ duḥkham anubhavanto niṣkāma-bhaktān stuvanta āhur dvābhyām | ye tvā tvām ananyena niṣkāmeṇa bhāvena bhaktyā dhyāyanti | ātmano jīvān bhāvayatīti tathā | svasmin protāni grathitāni bhuvanāni yena | cetanācetana-prapañca-kāraṇam ity arthaḥ | tatra hetuḥ—sad-asad-ātmakaṁ kārya-kāraṇa-rūpaṁ, vastutas tābhyāṁ param ||6||

jitaḥ śvāsaḥ indriyāṇy ātmā manaś ca yaiḥ | ataḥ supakva-yogāḥ | ata eva prāpto yuṣmat-prasādo yais teṣām ||7||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : ye tveti yugmakam | ananya-bhaktyā labdha-yuṣmat-prasādas tataḥ sunirvyūḍha-bhakti-yogatvena svata eva śvāsādi-jaya iti kramaḥ ||6-7||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tad api ananyena parameśvarād abhinnena bhāvena ye hiraṇyagarbhopāsakāḥ | yad vā, ananyena niṣkāmeṇa bhāvena bhaktyā bhāvayanti dhyāyanti ātmano jīvān bhāvayati sṛjatīti tathā | samaṣṭitvād ātmani svasmin protāni prathitāni bhuvanāni yena tam | ataḥ santo’santaś ca ātmano devāsurādyā jīvā yasmiṁs tam ||6-7||

...

|| 3.15.8 ||

yasya vācā prajāḥ sarvā gāvas tantyeva yantritāḥ |

haranti balim āyattās tasmai mukhyāya te namaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : anye tu nityaṁ karma-kleśina ity āhuḥ—yasyeti | tantyā dāmanyā āyattā adhīnāḥ | mukhyāya niyantre prāṇa-rūpāyeti vā | tathā ca śrutiḥ—tasya vāk-tantir nāmāni dāmāni ity-ādiḥ ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : vācā veda-lakṣaṇayā | tathā ca śrutiḥ—tasya vāk tantrir nāmāni dāmānīty ādi ||8||

...

|| 3.15.9 ||

sa tvaṁ vidhatsva śaṁ bhūmaṁs tamasā lupta-karmaṇām |

adabhra-dayayā dṛṣṭyā āpannān arhasīkṣitum ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tamasā aho-rātra-vibhāgābhāvena luptāni karmāṇi yeṣām | āpannān āpad-gatān asmān ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tamasā aho-rātra-vibhāgābhāvena | āpannān asmān | adabhra-dayayā analpa-kṛpayā ||9||

...

|| 3.15.10 ||

eṣa deva diter garbha ojaḥ kāśyapam arpitam |

diśas timirayan sarvā vardhate’gnir ivaidhasi ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : āpat-kāraṇam āhuḥ | eṣa garbhaḥ tasya viśeṣaṇam | arpitaṁ nikṣiptaṁ kāśyapam ojo vīryam | diśas timirayan tamo-vyāptāḥ kurvan ||10||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : garbha-nidānam eva pṛcchāmaḥ | garbhāt tamo jātam iti tu jānīma evety āha—eṣa iti | edhate iti kvacit pāṭhaḥ ||10||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : nanu, viśeṣa-vārtāṁ bruteti tatrāhuḥ—kāśyapam ojo vīryam arpitaṁ sat diter garbhaḥ ||10||

...

|| 3.15.11 ||

maitreya uvāca—

sa prahasya mahā-bāho bhagavān śabda-gocaraḥ |

pratyācaṣṭātma-bhūr devān prīṇan rucirayā girā ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : diteḥ kuceṣṭitaṁ jñātvā prahasya devānāṁ ye śabdā vijñapti-vākyāni teṣāṁ gocaro viṣaya-bhūtaḥ pratyabhāṣata ||11||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : vṛddho bālakān pratīva prahasya | śabda-mātra-gocara iti brahmaṇaḥ sādhāraṇāgocaratvaṁ vyajya mahimānaṁ darśayati ||11||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : aho, ajñā mām eva stuvanti—etad vipatti-trāṇe ko’haṁ varāka iti vṛddho bālān pratīva prahasya | śabdā devānāṁ vijñapti-vākyāni, teṣāṁ gocaraḥ viṣayaḥ ||11||

...

|| 3.15.12 ||

brahmovāca—

mānasā me sutā yuṣmat- pūrvajāḥ sanakādayaḥ |

cerur vihāyasā lokāl lokeṣu vigata-spṛhāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yuṣmat-sakāśāt pūrvaṁ jātāḥ ||12||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : etad-vipat-kāraṇavatīṁ prācīnām adbhutāṁ kathāṁ tāvat śṛṇutety āha—mānasā iti ||12||

|| 3.15.13 ||

ta ekadā bhagavato vaikuṇṭhasyāmalātmanaḥ |

yayur vaikuṇṭha-nilayaṁ sarva-loka-namaskṛtam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : vaikuṇṭhākhyaṁ lokaṁ yayuḥ ||13||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : amalāḥ śuddhā bhavanty ātmāno’ntaḥkaraṇāni yatas tasya ||13||

...

|| 3.15.14 ||

vasanti yatra puruṣāḥ sarve vaikuṇṭha-mūrtayaḥ |

ye’nimitta-nimittena dharmeṇārādhayan harim ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : animittena viṣṇv-arpaṇena |

muktāś caivādhikārasthā dveṣā vaikuṇṭha-lokagāḥ |

amuktānāṁ bhramaḥ kvāpi na muktānāṁ kvacid bhavet || iti bhaviṣya-purāṇe |

kṛṣṇātmanāṁ manaraja ādadhur iti ca |

tiraścīnā sthāvarāś ca sarve jñānād vikuṇṭhagāḥ |

amuktā muktim āyānti niyamāt karmaṇaḥ kṣaye || iti ca ||14||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : taṁ varṇayati—vasantīty ādi-dvādaśabhiḥ | vaikuṇṭhasya harer iva mūrtir yeṣāṁ te | nimittaṁ phalaṁ na nimittaṁ pravartakaṁ yasmin | niṣkāmeṇa dharmeṇa ity arthaḥ | ārādhayan ārādhitavantaḥ ||14||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : vaikuṇṭhasyeva nityānanda-rūpā mūrtir yeṣāṁ te yatra vasanti | tathā na vidyate nimittaṁ kāraṇaṁ yasya sa śrī-bhagavān eva nimittaṁ phalaṁ yatra, tena dharmeṇa bhāgavatākhyena ye ca harim ārādhayan, te ca yatra vasantīty anvayaḥ | hari-padān atimātra-dṛṣṭaiḥ [bhā.pu. 3.15.20] iti, yan na vrajanti [bhā.pu. 3.5.23] ity-ādi vakṣyamāṇāt ||14||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : vaikuṇṭha-mūrtayaḥ bhagavat-sārūpyavantaḥ | animittaṁ sva-prayojanābhāva eva nimittaṁ pravartakaṁ yasmiṁs tena niṣkāmeṇa parama-dharmeṇa ity arthaḥ | ārādhayan pūrvam ārādhitavantaḥ ||14||

|| 3.15.15 ||

yatra cādyaḥ pumān āste bhagavān śabda-gocaraḥ |

sattvaṁ viṣṭabhya virajaṁ svānāṁ no mṛḍayan vṛṣaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yatreti | śabda-gocaro vedāntaika-vedyaḥ | virajaṁ sattva-mūrtiṁ viṣṭabhya dhṛtvā | vṛṣo dharma-mūrtiḥ | svānāṁ svān ||15||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīnātha-cakravartī (caitanya-mata-mañjuṣā) : yatra cādyaḥ pumān ity-ādi | ādyaḥ puruṣaḥ puruṣottamaḥ san śrī-kṛṣṇaḥ | sato bhāvaḥ sattvaṁ, sattā-svarūpaṁ śrī-vigraho nitya-vigrahaḥ śrī-kṛṣṇa iti yāvat | virajaṁ śuddhaṁ, śabda-gocaraḥ śruti-gocaraḥ, na tu dṛśyaḥ | vṛṣo dharma-mayaḥ | svānāṁ svān no’smān mṛḍayan pratipālayan |

yad vā, yatra pumān āste, ca-kāra āvartanīyaḥ, ādyo bhagavān śrī-kṛṣṇaḥ pumān viṣṇur vaikuṇṭhanāthaḥ | ādyo bhagavān kīdṛśaḥ ? no’smākaṁ dṛg-gocaraś ca, svānām iti pṛthag vyākhyeyam, svāṁśān svān mṛḍhayan no’smākaṁ śruti-gocara iti | ca-kāraḥ samuccaye | ekaḥ śruti-gocaratvena, anyo dṛg-gocaratvena | dṛg-gocaratvam ākṣepa-labhyam |

sa vā kīdṛśo bhagavān ? ubhaya-viśeṣaṇam | kiṁ kṛtvā āste ? sattvam avaṣṭabhya virajaṁ śuddham avaṣṭabhya adhaḥ-kṛtya uparibhūyety arthaḥ | eṣa pakṣa ādyaṁ prati, anyaṁ prati virajaṁ śuddhaṁ sattvam avaṣṭabhya ālambya | eṣa pakṣaḥ pumāṁsaṁ prati | vṛṣo varṣatīti vṛṣaḥ kāmadaḥ, ubhaya-viśeṣaṇam |

athavā yatra pumān āste, ādyo bhagavān śrī-kṛṣṇaḥ śabda-gocara āste, anyasya śabda-gocaratvād anyasya dṛg-gocaratvam ||15||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : sattvaṁ guṇaṁ sānnidhyenopakṛtya vṛṣaḥ sarvānanda-varṣī ||15||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : virajaṁ sattvaṁ śuddha-sattvaṁ viṣṭabhya dhṛtvā yasya nāma-rūpa-guṇa-līlādikaṁ sarvam eva śuddha-sattvam ity arthaḥ | svānāṁ svān vṛṣaḥ sākṣāt parama-dharma-rūpaḥ ||15||

|| 3.15.16 ||

yatra naiḥśreyasaṁ nāma vanaṁ kāma-dughair drumaiḥ |

sarvartu-śrībhir vibhrājat kaivalyam iva mūrtimat ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tatratyaṁ vanaṁ viśinaṣṭi—yatreti caturbhiḥ | sarveṣv apy ṛtuṣu śṛīḥ puṣpādi-saṁpady eṣāṁ taiḥ ||16||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīnātha-cakravartī (caitanya-mata-mañjuṣā) : nanu kathaṁ tayor ekatra sthitau kaścit śruti-gocaraḥ, kaścid anyathety āha—yatrety-ādi | yatra iti aupacārikam adhikaraṇaṁ, yathāṅguly-agre kari-śatam aṅgulyā nirdiṣṭe kari-śate’ṅgulisthatvenopacāraḥ, tathā yatreti yena nirdiṣṭe naiḥśreyasa-vane yatrety upacāraḥ | naiḥśreyasaṁ nāma vanaṁ vṛndāvanasyaiva nāmāntaram idam | kāma-dughaiḥ kalpa-drumatvāt | kaivalyam iva mūrtimat, kevalaṁ brahma svārthiko ṣaṇ tat-svarūpam | vakṣyati ca—kṣemaṁ vindanti mat-sthānaṁ yad brahma paramaṁ viduḥ [bhā.pu.11.20.37] iti | atas tatra vane ādyaḥ śrī-kṛṣṇas tiṣṭhati, sa tu tat-tad-upāsakānām eva dṛśyaḥ | brahmādīnām api śruti-gocara eveti tathā pūrvam uktam | yatreti vaikuṇṭhākhye dhāmani, tatra ubhayaṁ boddhavyam—yatreti param ekam, anyan naiḥśreyasākhyaṁ vanaṁ, tat tu pūrvoktādhikaraṇa-balāt tato’pi param | ubhayatra ubhayaṁ rūpam | pure pure puruṣaḥ, vane ādyaḥ śrī-kṛṣṇaḥ, puruṣaḥ khalu brahmādi-dṛśyaḥ, ādyaḥ khalu śruti-gocara ity arthaḥ | ata uktam—draṣṭāsi māṁ tataṁ brahman [bhā.pu. 3.9.31] ity-ādi bhauma-līlāyām āvirbhūtaṁ vṛndāvane iti ||16||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : kaivalyaṁ śuddhaṁ bhakti-sukhaṁ tad eva mūrtimad iva jātam, tad-eka-sampan-mayatvāt ||16||

|| 3.15.17 ||

vaimānikāḥ sa-lalanāś caritāni śaśvad

gāyanti yatra śamala-kṣapaṇāni bhartuḥ |

antar-jale’nuvikasan-madhu-mādhavīnāṁ

gandhena khaṇḍita-dhiyo’py anilaṁ kṣipantaḥ ||

na hy antaraṁ bhagavatīha samasta-kukṣāv

sarve daragataḥ brahma ye bhedena vicakṣate |

sarvatrāpi bhayaṁ teṣāṁ mṛtānāṁ tama eva ca || iti tattva-viveke ||33||

te vā amuṣya vadanāsita-padma-kośam

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : harṣa-kāritaṁ saṁbhramam āha dvābhyām | tevai kila amuṣyavadanam eva asita-padmasya kośo’ntar-bhāgaḥ, tam | asita-padma-kośam ity abhūtopamā | sundaratare aruṇe adharoṣṭhe kundavad dhāso yasmiṁs tam | ut ūrdhvaṁ vīkṣyalabdha-manorathāḥ santo nakhā eva aruṇa-maṇayas teṣāṁ śrayaṇam āśraya-bhūtam aṅghri-dvandvaṁpunaravekṣya adho-dṛṣṭyā vīkṣya, punaḥ punar evaṁ nirīkṣya yugapat sarvāṅga-lāvaṇya-grahaṇāśakteḥ paścān nidadhyur dhyātavantaḥ ||44||

|| 3.15.46 ||

kumārā ūcuḥ—

yo’ntar-hito hṛdi gato’pi durātmanāṁ tvaṁ

so’dyaiva no nayana-mūlam ananta rāddhaḥ |

yarhy eva karṇa-vivareṇa guhāṁ gato naḥ

pitrānuvarṇita-rahā bhavad-udbhavena ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : nityaṁ brahma-rūpeṇa prakāśase, na tac citram | idānīṁ tu parama-maṅgala-viśuddha-sattva-śrī-mūrtyā pratyakṣo’si | aho bhāgyam asmākam ity āhuḥ—he ananta, yastvaṁ hṛd-gato’pi durātmanām antar-hito na sphurasi, sa no’smākam antar-hito na bhavasi | nayana-mūlaṁ tv adyaiva rāddhaḥ prāpto’si | antardhānābhāve hetuḥ—bhavataḥ sakāśād udbhavo yasya tenāsmat-pitrāyarhi yadā evānuvarṇita-rahā upadiṣṭa-rahasyaḥ, tadaiva naḥkarṇa-mārgeṇa guhāṁ buddhiṁ gataḥ prāpto’sīti ||46||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīnātha-cakravartī (caitanya-mata-mañjuṣā) : yadyapi brahma-rūpeṇa nityatvaṁ prakāśase, tathāpi no na nirvṛtiḥ | ataḥ pitur mukhāt śrutvā draṣṭum āgatāḥ smaḥ, tatrāsmākaṁ bhāgya-vaśāt siddham evety āha—yo’ntar-hita ity-ādi | durātmanāṁ no’smākaṁ brahma-niṣṭhānāṁ hṛdi-gato’py antarhita eva tvam | evaṁ-rūpeṇa na prakāśase, so’dyaiva tvaṁ nayana-mūlaṁ rāddhaḥ prāptaḥ, etāvantaṁ kālam iyatā bhāgyena vañcitāḥ sma iti durātmatattv nirvedo darśitaḥ ||46||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : atha śrī-bhagavatas tādṛśatve tādṛśatva-vyañjinīṁ nijāṁ mūrtiṁ teṣām eva sva-hārdābhivyakti-kareṇa stuti-vākyena pramāṇayati—śrī-kumārā ūcur iti |

stutim āha—ya iti pañcabhiḥ | atra akṣara-juṣām api [bhā.pu. 3.15.43] ity anusṛtya vyākhyāyate | nityaṁ brahma-rūpeṇa prakāśase na tac citram | idānīṁ tu viśuddha-sattva-laksaṇena svarūpa-śakti-vṛtti-viśeṣeṇa prakāśitayā ghana-prakāśa-para-tattvaika-rūpayā mūrtyā pratyakṣo’si | aho bhāgyam asmākam ity āhuḥ—he ananta ! yas tvaṁ duṣṭātmanām antaryāmi-rūpeṇa hṛd-gato’py antarhito bhavasi, na sphurasīty arthaḥ, sa tvaṁ no’smākam antarhito na bhavasi |

tatra hetuḥ—yarhy evabhavad-udbhavena asmākaṁ pitrā asmabhyam anuvarṇita-rahā upadiṣṭa-catuḥślokī-prokta-prema-bhakty-ākhya-rahasyaḥ proktaḥ, tarhy eva karṇa-vivareṇa tad-rūpatayā guhāṁgato’sy antaḥ sphurasīti nayana-mūlaṁ tv adyaiva rāddhaḥ prāpto’si | kṛpā-vaiśiṣṭyena tarhy eva samyaṅ nayanaṁ praviṣṭaḥ, tatrāvirbhūta ity arthaḥ | raha ity ad-anto’pi kvacit | tan-mātaro yad abhajan raha-rūḍha-bhāvāḥ [bhā.pu. 10.55.40] ity-ādiṣu dṛśyate | nādyaivety atra “so’dyaiveti kva” iti tu na ṭīkā-sammatam | “no’dyaiva” iti kvacit ||46||

sanandanādyair hṛdi saṁvibhāvyam [bhā.pu. 9.8.23]

iti śrīmad-aṁśumad-vākyādau, ihātma-tattvaṁ samyag jagāda munayo yad acakṣatātman [bhā.pu. 2.7.5] iti brahma-vākyādau, tasmai mṛdita-kaṣāyāya tamasaḥ pāraṁ darśayati bhagavān sanat-kumāraḥ [chā.u. 7.26.2] ity-ādi-śrutau ca tathā prasiddham |

āsannānubhavasyaiva tu siddhasyāṇimādibhir vighno’pi sambhāvyaḥ, na tu siddhānubhavasya,

taṁ sa-prapañcam adhirūḍha-samādhi-yogaḥ |

svāpnaṁ punar na bhajate pratibuddha-vastuḥ || [bhā.pu. 3.28.38] iti śrī-kapila-deva-vākyāt |

ata eva teṣāṁ pradhvasta-māyā-guṇa-bheda-mohānāṁ krodhādikam api durghaṭa-ghaṭanā-kāriṇyā śrī-bhagavad-icchayaiva jātam iti tair api vyākhyātam | tad evaṁ teṣāṁ satata-brahmānanda-magnatvaṁ siddham | tad uktam—akṣara-juṣām api [bhā.pu. 3.15.43] iti, yo’ntarhitaḥ [bhā.pu. 3.15.46] ity-ādi ca |

śrūyate cānyatra brahma-juṣām avikṣipta-cittatvam | tathā saptame śrī-nārada-vākyam—

kāmādibhir anāviddhaṁ praśāntākhila-vṛtti yat |

cittaṁ brahma-sukha-spṛṣṭaṁ naivottiṣṭheta karhicit || [bhā.pu. 7.15.35] iti |

tathāpi teṣāṁ bhagavad-ānandākṛṣṭa-cittatvam ucyate | evam anyeṣām apy ātmārāmāṇāṁ tādṛśatvaṁ śrūyate—sva-sukha-nibhṛta-cetās tad-vyudas tāny abhāvo’py ajita-rucira-līlākṛṣṭa-sāraḥ [bhā.pu. 12.12.69] ity-ādiṣu |

atha loka-saṅgrahārthair vaiṣā teṣāṁ bhakti-prakriyā prācīna-saṁskāra-vaśā vā ? naivam | ubhayatrāpi vāso yathā parikṛtaṁ madirā-madāndha [bhā.pu. 3.28.37] itivat tatrāveśāsambhavāt | dṛśyate tv anyatrānāveśaḥ—

mānasā me sutā yuṣmat-pūrvajāḥ sanakādayaḥ |

cerur vihāyasā lokān lokeṣu vigata-spṛhāḥ || [bhā.pu. 3.15.12] ity-abhidhānāt |

bhagavati tv āveśaḥ, paramahaṁsa-mahā-munīnām anveṣaṇīya-caraṇau [bhā.pu. 3.15.37] ity atra yādṛcchikatāvirodhy anveṣaṇīyatvābhidhānāt | pañcame tu, asaṅga-niśita-jñānānala-vidhūtāśeṣa-malānāṁ bhavat-svabhāvānām ātmārāmāṇāṁ munīnām anavarata-pariguṇita-guṇa-gaṇa-kathano'si [bhā.pu. 5.3.11] ity atra gadye tad-eka-niṣṭhatvam apy uktam | ajita-rucira-līlākṛṣṭa-sāra [bhā.pu. 12.12.69] ity atraiva ca | atrāpi teneśa nirvṛtim avāpur alaṁ dṛśo na [bhā.pu. 3.15.50] ity ādau sukhadatvam api sākṣād evoktam |

atra pūrvokta-hetoś ca, stutau pratyutopālambha-prasaṅgāc ca, snehāvaloka-kalayā hṛdi saṁspṛśantaṁ [bhā.pu. 3.15.39] iti sākṣād-ukteś ca, dṛśām eva sukhaṁ jātam ity anāsaktir eva vyañjitety api na vyākhyeyam | tasmād ātmārāmāṇāṁ ramaṇāspadatvād brahmākhyam ātma-vastv eva śrī-bhagavān | tatrāpi cakāra teṣāṁ saṅkṣobham akṣara-juṣām api citta-tanvoḥ [bhā.pu. 3.15.43] iti śravaṇāt tato’pi ghana-prakāśaḥ | tat tad vicitra-śrī-bhagavad-aṅgopāṅgādy-abhiniveśa-darśanānanda-vaicitrī copalabhyate, sā cānyathānupapattyā svarūpa-śakti-vilāsa-rūpaiveti |

nanu, bhavatu teṣām ānandādhikyāt tasmin nirviśeṣa-svarūpānandasyaiva ghana-prakāśatā, upādhi-vaiśiṣṭyāt | yataḥ, viśuddha-sattva-sambhāvitāyāṁ citta-vṛttau yad brahma sphurati, tad eva ghanībhūtākhaṇḍa-viśuddha-sattva-maye bhagavati sphurat tad-adhyastatayā tad-aikyam āpannāyāṁ tasyāṁ viśeṣata eva sphurati | ata eva śrī-vigrahādi-para-brahmaṇor abheda-vākyam api tad-atyanta-tādātmyāpekṣayaiva | ata eva tatra tatropādhāv eka eva nirbheda-paramānandaḥ samupalabhyate, na tu viśeṣākāra-gandho’pi, tat-tad-upādher apekṣaṇaṁ tu pratipada-tad-ānanda-samādhi-gata-kautuka-nibandhanam | tasmāt katham anena pramāṇena tat-tad-upādhīnām api para-tattvākāratvaṁ sādhyate ? iti cet, ucyate—

bhavan-mate tāvad yac chuddha-citta-vṛttau para-brahma sphurati, tat samyag eva sphurati | bhedāṁśa-leśa-parityāgenaiva brahma-vidyātvāṅgīkārāt | asamyag-jñānasya tattvānaṅgīkārāt tena kaivalyāsambhavāc ca | ato na śrī-vigrahādāv adhikāvirbhāvāṅgīkāro yujyate | kiṁ ca, viśuddha-sattva-maya-vigrahā vilakṣaṇopādhaya iti vadatas tava ko’bhiprāyaḥ ? kiṁ tat pariṇāmās te tat-pracurā vā ? nādyaḥ, rajo’sad-bhāvena pariṇāmāsambhava iti hy uktam | na cāntyaḥ, yeṣu vigrahādiṣu tat-prācuryaṁ, te miśra-sattvasya kārya-bhūtā ity arthāpattau, sattvaṁ viśuddhaṁ śrayate bhavān sthitau [bhā.pu. 10.2.34] ity-ādi-vacana-jāte viśuddha-pada-vaiyarthyam iti coktam eva | astu vā vimiśratvaṁ, tathāpi tādṛśe brahma-sphuraṇa-yogyataiva na sambhavet, kiṁ punar viśeṣeṇety uddeśya-vismṛtiś ca syāt | athākhaṇḍa-viśuddha-sattvāśrayatvena te’pi tad-rūpatayaivocyante |

tataś ca teṣv anubhūtākhaṇḍa-śuddha-sattve tasmin brahmānubhavantīti cet, tad ayuktaṁ kalpanā-gauravāt, te’cakṣatākṣa-viṣayaṁ sva-samādhi-bhāgyaṁ [bhā.pu. 3.15.38] iti sākṣād eva gocarī-kṛtatvenoktatayā paramparā-dṛṣṭatva-pratighātāc ca | tasmāt tasya śuddha-sattvasya prākṛtatvaṁ tu niṣiddham eva | tasmān na te prākṛta-sattva-pariṇāmāḥ, na vā tat-pracurāḥ, kintu sva-prakāśatā-lakṣaṇa-śuddha-sattva-prakāśitā iti prāktanam evoktaṁ vyaktam | ata eva teṣām upādhitva-nirākṛtes tat-tad-anubhavānanda-vaicitrī ca sampadyate | tathaiva tam evam evambhūtam acakṣateti tat-tad-viṣaya-saundarya-varṇanaṁ prastutopakāritvāt sārthakaṁ syāt | akhaṇḍa-śuddha-sattva-mayatva-kathana-mātreṇaivābhipreta-siddheḥ | ata eva nirīkṣya ca na vitṛpta-dṛśa [bhā.pu. 3.15.42] iti dṛk-sambandhitvād rūpa-kṛtaivātṛptir uktā |

tathaiva ca-śabdenaivākṣara-jayitvaṁ padāravinda-parimalātmaka-vāyu-lakṣaṇasya tad-viśeṣasya darśitam | anyathobhayatrāpi brahmānandasyaiva nirviśeṣatayopalabhyamānatve vidyā-juṣām apīty upādhi-pradhānam evocyate, upādhi-yugalasyaiva mithaḥ-spardhitva-prāpteḥ | anenākṣarānubhava-sukha-jayitva-kathanena vaśiṣṭhādīnaṁ putra-śokādikam iva tad-āveśābhāsa evāyam ity api nirastam | ata evam evoktaṁ śrī-svāmibhir api—svarūpānandād api teṣāṁ bhajanānandādhikyam āha iti |

tasmād asti vaicitryam api | ata eva tair api vicitratayaiva prārthitaṁ—ceto’livad yadi nu te padayo rameta [bhā.pu. 3.15.49] ity-ādau | akke cen madhu vindeta kim arthaṁ parvataṁ vrajet ? iti nyāyena tad-upādhy-antarānveṣaṇa-vaiyarthyāt, teṣām atad-anveṣaṇa-kautukābhāvāc ca |

kiṁ ca, na teṣām abhedātmako’nubhavo vā dṛśyate, pratyuta nemur nirīkṣya na vitṛpta-dṛśo mudā kaiḥ [bhā.pu. 3.15.42], kāmaṁ bhavaḥ sva-vṛjinair nirayeṣu naḥ stād [bhā.pu. 3.15.49] ity-ādāv abhedasya tat-pratiyogi-namaskārādy-upalakṣita-bhedātmaka-bhakti-sukham eva dṛśyate | tasmān māyikopādhi-nirhīnatvād dheyāṁśatayā pratibhātatvāc ca na taj-jātīyaṁ sukham apy anya-jātīyaṁ kartuṁ śaknotīti | santy evānyathānupapatti-siddhāyāḥ svarūpa-śakter eva vilāsāḥ |

api ca—astu tāvaj jīvan-mukta-daśāyāṁ tan-mate vidyopādhi-pratiphalitasyaiva sato brahmaṇaḥ sakāśāt śrī-bhagavato ghana-prakāśatā, sarvopādhi-vinirmukta-mukti-daśāyām api sākṣāt tādṛśatāsty eveti suvyaktaṁ, nātyantikaṁ vigaṇayanty api te prasādaṁ [bhā.pu. 3.15.48] ity-ādau | tasmān nopādhi-tāratamya-cintā | bhavataḥ kathāyāḥ [bhā.pu. 3.15.48] ity anena nirupādhi-brahma-bhūyād upari ca vaicitrī sphuṭam evāsau svīkṛtā |

tasmāt sāntaraṅga-vaibhavasya bhagavataḥ sukhaika-rūpatvaṁ, tad-rūpatve’pi brahmato’pi ghana-prakāśatvaṁ, svarūpa-śakit-vilāsa-vaicitrī ceti vidvad-anubhava-pramāṇena nirṇītam | tatra, muktā api līlayā vigrahaṁ kṛtvā bhagavantaṁ bhajanta iti, yaṁ sarve devā āmananti mumukṣavo brahma-vādinaś ca [nṛ.tā.u. 2.4] ity atra śrutāv advaita-vāda-guravo’pi | kṛṣṇo muktair ijyate vīta-mohaiḥ iti bhārate |

brahma-bhūtaḥ prasannātmā na śocati na kāṅkṣati |

samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu mad-bhaktiṁ labhate parām || [gītā 18.54] iti śrī-bhagavad-gītopaniṣatsu |

muktānām api bhaktir hi nityānanda-svarūpiṇī iti bhārata-tātparye pramāṇitā śrutiś ca | tathā ā-prāyaṇāt tatrāpi hi dṛṣṭam [ve.sū. 4.1.12] ity atra ca madhva-bhāṣya-pramāṇitā sauparṇa-śrutiḥ—sarvadainam upāsīta yāvan-mukti muktā hy enam upāsata iti |

ata eva śrī-prahlāda-bali-prabhṛti-mahā-bhāgavata-sambandham abhipretya śrī-viṣṇu-purāṇe’py uktaṁ, pātāle kasya na prītir vimuktasyāpi jāyate [vi.pu. 2.5.7] iti |

atra viśeṣo śrī-bhagavat-sandarbhe jñeyaḥ ||50|| [bhagavat-sandarbha 85]
