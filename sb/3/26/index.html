SB 3.26

(3.26)

atha ṣaḍviṁśo’dhyāyaḥ

mahad-ādi-tattvānām utpatti-nirūpaṇaṁ sad-dharma-varṇanaṁ ca |

|| 3.26.1 ||

śrī-bhagavān uvāca—

atha te sampravakṣyāmi tattvānāṁ lakṣaṇaṁ pṛthak |

yad viditvā vimucyeta puruṣaḥ prākṛtair guṇaiḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

ṣaḍviṁśe puṁ-prakṛtyos tu vivekāyopavarṇyate |

sāṅkhyena sarva-bhāvānāṁ janma-lakṣaṇa-bhedataḥ ||

dhātrā putrāya yat proktaṁ kṣattre mitrā-sutena yat |

mātre sāṅkhyaṁ tad adhyātmaṁ prādhānyenāha tattvavit ||

tattvāmnāyaṁ yat pravadanti sāṅkhyam ity atra sāṅkhyaṁ bhaktir yogaś ceti trayam upakṣiptam | tatra bhaktim uktvedānīṁ sāṅkhyam āha—atheti ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tad evaṁ kṛtārthayitvā viveka-mātrāya sāṅkhya-jñāna-yogādi-mārgān apy āha yāvat-samāpti—yatheti ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

ṣaḍviṁśe mahad-ādīnām utpatti-lakṣaṇaṁ tathā |

tair virāṭ tasya caitanyam uktam ātma-praveśataḥ ||

mātrā pṛṣṭeṣu bhakti-jñāna-yogeṣu bhaktim uktvā mātaraṁ tayaiva kṛtārthīkṛtyāpi karma-jñāna-yogādibhiḥ svarga-mokṣādi-phalānāṁ bhaktyā vinā dātum aśakyatvāt teṣv api bhakti-mahā-devyā adhikārāt sarvatra sāmrājyavatyās tasyā upāsaka-janair api kautka-vaśāt karma-jñānādayo’pi jijñāsanīyā eveti sāmprataṁ jñānaṁ vaktum āha—atheti ||1||

...

|| 3.26.2 ||

jñānaṁ niḥśreyasārthāya puruṣasyātma-darśanam |

yad āhur varṇaye tat te hṛdaya-granthi-bhedanam ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : jñānam iti | ātma-darśana-rūpaṁ jñānam ata eva hṛdaya-granthi-bhedanam ahaṅkāra-nivartakaṁ niḥśreyasa-prayojanāya yad āhus tat te varṇayāmi | tattva-lakṣaṇa-jñānād eva viviktātma-jñānaṁ bhavatīti bhāvaḥ ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : nanu, tattva-lakṣaṇa-jñānena kiṁ bhaviṣyatīty ata āha—jñānam iti | tattva-lakṣaṇa-jñānād eva vivktātma-jñānaṁ bhavatīti bhāvaḥ ||2||

...

|| 3.26.3 ||

anādir ātmā puruṣo nirguṇaḥ prakṛteḥ paraḥ |

pratyag-dhāmā svayaṁ-jyotir viśvaṁ yena samanvitam ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : sa paramo na jāyate na miryate iti prasiddhaṁ hi | dehādy-upādhibhir ātta-dharmo jīvo’pi sva-pravada bhrāntyā jāyate mriyate ca | bhrāntitvād dehātmatvasya kim u | sarvajña-svatantratvādi-vailakṣaṇya-yukta īśvaraḥ |

parasya janma-mṛtyv-ādyāḥ syuḥ svatantrasya kiṁ punaḥ |

jīvasyāpi yato bhrāntyā janma-mṛtyv-ādi-saṅgatiḥ || iti mahā-kaurme ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tatra puruṣaṁ lakṣayati—anādir iti | ātmaiva puruṣaḥ | ko’sāv ātmā ? pratyak pratilomaṁ dhāma sphūrtir yasya | kṣaṇika-pakṣaṁ vyāvartayati—anādir iti | saṁsāritva-pakṣaṁ vyāvartaytai—prakṛteḥ paraḥ | anyo’saṅgaḥ | jñānādi-guṇatvaṁ vārayati—nirguṇaḥ | mīmāṁsakādy-abhimata-jñāna-viṣayatvaṁ vārayati—svayaṁ-jyotiḥ | anenaiva prābhākarābhimataṁjñānādhāratvena sphuraṇam api nirastam | svayaṁ-jyotiṣṭve hetuḥ—viśvaṁ yena samanvitam, prakāśate iti śeṣaḥ | etair eva hetubhiḥ puruṣāsya prakṛteḥ paratvam api siddham ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : anādir iti | ātmā paramātmaiva puruṣo nirguṇaḥ prākṛta-guṇa-rahitaḥ | viśvaṁ yena samanvitam iti sarva-kāraṇatvam | tena vicitra-prakāśakatayānanta-śaktitvaṁ darśitam ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tatra puruṣaṁ lakṣayati—anādir nityaḥ ātmā paramātmaiva puruṣaḥ nirguṇaḥ prākṛta-guṇa-rahitaḥ | yataḥ prakṛteḥ paraḥ | pratyak sarvendriyāgamyaṁ dhāma kāraṇārṇavo yasya saḥ | ata eva svayaṁ-jyotiḥ sva-prakāśaḥ | svayaṁ-jyotiṣṭve hetuḥ—viśvaṁ yena samanvitaṁ sat prakāśata iti śeṣaḥ ||3||

|| 3.26.4 ||

sa eṣa prakṛtiṁ sūkṣmāṁ daivīṁ guṇa-mayīṁ vibhuḥ |

yadṛcchayaivopagatām abhyapadyata līlayā ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : upāgatāṁ samīpasthām ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : atrāvaraṇa-vikṣepa-śakti-bhedena prakṛtir dvi-vidhā | tatrāvaraṇa-śaktyā saiva jīvopādhir avidyā | vikṣepa-śaktyā saiva māyā pārameśvarī | puruṣaś ca jīveśvara-rūpeṇa dvi-vidhaḥ | tatra yaḥ prakṛty-avivekena saṁsarati sa jīvaḥ | yas tu prakṛtiṁ vaśīkṛtya viśva-sṛṣṭy-ādi karoti sa īśvaraḥ | tatra prakṛty-avivekena jīvasya saṁsāra-prakāram āha—sa eṣa iti pañcabhiḥ | sūkṣmām avyaktāṁ daivīṁ devasya viṣṇoḥ śaktiṁ līlayopagatāṁ yadṛcchayaivābhyapadyatety anvayaḥ ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : sa eṣa paramātmā yadṛcchayā svairitayā yā līlā, tayābhyapadyata aikṣata | lokavat tu līlā-kaivalyaṁ [ve.sū. 2.1.33] iti nyāyāt | sa aikṣata [bṛ.ā.u. 1.2.5] iti śruteḥ ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : eṣa paramātmā yadṛcchayaiva svairitayaiva sva-śaktitvād upagatāṁ karma-bandha-jagat-sisṛkṣā-samaya eva līlayā abhyapadyata jīva-śakti-rūpaṁ vīryaṁ tasyām ādadhāna aikṣatety arthaḥ ||4||

|| 3.26.5 ||

guṇair vicitrāḥ sṛjatīṁ sa-rūpāḥ prakṛtiṁ prajāḥ |

vilokya mumuhe sadyaḥ sa iha jñāna-gūhayā ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : mumuhe mohayāmāsa | tad etan me vijānīhi—kṛtvā vivāham ity-ādivat ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tasyā līlām āha—guṇair iti | jñānaṁ gūhayaty āvṛṇotīti jñāna-gūhā tayā | tathā ca śrutiḥ—

ajām ekāṁ lohita-śukla-kṛṣṇāṁ

bahvīṁ prajāṁ janayntīṁ sarūpām |

ajo hy eo juṣamāṇo’nuśete

jahāty enāṁ bhukta-bhogām ajo’nyaḥ || [śve.u. 4.5] iti |

mumuhe ātmānaṁ vismṛtavān ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tataś ca puruṣāntarasyāvasthām āha—guṇair iti | tāṁ caikāṁśena sṛjantīm | iha śarīre sa prasiddho jīvākhyaḥ puruṣas tu mumuhe | jñāna-gūhayā tasyāḥ prakṛter evāvidyākhyayā vṛttyā | atra vilokyety anena | mumuhe ity anena jñāna-gūhayety anena ca parāg-bhūtāyāḥ prakṛtes tat-kṛtād ajñānāc ca pratyag-bhūtaṁ yaj jñānaṁ, tat tasya svarūpa-śaktir eva syād iti gamyate | śrī-gītopaniṣadi ca tathā—ajñānenāvṛtaṁ jñānaṁ tena muhyati jantavaḥ [gītā 5.15] iti ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tatra jīvasya moham āha—prakṛtiṁ vilokya sa jīvātmā iha prakṛtau sthitaḥ sadyaḥ prakṛit-saṁsarga-samaya eva jñāna-gūhayā prakṛter evāvidyākhya-vṛttyā yukto mumuhe svarūpaṁ visasmāra, sadaivānādya-vidyayā yukto’pi jīvaḥ suṣuptau yathā svarūpaṁ kiñcid upalabhate, tathaiva sṛṣṭeḥ pūrvaṁ pralaye’pi svarūpam upalabhamāna evāsīt | sṛṣṭārambhe tu tad visasmārety arthaḥ | kīdṛśīm ? guṇaiḥ sattvādibhir vicitrāḥ prajāḥ sṛjantīm | tathā ca śrutiḥ—

ajām ekāṁ lohita-śukla-kṛṣṇāṁ

bahvīṁ prajāṁ janayntīṁ sarūpām |

ajo hy eo juṣamāṇo’nuśete

jahāty enāṁ bhukta-bhogām ajo’nyaḥ || [śve.u. 4.5] iti |

|| 3.26.6 ||

evaṁ parābhidhyānena kartṛtvaṁ prakṛteḥ pumān |

karmasu kriyamāṇeṣu guṇair ātmani manyate ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

yatra kārayitātīva svatantras tatra kartṛtā |

procyate tu yathā brahmatva-jñaḥ saṁsāra-bhāg yataḥ || iti ca |

laye vāpy athavā sṛṣṭau tv antarālepinaḥ kvacit |

prakṛtyā rahitaṁ brahma kadācid api tiṣṭhati || iti kāpileye |

evaṁ parābhidhyānena parātmecchayā | prakṛteḥ kartṛtvaṁ jīva ātmani manyate ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : parābhidhyānaṁ prakṛty-adhyāsas tena | prakṛter guṇaiḥ karmasu kriyamāṇeṣu kartṛtvam ātmani manyate ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : parābhidhyānena prakṛtyāveśena prakṛtir evāham iti mananena prakṛter guṇaiḥ kriyamāṇeṣu karmasu kartṛtvam ātmani manyate | atra nirahambhāvasya parābhidhyānāsambhavāt parāveśajātāhaṅkārasya cāvarakatvād asty eva tasminn anyo’hambhāva-viśeṣaḥ | sa ca śuddha-svarūpa-mātra-niṣṭhatvān na saṁsāra-hetur iti spaṣṭam |

etad evāhaṅkāra-dvayaṁ sanne yad indriya-gaṇe’hami ca prasupte kūṭastha āśayam ṛte tad-anusmṛtir naḥ [bhā.pu. 11.3.39] ity atra darśitam upādhy-abhimānātmakasyāhaṅkārasya prasuptatvāt | tad-anusmṛtir na ity anena sukham aham asvāpsvam ity ātmano’hatayaiva parāmarśāc ca | ata eva mām ahaṁ nājñāsiṣam ity atra parāmarśe’pi upādhy-abhimānino ’nusandhānābhāvaḥ anyasya tv ajñāna-sākṣitvenānusandhānam iti dik ||6|| [paramātma-sandarbha 30]

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : evaṁ nṛtyato gāyataḥ paśyan yathaivānukaroti tān [bhā.pu. 11.22.53] iti rītyā parābhidhyānena prakṛty-adhyāsena sā ca prakṛtir deha eveti deha evāham iti mananena prakṛter guṇaiḥ kriyamāṇeṣu karmasu rūpādi-grahaṇeṣu kartṛtvam ātmani manyate | tatra nirahaṁ-bhāvasya parābhidhyānāsambhavāt parāveśa-jātāhaṅkārasya cāvarakatvād asty eva tasminn anyo’haṁbhāva-viśeṣaḥ | sa ca śuddha-svarūpa-mātra-niṣṭhatvān na saṁsāra-hetur iti spaṣṭam | vipra-kumārasya sāhaṅkārasyaiva bhūtāveśatve sati bhūto’ham iti vaditi vivecanīyam ||6||

[atra brahma-sūtre 3.2.5 parābhidhyānāt tu tirohitaṁ tato hy asya baddha-viparyayu ity etat sūtraṁ draṣṭavyam |]

|| 3.26.7 ||

tad asya saṁsṛtir bandhaḥ pāra-tantryaṁ ca tat-kṛtam |

bhavaty akartur īśasya sākṣiṇo nirvṛtātmanaḥ ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

viṣṇoḥ surāṇāṁ gurūṇāṁ nityā jīva-svatantratā |

yat tu tasyānya-tantratvaṁ taj-jñānād vinivartate || iti ca |

akartur anīśasya sakāśāt | akliṣṭatvād akartā sā akāryatvād athāpi vā iti ca ||7||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tad iti kartṛtva-mananam eva | asya puruṣasya | sākṣi-mātratvād akartur eva sataḥ karmabhir bandhaḥ | īśasyāparatantrasyaiva karma-bandha-kṛtaṁ bhoge pāratantryaṁ nirvṛtātmanaḥ sukhātmakasyaiva saṁsṛtir janma-mṛtyu-pravāhaḥ prakṛty-aviveka-kṛtam etat sarvaṁ bhavatīty arthaḥ ||7||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : tad iti kartṛtva-mananam evāsya jīvasya, vastutaḥ sākṣi-mātratvād akartur eva sataḥ karmabhir bandhaḥ | yathā rājakīyaḥ puruṣo rājocyate, tathaiva īśasya īśa-śabda-vācyasyeśvara-śakti-rūpasyāpi karma-bandha-kṛtaṁ bhoga-pāratantryam | nirvṛtātmanaḥ sukha-svarūpasyāpi saṁsṛtir janma-mṛtyu-pravāhaḥ ||7||

|| 3.26.8 ||

kārya-kāraṇa-kartṛtve kāraṇaṁ prakṛtiṁ viduḥ |

bhoktṛtve sukha-duḥkhānāṁ puruṣaṁ prakṛteḥ param ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : eṣa kartā na kriyate kāraṇaṁ ca jagat-prabhur iti bhārate |

brahmādibhiḥ sarga-karī śrī-viṣṇu-bala-saṁśrayāt |

sukha-duḥkha-prado viṣṇuḥ svayam eva sanātanaḥ ||

kartṛtvaṁ sukha-duḥkhānāṁ karoty eko hariḥ svayam |

bhoktṛtva-mātra-hetutvaṁ jīve nānyatra kutracit || iti bhaviṣyat-parvaṇi ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : nanu caitanya-sāmānādhikaraṇyenaiva kartṛtvādi-pratīteḥ svasyaiva tad-aṅgīkriyatāṁ, nety āha | kāryaṁ śarīram | kāraṇam indriyam | kartā devatā-vargaḥ | tad-bhāvāpattau puruṣasya prakṛtiṁ kāraṇaṁ viduḥ | kūṭasthasya svato vikārābhāvāt prakṛti-pariṇāma-bhūta-dehādy-ahaṅkāra-kṛtam eva kartṛtvādikam ity arthaḥ | bhoktṛtve tu puruṣaṁ kāraṇaṁ viduḥ ity asyāyaṁ bhāvaḥ—yadyapy ahaṅkāra-gatam eva kartṛtvādikaṁ bhoktṛtvaṁ ca, tathāpi vikārasya jaḍāvasānatvād upādhi-prādhānyaṁ bhogasya cid-avasānatvād upahita-prādhānyam iti ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : tad-upādhi-prādhānyena pravartamānam upādhi-dharmatvena vyapadiśyata ity āha—kāryeti | atra kārya-kāraṇa-kartṛtve kāraṇaṁ prakṛtiṁ viduḥ ity ādau paramātma-prādhānyena pravartamānaṁ tu nirupādhikam eva | yathā—

sāttvikaṁ kārako’saṅgī rāgāndho rājasaḥ smṛtaḥ |

tāmasaḥ smṛti-vibhraṣṭo nirguṇo mad-apāśrayaḥ || [bhā.pu. 11.25.26] iti |

atha bhoktṛtvaṁ saṁvedana-rūpatvena yathā tathā tatraiva cid-rūpe paryavasyatīty āha—bhoktṛtva iti | kāraṇaṁ viduḥ iti pūrveṇaivānvayaḥ | atra ṭīkā-mate pratibimba dṛṣṭānto jñeyaḥ | atra hi kampādika upādher dharmaḥ | prakāśas tūpahitasya | sva-mate tu tad api neṣyate, kriyāpi caitanyaṁ vinā na sambhavatīti dṛṣṭānta-vaiṣamyāt ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : jīvasya karmabhir eva bandhas teṣāṁ karmaṇāṁ ca sādhane phala-bhoge ca krameṇa prakṛti-puruṣāv eva kāraṇe ity āha—kāryeti | bhūtendriya-devatādibhir eva karma-siddhes teṣāṁ bhūtendriya-devatānāṁ ca kārya-kāraṇa-kartṛtve prakṛtim eva kāraṇaṁ viduḥ | prakṛtyaiva teṣāṁ tad-bhāvasya sṛṣṭatvāj jīvasya karma-kāraṇaṁ māyādhīnam ity arthaḥ | karma-phala-dātā ca parameśvara eveti jīvasya karma-phala-bhoktṛtvam īśvarādhīnam evety āha—bhoktṛtvaṁ jīvasya karma-phalānāṁ bhoge puruṣaṁ kāraṇaṁ vidur ity anvayaḥ ||8||

|| 3.26.9 ||

devahūtir uvāca—

prakṛteḥ puruṣasyāpi lakṣaṇaṁ puruṣottama |

brūhi kāraṇayor asya sad-asac ca yad-ātmakam ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : prakṛtiḥ puruṣaṁ caiva vidhy anādī iti ca ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : tad evaṁ saṁsāriṇaṁ puruṣaṁ tad-dhetuṁ prakṛtiṁ ca jñātvedānīṁ jagat-kāraṇam īśvaraṁ tat-prakṛtiṁ ca pṛcchati—prakṛter iti | asya viśvasya | sad asac ca sthūlaṁ sūkṣmaṁ ca kāryaṁ yad ātmakaṁ tayoḥ prakṛti-puruṣayoḥ ||9||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

|| 3.26.10 ||

śrī-bhagavān uvāca—

yat tat tri-guṇam avyaktaṁ nityaṁ sad-asad-ātmakam |

pradhānaṁ prakṛtiṁ prāhur aviśeṣaṁ viśeṣavat ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

vyaktāvyaktātmakaṁ yat tad vindyāt sad-asad-ātmakam |

asargā kevalā vyaktā sisṛkṣur ubhayātmikā ||

vyaktaiva kārya-rūpā tu prakṛtis trividhā matā |

kāryataḥ sā pradhānatvāt sā pradhānam iti kīrtyate |

aviśeṣād akāryatvāt sā ca śrī-viṣṇu-saṁśrayā || iti harivaṁśeṣu |
