SB 3.6

(3.6)

atha ṣaṣṭho'dhyāyaḥ

īśa-śakti-praveśena sthūla-bhūtānāṁ jagan-nirmāṇa-śaktitva-pradarśana-pūrvakaṁ virāḍ-vigrahotpatti-kathanam

|| 3.6.1 ||

ṛṣir uvāca—

iti tāsāṁ sva-śaktīnāṁ satīnām asametya saḥ |

prasupta-loka-tantrāṇāṁ niśāmya gatim īśvaraḥ ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

śakyatvāc chaktayo viṣṇor mahad-ādyā ramā tathā |

svarūpa-śaktiḥ śaktitvāt mukhya-śaktitvāt mukhya-śaktir hi sā yataḥ || iti brahma-tarke |

sametyāsatīnām asametānāṁ prasupta-loka-tantrāṇām anāvirbhūta-loka-sṛṣṭi-śaktīnām |

tanute yena kāryaṁ taṁ tantraṁ sādhanam ucyate |

kāraṇānāṁ sva-śaktir vā pradhānaṁ sādhanaṁ yataḥ || iti brahma-tarke ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) :

ṣaṣṭhe tair īśvarāviṣṭaiḥ sṛṣṭim āha virāṭ-tanoḥ |

adhidaivādi-bhedaṁ ca tatraiva bhagavat-kṛtam ||

sva-śaktīnāṁ mahad-ādīnām asametyāmilitvā satīnāṁ sthitānām | prasuptaṁ loka-tantraṁ viśva-racanā yāsām | yad vā, prasupta-jīvopakaraṇānāṁ gatiṁ sthitiṁ dṛṣṭvā āviśad ity uttareṇānvayaḥ ||1||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) :

ṣaṣṭhe virāḍ abhūt tattvair antaryāmi-praveśataḥ |

adhibhūtādi-bhedaś tasya dehe prapañcitaḥ ||

sva-śakt-kāryatvāt sva-śaktīnāṁ mahad-ādīnām asametyāmilitvā satīnāṁ sthitānām | prasuptaṁ loka-tantraṁ viśva-nirmāṇa-kriyā yāsāṁ tāsāṁ gatiṁ dṛśā dṛṣṭvā ||1||

...

|| 3.6.2 ||

kāla-saṁjñāṁ tadā devīṁ bibhrac chaktim urukramaḥ |

trayoviṁśati tattvānāṁ gaṇaṁ yugapad āviśat ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) :

śabdādyā nabha-ādyāś ca mano-yuktendriyāṇi ca |

ahaṅkāro mahāṁś caiva trayoviṁśatiko gaṇaḥ ||

devatendriyor aikyān na pṛthag-gaṇanaṁ tayoḥ |

prakṛtis tu caturviṁśā pañca-viṁśā hariḥ svayam ||

yadā jaḍāṁśa-svīkāro jīvas tat-pañca-viṁśakāḥ |

ṣaḍ-viṁśako mahā-viṣṇuḥ śriyā vā sapta-viṁśakāḥ || iti tattva-nirṇaye |

trayoviṁśati-tattvāni prāviśat ramayā saha |

kālākhyayā svayaṁ viṣṇuḥ śakyatvāt śakti-rūpayā ||

sarva-ceṣṭaka-rūpeṇa sva-sāmarthyena keśavaḥ |

tāni bhinnāni tattvāni yojayāmāsa cāṁśataḥ || iti ca ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : kālena saṁjñā udbodho yasyāḥ, kālayati kṣobhayati sva-kāryāṇīti vā kāla-saṁjñā prakṛtis tāṁ śaktim | prakṛtyā saha praveśāt trayo-viṁśati-tattvānām ity uktam | antaryāmitayā prāviśat ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : kālayati sarvaṁ melayatīti kālaḥ | kālo vṛtti-viśeṣaḥ | kāla evaṁ saṁjñā yasyās tām | trayoviṁśati-tattvānām iti | mahad-ādīnām eva paraspara-melanāt prakṛtes tu sarvānugateḥ ||2||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : kālenaiva saṁjñā mahad-ādyā vividhā yasyā sā kāla-saṁjñā prakṛtiḥ tāṁ bibhrat san āviśat | prathamaṁ saṁhanana-kāriṇyā śaktyaiva tato varṣa-sahasrānte antaryāmitayety arthaḥ | prakṛtyā saha praveśāt trayoviṁśatīty uktaṁ mahad ahaṅkāraḥ pañca-tanmātrāni pañca-mahā-bhūtāni ekādaśendriyāṇīti trayoviṁśatiḥ | yad uktaṁ—

mūla-prakṛtir avikṛtir mahad-ādyāḥ prakṛti-vikṛtayaḥ sapta |

ṣoḍaśakas tu vikāro na prakṛtir na vikṛtiḥ puruṣaḥ || [sāṅkhya-kārikā 3]iti |

tat sṛṣṭvā tad evānuprāviśat [tai.u. 2.6] iti śruteḥ ||2||

...

|| 3.6.3 ||

so’nupraviṣṭo bhagavāṁś ceṣṭā-rūpeṇa taṁ gaṇam |

bhinnaṁ saṁyojayām āsa suptaṁ karma prabodhayan ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : mātrābhiḥ aṁśaiḥ ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : ceṣṭā-rūpeṇa kriyā-śaktyā | karma teṣāṁ kriyāṁ jīvānām adṛṣṭaṁ vā ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : ceṣṭā-rūpeṇa pūrvoktena kālākhyenaiva kāraṇa-bhūtena | yo’yaṁ kālas tasya te’vyakta-bandho ceṣṭām āhur ity ukteḥ ||3||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : ceṣṭā-rūpeṇa kriyā-śaktyā karma teṣāṁ kriyāṁ jīvānām adṛṣṭaṁ vā ||3||

...

|| 3.6.4 ||

prabuddha-karma daivena trayoviṁśatiko gaṇaḥ |

prerito’janayat svābhir mātrābhir adhipūruṣam ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : mātrābhiḥ aṁśaiḥ ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : prabuddhaṁ karma kriyā-śaktir yasya sa trayo-viṁśater gaṇaḥ daiveneśvareṇa preritaḥ | mātrābhir aṁśaiḥ | adhipūruṣaṁ virāḍ-deham ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : adhipūruṣaṁ brahmāṇḍa-rūpaṁ, kvāhaṁ tamo-mahad-ahaṅ-kha-carāgni-vār-bhūḥ ity ādy ukteḥ ||4||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : prabuddhaṁ karma kriyā-śaktir yasya saḥ | daiveneśvareṇa | mātrābhir aṁśaiḥ | adhipūruṣaṁ virāḍ-deham ||4||

...

|| 3.6.5 ||

pareṇa viśatā svasmin mātrayā viśva-sṛg-gaṇaḥ |

cukṣobhānyonyam āsādya yasmin lokāś carācarāḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : jananam evāha—pareṇeśvareṇa | viśva-sṛjāṁ sattvānāṁ gaṇaḥ | mātrayā cukṣobha pariṇato na sarvātmanā | yasmil lokāḥ sthitāḥ ||5||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : janana-prakāram āha—viśva-sṛjāṁ tattvānāṁ gaṇaḥ svasmin pareṇa parameśvareṇa mātrayā aṁśena viśatā satā anyonyam āsādya pradhāna-guṇa-bhāvaṁ prāpya cukṣobha garbha-rūpeṇa pariṇato’bhūt ||5||

...

|| 3.6.6 ||

hiraṇmayaḥ sa puruṣaḥ sahasra-parivatsarān |

āṇḍa-kośa uvāsāpsu sarva-sattvopabṛṁhitaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : sa puruṣo’dhipuruṣa ity uktaḥ | āṇḍa-kośe brahmāṇḍa-madhye | sarvaiḥ sattvair anuśāyibhir jīvaiḥ sahitaḥ ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : hiraṇmayo brahmāṇḍaḥ, tasmin sthito hiraṇyagarbhaḥ | apsu iti pūrvaṁ jalenāntaḥ pūrṇatvāt ||6||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : sa samaṣṭi-virāṭ hiraṇmayaḥ caturdaśa-bhuvanātmaka aṇḍa-koṣa yā āpaḥ garbhodakāni, tāsu | yad vā, apsu yo’ṇḍa-koṣaḥ, tasmin sarva-sattvair anuśāyibhir jīvair upabṛṁhito vistṛtaḥ ||6||

...

|| 3.6.7 ||

sa vai viśva-sṛjāṁ garbho deva-karmātma-śaktimān |

vibabhājātmanātmānam ekadhā daśadhā tridhā ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : īśvaro daivam uddiṣṭaṁ sarvasyāpi prabhutvataḥ iti ca | ātma-śaktiḥ prakṛtiḥ ||7||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : garbhaḥ kārya-rūpo virāṭ | deva-śaktir jñāna-śaktis tayaikadhā hṛdayāvacchinna-caitanya-rūpeṇa | karma-śaktiḥ kriyā-śaktis tayā daśadhā prāṇa-rūpeṇa prāṇādayaḥ pañca—nāgaḥ kūrmaś ca kṛkalo devadatto dhanañjayaḥ ity ete pañca | evaṁ vṛtti-bhedena daśa-vidhaḥ prāṇaḥ | ātma-śaktir bhoktṛ-śaktis tayādhyātmādhibhedena tridhātmānaṁ vibhaktaṁ kṛtavān ||7||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : sa vai iti daiva-karmātma-śaktimān ity atra daiva-śaktyā karma-śaktyā ātma-śaktyā ca yukta ity arthaḥ ||7||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : atha tasya mukhādy-aṅga-siddhy-arthaṁ prathamaṁ tri-śaktitvam āha—viśva-sṛjāṁ mahad-ādīnāṁ garbhaḥ kārya-rūpānand | daiva-karmātma-śaktimān jīva-prāṇādhyātmādi-śakti-yuktaḥ | ātmanaivātmānaṁ vibabhāja vibhaktaṁ kṛtavān | ekadhā jīva-śaktyā daśadhā prāṇa-śaktyā tridhā adhātmādi-śaktyā ||7||

...

|| 3.6.8 ||

eṣa hy aśeṣa-sattvānām ātmāṁśaḥ paramātmanaḥ |

ādyo’vatāro yatrāsau bhūta-grāmo vibhāvyate ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : puruṣeṇātma-bhūtena iti yo’ṇḍam asṛjat | sa eva ity uktaḥ |

ādyo’vatāro viṣṇos puruṣo nāma kīrtitaḥ |

asṛjat sa mahat-tattvaṁ sa evāntaṁ samāviśat |

sa brahmaṇo hṛdi-sthatvād dhṛdayaṁ ceti kīrtyate || iti ca ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : evaṁ vibhāga-sāmarthyāya tasyotkarṣam āha—eṣa hīti | aśeṣa-sattvānāṁ prāṇinām ātmā | vyaṣṭīnāṁ tad-aṁśatvāt | aṁśo jīvaḥ | avatāratvoktis tasmin nārāyaṇāvirbhāvābhiprāyeṇa ||8||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : paramātmopāsakānāṁ citta-śuddhārthaṁ prathama-mayam evopāsya ity āha—eṣa hīti | aśeṣa-sattvānāṁ prāṇinām ātmā, vyaṣṭīnāṁ tad-aṁśatvāt | avatāra iti yogināṁ tad-antaryāminā saha tasyaikya-bhāvanayā bhūta-grāmo deva-manuṣyādi-samūhaḥ ||8||

...

|| 3.6.9 ||

sādhyātmaḥ sādhidaivaś ca sādhibhūta iti tridhā |

virāṭ prāṇo daśa-vidha ekadhā hṛdayena ca ||

madhvācāryaḥ (bhāgavata-tātparyam) : prāṇādi-pañcakaṁ caiva tathā nāgādi-pañcakam |

sa-nāga-kūrma-kṛkala-devadatta-dhanañjayāḥ |

evaṁ tu daśadhā prāṇaḥ adhyātmādi-tridhākhilā || iti ca vyoma-saṁhitāyām |

prāṇaḥ prathamajo yas tu pradhāno vāyur īritaḥ |

tvag-ātmādyās tu tat-putrā dvidhābhūtam udāhṛtam || iti tattva-nirṇaye ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : pūrva-ślokārthaṁ vivṛṇoti | sādhyātmaḥ adhyātmānīndriyāṇi tat sahitaḥ | virāḍ iti sarvatra ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : atra devasya īśvarasya jīva-rūpā cic-chaktiḥ tayā hṛdaya-sthayā ekadhā ||9||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : ekadhā daśadhā tridheti pūrva-ślokoktaṁ vyutkrameṇa vivṛṇoti—sādhyātmaḥ ādhyātmānīndriyāṇi tat-sahitaḥ | adhidaivānīndriyāṇāṁ devatāḥ | adhibhūtāni adhiṣṭhānāni viṣayāś ca |

prāṇo’pānaḥ samānaś ca udāno vyāna eva ca |

nāgaḥ kūrmaś ca kṛkalo devadatto dhanañjayaḥ ||

iti vṛtti-bhedena daśavidhaḥ prāṇa iti daśavidhenānena virāḍ eva daśavidha ity arthaḥ | tatra prāṇaḥ prāk-kramaṇo nāsāgra-vartī | apāno’vāk-kramaṇaḥ pāyv-ādi-sthāna-vartī | samāno bhukta-pītānnādi-samīkaraṇaḥ śarīra-madhya-vartī | udāna utkramaṇaḥ kaṇṭha-sthāna-vartī | nāgaḥ udgīraṇa-karaḥ | kūrmaḥ unmīlana-karaḥ | kṛkaraḥ kṣudhā-karaḥ | devadatto jṛmbhā-karaḥ | dhanañjayaḥ poṣaṇa-karaḥ | hṛdayena hṛdayāvacchinnena caitanyena jīvena ||9||

...

|| 3.6.10 ||

smaran viśva-sṛjām īśo vijñāpitam adhokṣajaḥ |

virājam atapat svena tejasaiṣāṁ vivṛttaye ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : adhyātmādi-bhedaṁ prapañcayitum āha—smarann iti | vijñāpitaṁ yāvad baliṁ te’ja harāma [bhā.pu. 3.5.48] ity-ādi | svena tejasā cic-chaktyā | atapat evaṁ kariṣyāmīty ālocitavān | yasya jñāna-mayaṁ tapaḥ iti śruteḥ | vivṛttaye vividha-vṛtti-lābhāya ||10||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : nanu virāṭ puruṣasyedṛśī śaktiś ced īśvara-praveśena kiṁ tatrāha smarann iti | adhokṣajāṁśaḥ puruṣaḥ | kāraṇānugraheṇaiva kārya-śaktir uddhavatīti bhāvaḥ | ata etad anantaram eva sa vai viśva-sṛjām ity-ādy-uktaṁ jñeyam | atapat svena tejasā prakāśayitum iṣṭavān ||10||

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : adhyātmādi-bhedaṁ prapañcayitum antaryāmi-rūpeṇa praviṣṭasyeśvarasya kim api kṛtyam āha—smarann iti | vijñāpitaṁ yāvad baliṁ te’ja harāma [bhā.pu. 3.5.48] ity-ādi | svena tejasā cic-chaktyā | atapat prakāśayāmāsa | eṣāṁ viśva-sṛjāṁ vivṛttaye vividha-vṛtti-lābhāya cakṣuṣaś cakṣur uta śrotrasya śrotram ity-ādi-śruteḥ | kāraṇa-śaktir eva kāryeṣūdbhavatīti bhāvaḥ ||10||

atha tasyābhitaptasya katidhāyatanāni ha |

nirabhidyanta devānāṁ tāni me gadataḥ śṛṇu ||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

|| 3.6.12 ||

tasyāgnir āsyaṁ nirbhinnaṁ loka-pālo’viśat padam |

vācā svāṁśena vaktavyaṁ yayāsau pratipadyate ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : āyatanāny evāha—tasyāgnir ity-ādi caturdaśabhiḥ | āsyaṁ nirbhinnaṁ pṛthag-jātam | padaṁ sva-sthānam | svāṁśena sva-śaktyā vācā saha aviśat | asau jīvaḥ | vaktavyaṁ pratipadyate—śabdam uccārayatīty arthaḥ | sarvatra yan nirbhinnaṁ tad adhiṣṭhānam | agny-ādi-prathamāntam adhidaivam | vāg-ādīndriyam tṛtīyāntam adhyātmam | pratipattavyam adhibhūtam ||12||

————————————————————————————————————————

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : na vyākhyātam.

————————————————————————————————————————

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : nirbhinnaṁ pṛthag-jātam āsyaṁ padaṁ sva-sthānaṁ vācā vāg-indriyeṇa saha agnir aviśat | svāṁśena svena ekāṁśena yayā vācaiva asau jīvaḥ virāṭ vaktavyaṁ pratipadyate śabdam uccārayatīty arthaḥ | evaṁ sarvatra yan nirbhinnaṁ dvitīyāntaṁ tad adhibhūtam adhiṣṭhānam | yad agny-ādi-prathamāntaṁ tad adhidaivam | yad vāg-ādi-tṛtīyāntaṁ tad adhyātmam | yat punar dvitīyāntaṁ kvacit ṣaṣṭhy-antaṁ tad adhibhūtaṁ viṣayaḥ ||12||

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : na cātīva jñāne nirbandhaḥ kāryaḥ, brahmaṇo’pi durjñeyatvād ity āha—ātmano harer mahimā yoga-vipakvayāpi saṁvatsara-sahasrānte’py ādi-kavinā brahmaṇāpi kim avasitaḥ kiṁ jñātaḥ ? iti kākūktyā etāvān iti na jñāta evety arthaḥ | yad vā, saṁvatsara-sahasrānta eva mahimā iti acintyatvānantatvābhyāṁ durjñeyatvena niścīyate, tad eva bhagavan-mahimno jñānam etāvad idam itthaṁ-kāreṇa jñānaṁ taj-jñānam eveti bhāvaḥ | tathā ca śrutiḥ—ayatiṣaṣṭhataṁ tasya mataṁ yasya na veda sa iti ||38||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : yat yasmāt svayam apy ātmā harir ātma-vartma sva-māyā-gatim etāvad iti na veda, anantatvāt ||39||

jīva-gosvāmī (krama-sandarbhaḥ) : ato ye tu cintyatvenaiva niścinvanti teṣām anyān pratimāyināṁ moha-kāriṇām api bhagavato māyā mohinī bhavatīty arthaḥ | ataḥ kāraṇāt tan-māyā māyinām api mohinīti sārdhenānvayaḥ | bhagavata ity atra bhagavatīti kvacit pāṭhaḥ | acintyatvam eva darśayati yad iti sārdhakena | yat yasmāt svayam api ātmā vartma-svarūpaiśvaryaṁ na veda sārvajñenāpi na paricchinatti anantatvāt atas tasmai nama eva kevalam ity arthaḥ ||39||

yato’prāpya nyavartanta vācaś ca manasā saha |

ahaṁ cānya ime devās tasmai bhagavate namaḥ ||

śrīdhara-svāmī (bhāvārtha-dīpikā) : ato durjñeyatvāt kevalaṁ namaskaroti—yata iti | yasya jñānāya pravṛttā vāco’pi manasā saha tam aprāpyaiva nyavartanta durjñeyatvāt | na kevalaṁ vāṅ manasī, aham ahaṅkārasyādhiṣṭhātā rudro’pi | ime indriyādhiṣṭhātāro devā anye ca ||40||

viśvanātha-cakravartī (sārārtha-darśiṇī) : ato durjñeyatvam eva sthāpayan namaskaroti | aprāpya antam alabdhvā yataḥ sakāśān nivartate vācaḥ samaṣṭi-vyaṣṭīnāṁ sarveṣām api vāg-indriyāṇi manasā saheti manāṁsi ca | yad vā, brahmaṇo mukhān nirgatāḥ sarve vedā eva vācaḥ, tasyaiva manasā saha, aham ahaṅkārādhiṣṭhātā rudraḥ, ime devā bṛhaspaty-ādayaś ca yato nivartante | kutaḥ ? aprāpya yan-nāma-rūpa-caritrādināṁ samyaṅ-mādhurya-grahaṇāsamarthyāt apārāṇāṁ teṣām anta-prāpta-sāmarthyāc cety arthaḥ | śrutir apy ācaṣṭe—yato vāco nivartante aprāpya manasā saha [tai.u. 2.4.1]iti | atrāpādāna-nirdeśe eva vāṅ-manaḥ-saṁśleṣa-pratyāyako nivṛttis tv anantatvena pramātum aśakyatvād iti jñeyam | sarvathaiva vāg-ādy-agamyatvaṁ tv ātmano na vyākhyeyam | vedaiś ca sarvair aham eva vedyaḥ [gītā 15.15] iti, manasaivānudraṣṭavyam etad ameyaṁ dhruvaṁ, tad viṣṇoḥ paramaṁ padaṁ sadā paśyanti sūrayaḥ ity-ādi-śruti-virodhāpatteḥ ||40||
